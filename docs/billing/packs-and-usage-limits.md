# Add-on packs and usage limits

Packs are blocks of messages you pay for once from your credits. The **Usage and limits** card on the **Credits** page shows how much of every pack and plan limit you have used. Takes about 5 minutes.

## Before you start

- You are signed in to Ohanvi. See [Sign in to Ohanvi](../sign-in.md).
- Your credits can pay for the pack. See [Add funds to your account](../add-funds.md).
- Your workspace is not billed through Shopify. See [Manage a plan billed through Shopify](shopify-billed-accounts.md).

## Which packs you can buy

| Pack | What you get | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bot Pack** | 1,000 bot messages: chatbot flow replies, order updates and reminders that your system sends on its own. | ₹499 |
| **AI Pack** | 1,000 AI replies from the AI agent. | ₹999 |
| **Automation Pack** | Both together: 1,000 bot messages and 1,000 AI replies. | ₹1,399 |
| **Review Pack** | 1,000 AI review drafts on your Google review page. | ₹1,000 |
| **Review + Voice Pack** | 1,000 AI review drafts, and customers can speak their review instead of typing it. | ₹1,500 |
| **Marketing packs** | 10,000 marketing messages | ₹1,299 |
| | 25,000 marketing messages | ₹2,599 |
| | 50,000 marketing messages | ₹3,499 |
| | 1,00,000 marketing messages | ₹4,299 |

A pack works on any plan, **Pay as you go** included.

- Bot, AI and Automation packs do not cover Meta's own WhatsApp charge. Your credits still pay it.
- A marketing pack pays **our fee** on each marketing message. Meta bills its own charge as usual. A message to a country where Meta charges more uses more than one credit.
- Packs are used **before** your credits are. A bot message takes a Bot credit once your plan's 1,000 automated and service messages for the month are used. On **Pay as you go** it takes a credit from the first message.
- AI replies take an AI credit.
- Each time a customer gets AI drafts on your Google review page, it takes a Review credit. With no Review credit left, ₹5 plus GST comes off your credits instead.
- Voice input on your Google review page works only while you have a **Review + Voice Pack**.

!!! note "A pack runs out after about a month"
    A pack ends a month after you buy it: **31 days** if you buy it in a 31-day month, otherwise **30 days**. This is the same on every plan and does not follow your billing cycle. What is left is not refunded.

    **Review Pack** and **Review + Voice Pack** last **1 year** from the day you buy them.

## Buy a pack

1. Open the **Credits** page. See [Credits and billing in Ohanvi](../credits-and-billing.md).
2. Under **Add-on packs**, click the pack you want.
3. Read the box that opens, then click **Buy [pack name]**.
4. The price comes off your credits and the message shows what you got and the date it runs out.

If your credits are lower than the price, the pack is not offered. Add funds first.

## Read your usage and limits

The **Usage and limits** card on the **Credits** page shows one bar for each thing that has a limit, with the numbers beside it, for example **250 of 1,000 used**. A bar turns amber near its limit and red past it.

| Group | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| **Messages** | Automated messages in your plan, the shared 1,000 automated and service messages, free WhatsApp messages on paid plans, inbound messages handled by the bot, and Developer API messages (10,000 a month on **Pay as you go**, no limit on paid plans). |
| **Packs** | **Bot Pack**, **AI Pack**, **Marketing Pack** and **Review Pack**: how many credits you used and the date they are gone. A pack you have not bought shows **None active**. |
| **Limits** | **Contacts**, **Team members**, **Storage** and **Active workflows**. |

Messages reset on the 1st of each month.

## Read the free-message counter in a chat

While a chat's 24-hour window is open, the chat header shows how many free service messages that WhatsApp number has left this month, for example **844 of 1,000 free left**.

- Meta gives each business WhatsApp number **1,000 free service messages a month** from 1 October 2026. After that, Meta charges for each one.
- The number is Meta's own count for finished days, plus what the number sent today. It goes down as soon as a message is sent.
- Hover over it to see how many were used and how many are left.
- If Meta cannot be reached, the counter is hidden.

## When your paid plan ends

If a paid plan is not renewed, your account moves to **Pay as you go** after the grace period. Packs you bought keep working until they run out.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A pack is not offered | Your credits are lower than its price. | [Add funds](../add-funds.md) and open the page again. |
| **None active** under a pack | You have not bought that pack, or it has run out. | Buy it from **Add-on packs**. |
| Bot credits did not go down | Your plan's 1,000 automated and service messages for the month are not used up yet. | Nothing to fix. Credits are used after that allowance. |
| The chat counter is missing | The 24-hour window is closed, or Meta could not be reached. | Wait for the customer's next message, or open the chat again later. |
| The counter did not match Meta's dashboard | Meta's reports can be several hours behind, and Meta counts days in its own timezone. | Check again later. The figure settles once Meta updates. |
