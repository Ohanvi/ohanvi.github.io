# Update your profile and password

Update your own name, email, phone number and password on the **My Account** screen. When you finish, your details are saved and your new password works the next time you sign in.

## Before you start

- You are signed in to Ohanvi. If you forgot your password, see [Reset your password](../reset-password.md).

## Steps

### Open My Account

1. Click your initials in the bottom-left corner. The profile menu opens.
2. Click your name at the top of the menu. The **My Account** screen opens.

### Change your details

1. Under **Your details**, edit **Name**, **Email** or **Phone Number**. All 3 are required.
2. Pick the country code, then type your number without it.
3. Click **Save changes**. The message **Your account details were updated.** appears.

You cannot change **Username**. It is the name you sign in with.

### Change your password

1. In **New password**, type the new password. Click the eye icon to show or hide it.
2. Watch the checklist below the box. Each rule turns green when it is met:
    - **8+ characters**
    - **Upper & lower case**
    - **2+ digits**
    - **1+ special character**
    - **Not a common or predictable password**
3. Click **Save changes**. The message **Your account details were updated.** appears and the box clears.
4. Sign in with the new password next time.

Leave **New password** blank to keep your current password.

A strong password also must not:

- be longer than 64 characters,
- contain runs or repeats like 1234, abcd, qwer or aaaa,
- contain your username, name or email.

Try a few unrelated words with digits and a symbol, for example Mango#River42Lamp.

### Check your role

The **Role** box shows the role your workspace gave you, such as **Super Admin**, **Manager** or **Executive**. You cannot change it here. **You are the account owner.** appears if you created the workspace. Otherwise, ask your workspace owner to change it. See [Manage team members, roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions.md).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Password needs: 8+ characters, 2+ digits** (or other rules) | The new password misses 1 or more rules. | Add what the message lists, then click **Save changes** again. |
| **This password is too common and easy to guess (like Password@123).** | The password is on the common-password list. | Use a few unrelated words, for example Mango#River42Lamp. |
| **Password must not contain sequences or repeats like 1234, abcd, qwer or aaaa.** | The password has a keyboard run or a repeated character. | Remove the run or repeat. |
| **Password must not contain your username, name or email.** | Part of your name or email is in the password. | Choose words that are not about you. |
| **Email … already exists with user: …** | Another active user already has that email. | Use a different email, or ask your workspace owner. |
| **Phone number is required.** | The phone box is empty. | Type your mobile number, then save. |
