# Read billing and usage

Billing & Usage shows Meta's charges for your WhatsApp messages and the usage of AI Smart Replies. At the end, you know how many messages were billable, which categories cost the most, and how you pay Meta. Pick 7, 30 or 90 days.

## Before you start

- You can open **Manage** in the **WhatsApp** panel. If not, ask your admin.
- Your WhatsApp number is connected to Ohanvi. See [Connect your WhatsApp number](connect-whatsapp-number.md).
- For your Ohanvi credits and top-ups, use [Credits and billing](../credits-and-billing.md). That is a different screen.

## What the screen shows

| Item | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Billable messages** | Template messages Meta delivered and charged for in the period. Some accounts see **Billable conversations** instead. |
| **Advance balance with Meta** | Prepaid credit with Meta. It draws down per message, or per conversation. |
| **By category** | Marketing, utility, authentication and service messages. Meta bills them at different rates. |
| **By pricing type** | Regular, free entry point and free tier. Some accounts see **By conversation type** instead. |
| **How you pay Meta** | Whether Meta is paid through Ohanvi or directly. |

## Steps

### Open Billing & Usage

1. Open **WhatsApp** in the left rail, then select **Manage**.
2. Select **Billing & Usage**. The title reads **Billing & Usage**, with **Meta conversation charges and AI usage** below it.

### Pick the data and the period

1. Choose **WhatsApp (Meta)** to see Meta's message charges.
2. Choose **AI Smart Replies** to see what AI replies used. See the section below.
3. Choose **7d**, **30d** or **90d**. The default is **30d**.

### Read your WhatsApp usage

1. Read **Billable messages** for the period, for example **Last 30 days**.
2. Read **Advance balance with Meta**, if your account has one.
3. Scroll to **By category** to see how marketing, utility, authentication and service messages split.
4. Scroll to **By pricing type** to see regular, free entry point and free tier messages.
5. Read **How Meta billing works** at the bottom. It says Meta charges the payment method on file in your Meta Business Manager.

### Choose how you pay Meta

1. Find the card **How you pay Meta**. It says **Switch anytime — this changes how WhatsApp usage is billed from your credits.**
2. Choose **Pay through Ohanvi** if you want Ohanvi to front Meta's credit line. Your credits are charged Meta's rate plus Ohanvi's commission.
3. Choose **Pay Meta directly** if you pay Meta on your own account. Your credits are charged only Ohanvi's usage commission.

4. Wait for the message **Billing method updated.**

### Pay Meta

1. Click **Pay to Meta** at the top. This button shows when a payment link is available.
2. Meta's payment settings open in a new tab. Pay there.

### See Meta's current rates

1. Scroll to **Exact ₹ rates on Meta's pricing page**.
2. Click **View Meta pricing**. Meta's own pricing page opens.

Ohanvi does not show exact rates. They change by category and region.

### Read AI Smart Replies usage

1. Choose **AI Smart Replies** at the top.
2. Read the charges for the period, shown as **AI charges · last 30 days** or as an estimated bill from your own AI provider.
3. Read **Tokens used**, **Prompt** and **Response**.
4. If you use your own AI key, click the **Go to … billing** link to pay that provider. Ohanvi does not bill it.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Could not load billing usage.** | The page could not fetch your usage. | Click **Retry**. |
| **Meta could not return usage figures** | Meta did not answer. | Click **Refresh** later. |
| **No conversation data for this period.** | You sent nothing in the period. | Pick a longer period, such as **90d**. |
| **Could not update billing method.** | The change did not save. | Choose the option again. |
| **Pay to Meta** is missing | No payment link is available for your account. | Pay from your Meta Business Manager. |
