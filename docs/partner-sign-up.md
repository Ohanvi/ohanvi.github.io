# Sign up as a partner

The partner programme is for people who help businesses use WhatsApp for their customers. You create a partner account and earn every month for as long as your clients stay active. Takes about 3 minutes.

## Before you start

- You have the partner sign-up link from Ohanvi. [VERIFY: the public partner sign-up address]
- You have a work email and mobile number.

## Steps

### Open the partner page

1. Open the partner sign-up link.
2. Read the **PARTNER PROGRAMME** panel. It lists **Recurring earnings**, **More value for your clients** and **Built to scale with you**.

### Create your partner account

1. Check the **PARTNER** badge on the form.
2. In **Enter your name**, **Enter your username**, **Enter your email** and **Enter your mobile number**, fill in your details.
3. In **Enter your password** and **Confirm your password**, set a password.
4. Click **Create an account**. Or click **Continue with Google**.
5. If you already have an account, click **I already have an account**.

The partner form hides the **Individual** and **Organization** switch, the organisation name and the referral code box.
