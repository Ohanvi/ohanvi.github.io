# Move leads through your stages

Work every lead from first enquiry to a deal in **CRM** → **Leads**. At the end, each lead sits in one of your own stages, and the leads that are ready have become a contact, an account and a deal in one step.

## Before you start

- You can see **CRM** in the left rail, with **Leads** in its panel. If not, ask your admin for CRM access.
- Optional: your lead stages match how your business works. Ohanvi starts you on **New**, **Contacted**, **Qualified**, **Unqualified**, **Lost** and **Converted**. See [Set up stages and fields](set-up-stages-and-fields.md).
- Optional: you have a list of leads in a file. See [Import leads and deals](import-leads-and-deals.md).

!!! note "Leads in CRM and in Contacts"
    **CRM** → **Leads** is the sales view of your leads, by stage. **Contacts** → **Leads** is the triage queue for new enquiries. Both show the same leads. See [Work with leads](leads.md).

## Steps

### Add a lead

1. Open **CRM** in the left rail, then select **Leads**. The screen opens on **List**.
2. Click **Add lead** at the top right. The lead form opens.
3. Fill **First Name** and a phone number. Fill the other fields you have, including your own fields at the bottom.
4. Click **Submit**. The lead appears in your first stage.

To add a lead straight into a stage, click **Board**, point at that stage's column and click **Add lead** at its foot.

### Work leads on the list

1. On **Leads**, keep **List** selected.
2. Click a lead. Its record opens on the right, with its score and why it scored that way.
3. Use the buttons on the record to call, message or convert the lead.

### Move leads on the board

1. On **Leads**, click **Board**. Each of your stages is a column of lead cards.
2. Drag a card to another column. The lead moves to that stage and its score is updated.
3. To move without dragging, right-click a card and pick a stage under **Move to**.
4. To find a lead, type in **Search leads**. The search looks through every lead, not only the cards on screen.

Each column header counts every lead in that stage. A column shows 20 cards at a time. Scroll down the column and the next 20 load by themselves.

Converted leads cannot be dragged. Their history stays on the lead.

### Convert a lead into a deal

1. On **Board**, drag the lead into your converted stage, for example **Converted**. Or click the convert icon on a card in your last working stage. The **Convert lead · …** window opens.
2. Keep **Create a new contact** selected. If the lead's email or phone matches a contact you already have, pick **Link the existing contact** instead.
3. Keep **Create account "…"** selected to save the lead's company as an account.
4. Keep **Create a deal** selected. Check **Deal name**, **Amount (₹)** and **Expected close**.
5. Pick the **Pipeline** and the **Starting stage** for the deal.
6. Click **Convert & open deal** to go to the new deal, or **Convert** to stay on the board.

The lead is kept and marked converted. A follow-up task is added for the deal owner, due in 2 days.

!!! tip "Leads move on their own"
    You do not need to drag every card. A completed call, email or WhatsApp against a lead in your first stage moves it to the next stage. A meeting held moves it to your last working stage, ready to convert.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A converted lead's card does not drag | Converted leads are closed. | Open the lead from **List** to read its history. Work the deal from **Deals**. |
| Dropping a card on **Converted** opens a window instead of moving it | Converting creates the contact, account and deal, so it asks first. | Fill the **Convert lead** window and click **Convert**. |
| A column shows fewer cards than its count | A column loads 20 cards at a time. | Scroll down the column. The next cards load as you reach the end. |
| A lead is missing from the search | The search matches name, company, email and phone. | Search by one of those, or open **List**. |
| **Could not move the lead.** | The move was refused or the connection dropped. | The card goes back. Try again, or refresh the page. |
