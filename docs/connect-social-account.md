# Connect a social media account

Authorize a brand channel so posts can be drafted, approved, and scheduled from Ohanvi.

## Before you start

- You are signed in as an Ohanvi admin.
- You have admin access to the social page or channel.
- Pop-ups are allowed in your browser for the authorization window.

## Connect the account

1. Open **Social** from the left navigation.
2. Select **Connected accounts**.
3. Choose the channel you want to connect.
4. Click **Connect**.
5. Sign in to the channel account.
6. Approve the requested permissions.
7. Return to Ohanvi after the success message appears.

## Verify the connection

1. Check that the account status shows **Connected**.
2. Confirm the correct page name and profile image appear.
3. Create a short draft post.
4. Confirm the connected page appears in the channel picker.
