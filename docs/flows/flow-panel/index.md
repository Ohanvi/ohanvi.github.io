# The flow panel

The flow panel opens beside the **Flows** list when you click a flow. It shows what the flow does, how its runs went, and its history. From here you can also test, publish, pause or open the builder.

**Where to find it:** open **WhatsApp** in the left rail, select **Flows**, then click a flow.

## Guides

| Tab | Guide | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Overview** | [Understand flows and the Flows list](../flows-overview-and-list.md) | You want to read a flow's steps, publish it, turn it on or off, copy or delete it. |
| **Recent runs** | [Test a flow and fix failed runs](../test-and-monitor-flows.md) | You want to run a test, read the run history or stop runs. |
| **Latest output** | [Read a run's output in detail](../read-a-runs-output.md) | You want to see what each step returned and why one failed. |
| **Versions** | [Review a flow's versions](../review-flow-versions.md) | You want to see which version is live and who published each one. |

## Before you start

- You can see **Flows** in the **WhatsApp** panel. If not, ask your admin.
- To publish, test or change a flow, your role also needs save access to flows.
