# Read Google review stats

See your Google rating, how it changed over time, and what happened on your review page: scans, drafts made, copies, Google opens and private notes. See what customers told you too: their stars, their answers to your questions and the names they picked. Use it to check whether asking for reviews is working, and what to fix. It takes about 2 minutes.

## Before you start

- Your review page is set up and linked to your Google listing. See [Get more Google reviews](get-more-google-reviews.md).

## Steps

### Open Stats

1. Click **More** on the left rail, then select **Google Reviews** → **Stats**.
2. If you have several branches, pick the branch in **Branch:**. See [Switch between branches](manage-branches.md#switch-between-branches).
3. If your page is not set up yet, the screen says **Set up your review page first**. Click **Open Review page** and finish setup.

### Read the tiles

The tiles at the top show:

| Tile | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Google rating** | Your listing's star rating on Google Maps. |
| **Google reviews** | The number of reviews on your Google listing. |
| **Scans today** | Times your review page was opened today, from the QR code or the link. |
| **Private notes** | Private notes customers sent you. See [Read private notes](read-private-notes.md). |

**Google rating** and **Google reviews** are marked **Data from Google Maps**. They refresh about once a week, so they can lag behind your listing. If no listing is confirmed, they read **Confirm your listing to see it**.

### Read the funnel

The **Funnel** shows how far customers got:

| Step | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| **Scans** | People who opened your review page. |
| **Drafts made** | Times the AI wrote drafts for a customer. One tap of **Write my review** counts once, for 3 drafts. |
| **Copies** | Times a customer copied a review to paste on Google. |
| **Google opens** | Customers sent to Google's review form. |

Under **Drafts made**, **Copies** and **Google opens**, a line such as **40% of scans** compares the step with **Scans**.

**Copies** is lower than **Google opens** when customers open Google with no text to copy, or when their phone blocks copying.

!!! note "Google opens are not posted reviews"
    Google doesn't tell Ohanvi who posted. A Google open means a customer reached Google's review form, not that they posted. Your real review count is in **Google reviews** above.

### Read your rating over time

The **Google rating over time** chart shows your Google rating as Ohanvi checked it, about once a week. Under the chart, a line such as **On 20 Oct: ★ 4.6 · 128 Google reviews** gives the latest check. The chart is marked **Data from Google Maps**.

The chart needs at least 2 checks. Until then it reads **The chart fills in as we check your rating on Google, about once a week. Confirm your Google listing to start.**

### Read the daily chart

The **Per day** chart shows each day of the last 30 days: scans, drafts made, copies, Google opens and private notes. Use it to spot the days when you asked fewer customers.

### See what customers told you

The **What customers told you** card adds up what customers picked on your review page. Pick **30 days**, **90 days** or **1 year** to choose the period.

| Part | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Visits | The number of visits in the period, such as **42** with **Visits in the last 30 days**. See how they are counted below. |
| **Stars given** | How many visits gave 5, 4, 3, 2 and 1 stars. |
| Each of your questions | The question, how many customers answered it, and the share of them who picked each answer. |
| **Names customers tapped** | The **Staff**, **Services** and **Products** customers picked, with how many times each. |
| **Languages** | The page languages customers used, with each one's share of visits. |

How visits are counted:

- Each tap of **Write my review** is 1 visit, even when no drafts come back.
- Each private note is 1 visit.
- A customer who taps **Write my review** and also sends a private note counts as 2 visits. A customer who taps **Write my review** twice counts twice.
- A scan with no tap of **Write my review** and no note doesn't count.

The card holds no personal data. It shows counts only, never who picked what. Answers are kept for 400 days, then deleted.

If you remove a question or an answer, the numbers already counted stay:

- A removed answer shows inside its own question, under **No longer on your page**, after the current answers.
- A removed question shows after your current questions, under **No longer on your page**.

With several branches, **Stats** shows the branch picked in **Branch:**. To see every branch together, open **Branches**. Below the **All N branches** card, a separate **What customers told you** card adds up all branches. It shows only when you have 2 or more branches.

Staff names show here even when **Staff names in drafts** is off. See [Get more Google reviews](get-more-google-reviews.md#set-up-the-page).

### Compare branches

When you have several branches, **Stats** also shows the card **All** followed by the number of branches. It adds up the last 30 days of every branch: **Scans**, **Drafts made**, **Copies**, **Google opens** and **Private notes**, with how many notes are unread.

The tiles, funnel and charts above it are for the branch picked in **Branch:**. Click **Manage branches** on the card to see each branch's numbers. See [Manage branches](manage-branches.md).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google rating** reads **Confirm your listing to see it** | No Google listing is confirmed. A pasted link does not bring the rating. | Confirm your business on **Review page**. See [Get more Google reviews](get-more-google-reviews.md#confirm-your-business-on-google). |
| **Google reviews** is lower than on Google Maps | The count refreshes about once a week. | Check your listing on Google Maps for today's number. |
| **Google rating over time** shows no chart | The rating has been checked fewer than 2 times, or no listing is confirmed. | Confirm your listing and check back in 2 weeks. |
| The numbers look low with several branches | The tiles show only the branch picked in **Branch:**. | Read the **All** branches card at the bottom, or pick another branch. |
| **Google opens** is high, but few new reviews | Some customers open Google and don't post. | No fix needed. Keep asking every customer. |
| **Drafts made** is 0 while scans go up | **AI review drafts** is off, the **Daily AI limit** is reached, or the branch has no **Google Reviews Annual Pack** and your credits can't cover ₹5. | Check **Settings** on **Review page**, [add funds](../add-funds.md), or [buy the Annual Pack for the branch](buy-the-annual-pack.md). |
| **Scans** is 0 | The QR code or link is not shared, or the page is off. | Check that **Page is live** is on and share the current link. |
| **What customers told you** has fewer visits than **Scans** | A visit counts only when a customer taps **Write my review** or sends a private note. Customers who scan and leave, or tap **Write it myself**, aren't counted. | No fix needed. |
| **What customers told you** has more visits than **Drafts made** | A tap of **Write my review** counts as a visit even when no drafts come back, and a private note is a visit of its own. | No fix needed. |
| A question shows no answers | No customer answered it in the period, or you added it recently. | Pick a longer period, such as **90 days**. |
