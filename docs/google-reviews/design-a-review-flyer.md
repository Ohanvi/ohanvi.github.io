# Design a review flyer

Design a printable flyer that carries your review page's QR code, then download it as a PNG image or a PDF in A6, A5, A4 or as a table tent. Customers scan it at your counter and post the review on Google themselves. It takes about 5 minutes.

## Before you start

- Your review page is set up and linked to your Google listing. See [Get more Google reviews](get-more-google-reviews.md).
- Your review page is turned on.
- To print your name and logo, **Business name** and **Logo URL** are set in the **Review page** settings.
- With several branches, each branch has its own flyer and QR code. Pick the branch in **Branch:** at the top. See [Manage branches](manage-branches.md).

## Steps

### Open the flyer

1. Click **More** on the left rail, then select **Google Reviews** → **Flyer**.
2. Read the line at the top: **Print this flyer for your counter or reception. Customers scan the code, pick what they liked and post the review on Google themselves.**
3. If a warning shows under it, click **Go to Review page** and fix it before you print:
    - **Your review page is off, so this QR code opens a "not found" page. Turn the page on before you print.**
    - **Your review page isn't linked to your Google listing yet, so it stays closed. Finish setup before you print.**

If you have no review page yet, the screen says **Set up your review page first**. Click **Go to Review page**.

### Design it

The **Design** card is on the left, or on top on a small screen. The **Preview** card shows the flyer as you change it.

1. Under **Theme**, pick **Classic**, **Fresh green**, **Warm amber** or **Dark**.
2. In **Headline**, type a short line above the QR code, up to 60 characters. The counter shows how many you have used. The default is `Loved your visit? Tell Google.`
3. Turn **Show business name** on to print your business name above the headline.
4. Turn **Show logo** on to print your logo. With no logo set, your initials are shown.
5. Drag **QR code size** between 100 and 240. A bigger code is easier to scan from further away.
6. Turn **Logo in QR code** on to put your logo in the middle of the code. Phones still read it. With no logo on the review page, the code prints without one.
7. Under **QR code colour**, pick a colour swatch, or type a colour code such as `#1E3A8A`. Dark colours scan best. The default is `#000000`, black.
8. Under **Paper size**, pick **A6 (105 × 148 mm)**, **A5 (148 × 210 mm)**, **A4 (210 × 297 mm)** or **Table tent (A4, folded)**. The default is A5.
9. Check the **Preview**. Under the QR code it shows the steps **1 Scan**, **2 Pick**, **3 Post** and the line **Takes under a minute · Opens Google reviews**.
10. Click **Save flyer**. The message **Flyer saved** appears. Your design opens the same way next time.

To drop your changes, click **Undo changes**. It brings back the last saved design.

!!! warning "Keep the QR code dark"
    Phones need strong contrast to read a QR code. A light colour shows **Pick a darker QR colour so phones can scan it.** You cannot save or download the flyer until you pick a darker one.

!!! warning "No rewards in the headline"
    Google doesn't allow offers in exchange for reviews. A headline with words such as discount, offer, free, cashback, coupon, voucher, gift, prize, reward or "% off" shows **Remove the reward. Google doesn't allow offers in exchange for reviews.** You cannot save or download the flyer until you remove it.

### Download and print

1. Under the preview, click **Download PNG** or **Download PDF**. The button reads **Preparing…** while the file is made.
2. The file saves as `google-review-flyer.png` or `google-review-flyer.pdf`. The message **Flyer downloaded** or **Flyer PDF downloaded** appears. When the app saves to a folder, it reads **Flyer saved to** or **Flyer PDF saved to**, followed by the file's location.
3. Print it and put it where customers wait or pay.

Downloads use the design shown here, saved or not. The PDF prints at full paper size:

| Paper size | The PDF |
| --- | --- |
| A6, A5 or A4 | One page of that size. |
| Table tent | One A4 landscape page with the flyer twice. The top copy is upside down. |

### Fold a table tent

1. Pick **Table tent (A4, folded)** and download the PDF.
2. Print it on A4 at full size.
3. Fold along the dashed line.
4. Stand it on a table. Both sides show the flyer.

!!! tip
    Scan the printed flyer with your own phone before you put it out. It should open your review page.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Remove the reward. Google doesn't allow offers in exchange for reviews.** | The headline mentions a reward. | Rewrite the headline without the offer. |
| **Keep the headline to 60 characters.** | The headline is too long. | Shorten it. |
| **Download PNG** and **Download PDF** do nothing, and the preview says **Remove the reward from the headline to download the flyer.** | The headline has a reward word. | Remove the reward from the headline. |
| The printed QR code opens a "not found" page | The review page is off, a new QR code was made, or the page is not linked to Google. | Click **Go to Review page**, fix the page, then download and print again. |
| The flyer shows initials instead of a logo | No logo is set on the review page. | Add a logo on **Review page**, or turn **Show logo** off. |
| Customers can't scan the code from a distance | The QR code is small. | Move **QR code size** up, or pick a bigger **Paper size**, then download again. |
| **Pick a darker QR colour so phones can scan it.** | The QR code colour is too light. | Pick one of the swatches, or a darker colour code. |
| **Use a colour code like #1A2B3C.** | The colour code is not 6 characters after `#`. | Type a full code, such as `#1E3A8A`. |
| The preview says **Fix the QR code colour to download the flyer.** | The QR code colour can't be used. | Fix the colour under **QR code colour**. |
| The QR code prints without your logo | No logo is set on the review page. | Add a **Logo URL** on **Review page**, then download again. |
| The table tent's sides don't line up | The PDF was printed scaled to fit. | Print at full size, without scaling. |
