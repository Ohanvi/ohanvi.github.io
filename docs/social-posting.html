Sample guide

Schedule a post across connected channels

Prepare content once, preview it per channel, and publish it at the best time.

Beginner8 minutesSocial Posting

Steps

  1. Open Social Media, then select Composer.
  2. Choose the channels for publishing.
  3. Add caption, media, hashtags, and tracking links.
  4. Preview each channel and resolve validation warnings.
  5. Select Schedule and choose date and time.

Media placeholder

Tips

  • Use UTM links when the post is part of a campaign.
  • Keep channel-specific captions short enough for mobile previews.
