# See what is scheduled for today

Use the **Today** view to see everything scheduled for today across WhatsApp, CRM, Email, Social and Appointments. At the end you will know what goes out today, who owns it, and which items need a change.

## Before you start

- You can see **Schedule** in the left rail. By default only the workspace's Super Admin role has it. Ask your admin if it is missing.
- Something is scheduled for today in at least one module. You create items in their own module.

## Steps

### Open Today

1. Open **Schedule** in the left rail, then select **Today**. The page title reads **Today** and the page opens in **List** view.
2. Read the list. Rows are in time order under one day heading, **Today**.
3. Each row shows the time, the title, the channel, the type and a status pill, for example **WhatsApp · Broadcast** and **Scheduled**.
4. Click a row. Its details open on the right: **When**, **Recurrence**, **Audience / with**, **Owner**, **Channel** and **Type**.

!!! note
    **Today** shows items of every status for today, including **Sent** and **Cancelled** ones. By the evening the list also shows what already went out.

### Narrow the list

1. Click a channel chip: **WhatsApp**, **CRM**, **Email**, **Social** or **Appointments**. The first click shows only that channel. Click more chips to add channels back.
2. Click **Everyone's schedule** and choose **My schedule** to see only items you own.
3. Click **Any status** and choose **Scheduled**, **Draft**, **Sent** or **Cancelled**.

### Change an item that is due today

1. Click the item. Its details open on the right.
2. Click **Reschedule** to move it, **Edit** to open it in its own module, **Cancel** to call it off, or **Delete** to remove it. See [Reschedule, cancel or delete a scheduled item](reschedule-cancel-delete.md).

### Look at today on the calendar

1. Click **Calendar** at the top of the page. The month view opens.
2. Find today's date. Only today's items are shown, because **Today** is still the filter.
3. Click **List** to go back.

### Add something new

1. Click **New schedule**. The **New schedule — pick a channel** sheet opens.
2. Choose **New in WhatsApp**, **New in CRM**, **New in Email**, **New in Social** or **New in Appointments**. Ohanvi opens that module's own screen, where you create the item.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nothing scheduled here** with the message **No schedules match these filters. Try another channel or clear the status filter.** | Nothing is due today, or a channel, owner or status filter hides it. | Click another channel chip, choose **Everyone's schedule**, or set the status to **Any status**. |
| An item due today is missing | It has no date and time, or it belongs to a channel you switched off. | Give the item a scheduled time in its own module. Turn the channel chip back on. |
| **Could not load your schedule.** | The page could not reach the server. | Click the retry button on the message. If it keeps failing, sign out and sign in again. |
| **Reschedule**, **Edit**, **Cancel** and **Delete** are not visible | The details pane is hidden on narrow windows. | Widen the browser window. |
