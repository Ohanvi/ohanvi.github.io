# Log activities and notes

Schedule calls, meetings and follow-up tasks against a lead, contact, account or deal, and write notes your whole team can read. At the end, you have one list of what is overdue, due today and due this week, plus a calendar and a notes feed.

## Before you start

- You can see **CRM** in the left rail. If not, ask your admin for CRM access.
- The lead, contact, account or deal you want to log against already exists. See [Create and manage deals](manage-deals.md).

**Activities**, **Activity Calendar** and **Notes** are not listed in the CRM panel. Open them from the search box at the top (**Search contacts, campaigns, templates…**), or press **Ctrl K** (**⌘K** on a Mac) and type the screen name.

## Steps

### Add an activity

1. Press **Ctrl K**, type **Activities**, and select it. The **Activities** screen opens.
2. Click **Add task**. The **Add Activity** form opens.
3. Pick a **Type**: **CALL**, **MEETING** or **TASK**.
4. In **Subject**, type what it is about, for example `Demo call with Asha`.
5. In **Related To**, pick the lead, contact, account or deal it belongs to.
6. Set the **Activity Status** (it starts as **PLANNED**) and the **Priority** (it starts as **MEDIUM**).
7. Set the time:
    - For a task, pick the **Due Date (task)**.
    - For a call or meeting, pick the **Start Date (call/meeting)**, **Start Time**, **End Date** and **End Time**.
8. Optional: fill **Location (meeting)**, or **Call Direction** and **Call Duration (min)** for a call.
9. Optional: pick a **Reminder Date** and **Reminder Time** to be reminded.
10. Click **Submit**. The activity shows in the list.

To add a follow-up from a deal instead, open the deal's full record, go to **Notes & Activities**, type in **Add a follow-up task…** and click **Add Task**.

### Work through your list

1. On **Activities**, keep **List** selected. Activities are grouped into **Overdue**, **Today** and **This week**.
2. Tick an activity when it is done. The message **Task completed** appears.
3. Tick a completed activity to open it again. The message **Task re-opened** appears.

### See activities on a calendar

1. On **Activities**, click **Calendar** to see the month with a count on each day.
2. For a fuller calendar, press **Ctrl K**, type **Activity Calendar**, and select it.
3. Switch between the month, week, day and schedule views [VERIFY: view switcher labels]. Calls and meetings sit on their scheduled time. Tasks sit on their due date.
4. Click **Refresh** to load changes.

### Write a note

1. Press **Ctrl K**, type **Notes**, and select it. The **Notes** feed opens, newest first.
2. Click **Add note**. The **Add Note** form opens.
3. In **Related To**, pick who or what the note is about.
4. Optional: add a **Title**.
5. In **Note**, type the note. It is required.
6. Optional: tick **Pinned** to pin the note.
7. Click **Submit**.

To read notes next to your tasks, click **Notes** on the **Activities** screen. To add a note from a deal, open the deal's **Notes & Activities** tab, type in **Add a note…** and click **Add Note**.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Subject is required.** | **Subject** is empty. | Type a subject and submit again. |
| **End date cannot be before start date.** | **End Date** is earlier than **Start Date (call/meeting)**. | Fix the dates. |
| **Note body is required.** | **Note** is empty. | Type the note and submit again. |
| **Could not update that task.** | The change did not save. | Refresh the page and tick the activity again. |
| An activity is missing from **Overdue**, **Today** and **This week** | It is due later, or it has no due or start date. | Open **Activity Calendar** to find it, and give it a date. |
| **Nothing due** | You have no activities scheduled yet. | Click **Add task**. |
