# Create and publish a social post

Write a post once in the composer and send it to every connected network you pick. You can add photos or a video, use the AI Assistant, preview each channel, and then publish right away or save a draft. To publish later instead, see [Schedule posts and manage the queue](schedule-social-posts.md).

## Before you start

- You can see **Social** in the left rail, with **Publish** in its panel. If not, ask your admin to give your role Social access. See the [Settings overview](../settings/index.md).
- At least one network is connected. See [Connect social accounts](connect-social-accounts.md).

## Steps

### Open the composer

1. Open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Publish**.
2. Click **New Post**. The **Create Post** window opens.

You can also click **New** at the top of the **Social** panel, then click **Post**.

If you closed the composer earlier without saving, Ohanvi asks **Resume Unfinished Post?**. Click **Resume Post** to continue it, or **Discard** to start fresh.

### Pick the networks

1. In the channel strip, click each network you want to post to. A post can go to several networks at once.
2. Hover over a grey chip to see why it is off. **… — connect it in Integrations → Social Connections** means the network is not connected yet.

The character limit shown while you write is the tightest limit of the networks you picked:

| Network | Character limit |
| --- | --- |
| **X** | 280 |
| **Threads** | 500 |
| **Google Business** | 1,500 |
| **Instagram** | 2,200 |
| **LinkedIn** | 3,000 |
| **Facebook** | 63,206 |

### Write the post

1. Type your text in the box that says **Start writing or get inspired with Templates**.
2. Use the toolbar to add an **Emoji** or a **Hashtag**, or to make selected text **Bold**, **Italic** or **Underline**.
3. To start from saved text, click **Templates** at the top of the composer, then pick a template in **Start from a template**. Replace each `[bracketed]` blank with your own words.
4. To let AI draft the text, click **AI Assistant**. Describe the post under **What should this post be about?**, then click **Generate post**.

!!! note
    Bold, italic and underlined characters count for more than 1 character on some networks. Underline adds 1 character per letter.

### Add photos or video

1. Click **Add photos or videos**, or drag files onto the **Drag & drop or select a file** area.
2. If the **Crop image** window opens, pick a shape and click **Crop**. Shapes include **Square** (1:1), **Instagram portrait** (4:5), **Twitter / X** (16:9) and **Stories / Reels** (9:16). One crop is used for every selected channel.

Media rules:

- Each image can be up to 25 MB. Each video can be up to 200 MB.
- Videos are MP4, MOV, M4V, WEBM, AVI or MKV files.
- **Instagram** does not accept text-only posts. Add a photo, or deselect Instagram.
- **Facebook** does not accept video posts. Remove the video, or deselect Facebook.

### Set Google Business options

These appear under **Google Business options** when Google Business is one of your channels.

1. Pick the post type: **Standard**, **Event**, **Offer** or **Alert**.
2. For an **Event**, fill in **Event title**, **Starts** and **Ends**.
3. For an **Offer**, fill in **Coupon code**, **Redeem URL** and **Terms & conditions**.
4. Optionally, add a **Link (optional)**. It shows under the post body on Google.
5. Optionally, pick a **Call to action**, such as **BOOK**, **ORDER**, **SHOP**, **LEARN_MORE**, **SIGN_UP** or **CALL**, then fill in **CTA URL**.

### Preview and publish

1. Click **Preview** to see how the post looks on each selected network.
2. Choose what to do:
    - Click **Publish Now** to send the post to every selected network. The message **Publishing to … — track it under Sent.** appears.
    - Click **Save Draft** to keep it for later. The message **Post saved as draft — publish it when ready** appears.
    - Click **Schedule** to pick a date and time. See [Schedule posts and manage the queue](schedule-social-posts.md).

To edit a draft later, open **Social** → **Publish**, click the **Drafts** tab, and click the **Edit** icon on the post.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The main button says **Connect a Channel to Post** and is greyed out | No network is picked. | Click a network in the channel strip. |
| **Post text is required** | The text box is empty. | Type the post text. |
| **Post text is over the … character … limit** | The text is longer than the tightest network you picked allows. | Shorten the text, or deselect the network with the lower limit. |
| **Instagram needs a photo or video — add one, or deselect it.** | Instagram has no text-only posts. | Add a photo, or deselect Instagram. |
| **Facebook does not accept video posts — remove the video, or deselect it.** | Ohanvi cannot post video to a Facebook Page. | Remove the video, or deselect Facebook. |
| **Too large to attach: … (over 25 MB)** | The file is over the size limit. | Compress the file and add it again. |
