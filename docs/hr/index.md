# HR

HR keeps your employee records and attendance in one place. Staff who don't use the app can still be added with a name and a WhatsApp number, mark attendance by sending a word on WhatsApp, and ask for their attendance by chat.

**Where to find it:** open **HR** in the sidebar. Attendance has its own menu items, including **Attendance Dashboard** and **WhatsApp attendance**. [VERIFY: HR entry name in the left rail]

## Guides

| Guide | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| [Add staff](add-staff.md) | You want to add people who work over WhatsApp, without a login or a paid seat. |
| [Mark attendance on WhatsApp](whatsapp-attendance.md) | You want staff to check in, check out and take breaks by sending a word. |
| [Read attendance reports and the dashboard](attendance-reports-and-dashboard.md) | You want to see who is in today, or send attendance reports by chat. |

## Before you start

- Your role has HR access.
- Your business has a WhatsApp number connected to Ohanvi, for the WhatsApp guides.
