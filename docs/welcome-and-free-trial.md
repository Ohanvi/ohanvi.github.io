# Start your free trial

Right after you create an account, the **Welcome aboard** screen starts your 14-day free trial. You can pick a plan now or skip and subscribe later. The trial runs either way. Takes about 1 minute.

## Before you start

- You have created your account. See [Sign up for Ohanvi](sign-up.md).

## Steps

### Read the welcome screen

1. After sign-up, the **Welcome aboard** screen opens.
2. Read the line under it. It says your account is ready and your 14-day free trial has started.
3. Read the note that follows. Your trial runs either way. Subscribing now only means you keep going without a break when the trial ends.

### Choose how many users

1. Under **How many people will use it?**, use the minus and plus buttons to set the number of users.
2. The screen says this is only so plans can be priced for you. You can change it any time.
3. Click **See the plans**.

### Pick a plan

1. Read the plan cards. If there are none, the screen says **No plans are available yet.**
2. Click **Choose** and a plan's name, for example the button reads **Choose** with that plan's name. The checkout title reads **Choose a plan for** your number **users**.
3. Complete payment. See [Manage your plan and users](billing/manage-plan-and-users.md).
4. To go back, click **Back**.

### Skip and keep the trial

1. Click **Continue on the free trial**, or **Skip — continue on the free trial**.
2. Ohanvi opens **Home**. Your trial keeps running.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No plans are available yet.** | No plans are published for your account. | Click **Continue on the free trial** and check again later. |
| You skipped the plan screen. | You chose the free trial. | Subscribe any time from **Plans & Pricing**. See [Plans and pricing](billing/plans-and-pricing.md). |
