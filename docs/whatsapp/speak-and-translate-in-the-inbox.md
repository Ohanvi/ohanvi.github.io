# Speak and translate in the inbox

Reply to customers in their language without typing it yourself. At the end, you can speak in your own language and get a translated reply or voice note, translate a typed draft before you send it, and read any message in the chat in the language you pick.

## Before you start

- You can see **Inbox** in the **WhatsApp** panel and a chat is open. Open it at [app.ohanvi.com/#/whatsapp/team-inbox](https://app.ohanvi.com/#/whatsapp/team-inbox). See [Use the WhatsApp inbox](use-whatsapp-inbox.md).
- You have the **Admin**, **Manager** or **Executive** WhatsApp role. Every WhatsApp role can use this. See [Add WhatsApp agents](add-whatsapp-agents.md).
- Your browser can use the microphone, for the voice parts.
- The chat is inside the 24-hour window. Outside it, WhatsApp delivers only approved templates. See [Use the WhatsApp inbox](use-whatsapp-inbox.md#reply-after-the-24-hour-window-closes).
- You have credits. Speech, voice notes and translations are paid from your balance. See [What it costs](#what-it-costs).

## Where the buttons are

| Layout | Where you find them |
| --- | --- |
| Computer | In the toolbar above the reply box: the microphone (**Speak and translate**), the language chip next to it, for example **HI → EN**, and **Translate** (the languages icon) after it. |
| Ohanvi mobile app | The microphone in the reply bar opens a sheet with the same settings and a large record button. A translate icon appears inside the message field once you have typed something. |

## Steps

### Choose your languages

1. Open a chat in [WhatsApp → Inbox](https://app.ohanvi.com/#/whatsapp/team-inbox).
2. Click the language chip next to the microphone, for example **HI → EN**. The language settings open.
3. Under **I speak**, type to search and pick the language you speak. Pick **Detect automatically** to let Ohanvi work it out.
4. Under **Write in**, type to search and pick the language your reply goes out in:
    - **Customer's language** — the default. Ohanvi detects it from the chat.
    - **Same as spoken (no translation)** — your words are written as you say them.
    - **Hindi in English letters** — Hindi written in the English alphabet.
    - Any other language from the list.
5. Click the swap button to exchange **I speak** and **Write in**.
6. Under **You get**, pick **Text in reply box** or **Voice note**.
7. Click **Done**. Your choices are saved for you and used in every chat until you change them.

### Speak a reply and get text

1. Check that **You get** is **Text in reply box**.
2. Click the microphone. Allow the microphone if your browser asks.
3. Speak. You can record up to 3 minutes.
4. Click **Stop**. Click **Discard** instead to throw the recording away.
5. Read the translated text in the reply box. A strip above it shows, for example, **Translated from Hindi to English** and **Heard: …** with your original words.
6. Optional: click **Use original** to put back your words as you said them, or **Undo** to remove the text.
7. Edit the text if needed, then click **Send Reply**. Nothing is sent until you do.

### Speak a reply and send a voice note

1. Click the language chip, set **You get** to **Voice note**, then click **Done**.
2. Click the microphone and speak.
3. Click **Stop**. A preview player appears.
4. Play the preview to listen.
5. Click **Send voice note** to send it. Click **Record again** to replace it, or **Discard** to delete it.

The customer receives a normal WhatsApp voice note in the language set under **Write in**.

### Translate a typed draft

1. Type your reply in the reply box.
2. Click **Translate**. Its tooltip reads **Translate draft to** and the language, for example **Translate draft to English**.
3. Read the new text. Your draft is rewritten in that language, and a strip shows **You typed: …** with your original words.
4. Optional: click **Use original** to put back your draft, or **Undo** to reverse the translation.
5. Click **Send Reply**.

If the draft is already in that language, you see **Already in English. Choose another language under Write in to translate it.**

### Translate a message in the chat

1. Hover over any message in the chat.
2. Click the round translate icon. Its tooltip is **Translate**.
3. Read the translation under the original message. It shows **From Hindi to** and a language picker.
4. Optional: pick another language in the picker to translate it again.
5. On a message you sent, click **Use in reply** to put the translation in the reply box. It goes out as a new message, because WhatsApp does not let a business edit a sent message.
6. Click **Hide** to close the translation.

Once a message is translated into a language, anyone on your team can reopen that translation.

### Use it on the Ohanvi mobile app

1. Open a chat in the Ohanvi mobile app.
2. To translate a message, long-press it, then tap **Translate**.
3. To speak a reply, tap the microphone. A sheet opens with the same **I speak**, **Write in** and **You get** settings and a large record button.
4. To translate a draft, type it, then tap the translate icon inside the message field.

## Languages

**Indian languages:** Hindi, English, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Odia, Assamese, Urdu, Nepali, Sanskrit, Konkani, Maithili, Sindhi, Kashmiri, Dogri, Manipuri, Bodo and Santali. **Hindi in English letters** is available under **Write in** only.

**World languages:** Arabic, Chinese, Dutch, French, German, Greek, Hebrew, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Persian, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Swahili, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian, Vietnamese, Filipino, Sinhala, Burmese, Khmer, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Czech, Hungarian and Romanian.

## What it costs

Each use is taken from your credits. The inbox does not show prices. See the rates in [Credits and billing](../credits-and-billing.md#ai-in-the-inbox).

- **Speech** is charged per started minute of recording.
- **A voice note** is charged per character when it is made, even if you discard the preview.
- **A text translation** is charged once for a draft, or once for a message into one language. Reopening that message translation is free for your whole team.
- **Nothing is charged** when no speech is heard, when the AI fails, or when the text is already in that language.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No speech heard. Check the microphone isn't muted and speak a little closer. Nothing was charged.** | The recording had no words in it. | Unmute the microphone, then click **Try again** and speak closer. |
| The microphone does not start, or the browser says it is blocked | The browser blocked the microphone for Ohanvi. | Click the lock icon in the address bar, allow **Microphone** in the site settings, then reload the page. |
| **Access Denied** when you translate | Your account's roles have not received the new translate permission yet. | It is granted when your account is updated. Contact Ohanvi support if it persists. |
| The voice note is refused as too long | A voice note can hold at most 2,000 characters. | Set **You get** to **Text in reply box**, or split the reply into shorter recordings. |
| **Already in English. Choose another language under Write in to translate it.** | The draft is already in the **Write in** language. | Pick another language under **Write in**, or send the draft as it is. |
| Recording stops on its own | A recording is limited to 3 minutes. At 3 minutes it stops and uses what you said so far. | Speak the rest in a second recording. |
