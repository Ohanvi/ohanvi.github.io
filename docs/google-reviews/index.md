# Google Reviews

Google Reviews gives your business a review page, a QR code and a link that help customers post a Google review in their own words. You confirm your business on Google, share the code, and track how many customers reach Google's review form.

## Where to find it

Click **More** on the left rail, then open the **Google Reviews** section. If you move it onto the rail, it shows there as **Reviews**. See [Customize the left rail](../customize-the-left-rail.md).

The section lists:

- **Review page** — set up your page, confirm your business on Google, and share the QR code and link.
- **Flyer** — design a printable flyer with your QR code.
- **Stats** — your Google rating, review count, and how many customers reached Google.
- **Private notes** — notes that 1 to 3 star customers chose to send you as well as posting on Google.
- **Profile check** — what your Google listing is missing, and how it compares with businesses nearby.
- **Auto-reply** — draft replies to new Google reviews, and approve them or let them post. This needs Google Business Profile.
- **Branches** — a review page, QR code and numbers for each branch.
- **Settings** — the AI model that writes drafts and replies. It is your organisation's default AI model.

**Private notes** needs its own permission, so a teammate may see the other items but not this one.

With several branches, every screen shows **Branch:** at the top. Pick the branch you want to work on. See [Manage branches](manage-branches.md).

## How it works for your customer

1. The customer scans your QR code, or taps the link you shared.
2. Your review page opens on their phone, in the language you picked. They can switch to another of the 11 page languages.
3. They pick 1 to 5 stars and answer 3 or 4 short questions about your kind of business by tapping an answer, such as **Excellent**. They can add a few words in the box **Anything else? e.g. the service you used**.
4. If you added names, they can tap who helped them and what they came for or bought.
5. They can add a few words of their own, typed or spoken with **Speak instead**.
6. They tap **Write my review**. Ohanvi's AI offers 3 drafts, **Short**, **Detailed** and **Warm**, made only from what they picked and said. For 4 or 5 stars, the drafts mention your business, the service and your area once, naturally. For 1 to 3 stars, they are short and plain.
7. They tap **Copy & post on Google** on a draft. The text is copied and Google's review form opens.
8. They choose their stars on Google, paste the text and tap **Post**.

They can edit a draft, or tap **Write it myself** and write their own. Customers who pick 1 to 3 stars can also send you a private note. The Google button stays on their screen.

!!! note "Customers post the review themselves"
    Ohanvi never posts a review for a customer. Google has no way to do that. The review goes up only when the customer taps **Post** on Google.

## Before you start

- Your role has access to Google Reviews. If you cannot see it under **More**, ask your admin. See [Team, roles and permissions](../settings/roles-and-permissions.md).
- Your business has a listing on Google Maps.

You do not need to connect Google Business Profile to collect reviews, run a profile check or show the website widget. You need it only to read and reply to reviews inside Ohanvi, and for auto-reply. See [Reply to Google reviews](../social/reply-to-google-reviews.md).

## Google's rules

Google removes reviews, and can restrict your listing, when you break its review rules. Google Reviews is built to follow them. Keep to them in what you say and print too.

- **Don't offer rewards.** No discounts, freebies, cashback or contest entries for a review. The flyer headline refuses reward words.
- **Ask every customer.** Don't ask only happy customers. Every customer sees the same **Copy & post on Google** button, whatever rating they pick.
- **Don't hide Google from unhappy customers.** The private note sits below the Google button. It never replaces it.
- **The customer's own words.** Don't write reviews for customers, and don't post from staff or family accounts. Drafts use only what the customer picked, typed or said, and the customer can edit them.
- **Names only when the customer picks them.** A draft mentions a staff name, service or product only when the customer tapped or typed it. Don't use names to stuff keywords into reviews.
- **No keyword stuffing.** Google removes reviews that look alike. Drafts mention your name, service and area once at most, word each draft differently, and say **These are suggestions. Please edit them to reflect your genuine experience.**

## FAQ

**Can I send unhappy customers to a private form instead of Google?**
No. Google does not allow review gating. A 1 to 3 star customer can send you a private note as well, but the Google button is still there.

**Do the drafts say how many stars the customer gave?**
No. A draft never states a star count. The customer chooses their stars on Google.

**Does Ohanvi know which customer posted a review?**
No. Google does not tell Ohanvi who posted. The page asks for no name or number, except an optional name and phone in a private note.

**Which AI writes the drafts?**
Your organisation's default AI model. See or change it in **Google Reviews** → **Settings**. See [Choose the AI model for Google Reviews](settings.md).

**Who pays for the AI drafts?**
You do. Each tap of **Write my review** that returns drafts costs ₹5 plus GST from your wallet, on every plan. A tap that returns no drafts costs nothing. **Suggest with AI** questions and AI flyer backgrounds are free. The daily AI limit caps how many run in a day. When the wallet is empty or the limit is reached, customers see only **Write it myself**.

**Do I need Google Business Profile?**
No. Ohanvi finds your listing on Google Maps. Google Business Profile is needed only to read and reply to reviews inside Ohanvi, and for auto-reply.

**Is the customer's voice recording kept?**
No. A recording is turned into text and dropped. It is never stored.

**I have several branches. Do they share one QR code?**
No. Each branch has its own review page, Google listing, QR code and flyer. See [Manage branches](manage-branches.md).
