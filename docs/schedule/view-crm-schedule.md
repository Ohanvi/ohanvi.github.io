# See your scheduled CRM activities

Use the **CRM** view to see every planned CRM activity in one calendar or list. At the end you can check what your team has to do and when, and move or call off any activity.

## Before you start

- You can see **Schedule** in the left rail. By default only the workspace's Super Admin role has it. Ask your admin if it is missing.
- Activities with a date and time exist in CRM. See [Log activities and notes](../crm/log-activities-and-notes.md).

## Steps

### Open the CRM schedule

1. Open **Schedule** in the left rail, then select **CRM**. The page title reads **CRM schedule**.
2. The page opens in **Calendar** view for the current month. The channel chips are hidden, because the page is already limited to CRM.
3. Click a day to see its items. Click **List** to see them by time under day headings.
4. Click an item. Its details open on the right: **When**, **Recurrence**, **Audience / with**, **Owner**, **Channel** and **Type**.

### Read what each type means

The **Type** shows what kind of item it is.

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Task** | A to-do. Tasks with no other type show as **Task**. |
| **Call** | A planned phone call. |
| **Meeting** | A planned meeting. |
| **Email** | A planned email follow-up. |
| **Whatsapp** | A planned WhatsApp follow-up. |
| **Whatsapp call** | A planned WhatsApp call. |

The **Audience / with** line shows the name of the lead, contact or deal the activity is about.

### Read the status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | Planned and not started. |
| **Live** | In progress. |
| **Sent** | Completed. |
| **Cancelled** | Called off. The record is kept. |

### Change an item

1. Click **Reschedule** to pick a new date and time. See [Reschedule, cancel or delete a scheduled item](reschedule-cancel-delete.md).
2. Click **Edit** to open the CRM activities screen. See [Log activities and notes](../crm/log-activities-and-notes.md).
3. Click **Cancel** to call it off. The activity's status is set to cancelled.
4. Click **Delete** to remove it from CRM.

### Narrow the view

1. Click **Everyone's schedule** and choose **My schedule** to see only items you own.
2. Click **Any status** and choose **Scheduled**, **Draft**, **Sent** or **Cancelled**.

### Add something new

1. Click **New schedule**, then choose **New in CRM**. Ohanvi opens the CRM activities screen, where you create the item.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nothing scheduled here** | No CRM activity has a date and time, or a filter hides it. | Give the activity a due time in CRM. Choose **Any status**. |
| A finished activity shows **Sent** | **Sent** is how the Schedule shows a completed activity. | No action needed. |
| **Could not cancel.** | The activity is already completed or cancelled, or your role cannot change it in CRM. | Check the status. Ask your admin for CRM access. |
