# What guests see

A guest scans the QR code, verifies their WhatsApp number, takes a selfie, and sees the photos they are in. There is no app to install. The page opens at an address such as `app.ohanvi.com/e/aarav-diya-wedding`. Use this page to try it yourself, or to answer a guest's question.

## Before you start

- The event is published. See [Create an event](create-an-event.md#publish-the-event).
- The **Guest link** is on. See [Share the gallery](share-the-gallery.md).

## Steps

### Find your photos as a guest

Try this on your own phone before the event.

1. Scan the guest QR code with the phone camera. The event page opens in the phone's browser, at an address such as `app.ohanvi.com/e/aarav-diya-wedding`.
2. Tap **Find my photos**. Enter your name and WhatsApp number, and tick the box to agree to the selfie.
3. Tap **Verify with WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens with a ready-made message to the studio. Send it, then go back to the page. Or tap **Get a 6-digit code instead** and type the code you get on WhatsApp.
4. Take a selfie. Face the camera in good light.
5. Read your photos. They are sorted by folder, for example Haldi, Sangeet and Reception.

While the event is live, new photos of you appear on the page as they arrive.

### Download and share photos

Guests can download when the studio turned on downloads. See [Change event settings](event-settings.md#downloads).

1. On the page with your photos, tap **Select**.
2. Tap the photos you want, or tap **Select all**.
3. Tap **Download**. The page prepares a ZIP file, for example **Preparing your ZIP of 12 photos…**, then shows **Your ZIP is ready**.
4. To send photos to family instead, tap **Share**. The page makes a link to those photos and the message **Link copied** appears.

- A ZIP holds up to 200 photos. Each guest can make 3 ZIPs an hour. A ready ZIP can be downloaded for 24 hours.
- A share link holds up to 50 photos and works for 7 days. Whoever opens it sees only those photos, not the rest of the event.
- Hosts download and share the same way from the **Full gallery** and **Album selection** links.

### The WhatsApp message

When new photos of a guest arrive, Ohanvi sends a WhatsApp message such as **We found 12 photos of you**, with a **View my photos** button.

- Matches are grouped into one message per WhatsApp window, set in the event's settings.
- A guest gets at most 6 of these live messages a day.
- To stop the messages, the guest replies **STOP**.

See [Change event settings](event-settings.md#whatsapp-window).

### VIP guests

Guests who open the **VIP** link take a selfie and see the photos they are in. They don't give a WhatsApp number, and they get no WhatsApp messages. To see new photos, they open the same link again. The studio sends this link only to the people it is for, such as parents and elders.

## Guest FAQ

**Do I need to install an app?**
No. The page opens in your phone's browser.

**Why does it ask for my WhatsApp number?**
To make sure it is you, and to tell you when new photos of you arrive. Reply **STOP** at any time to stop the messages.

**What is my selfie used for?**
Only to find your photos at this event. It is never used to search another event.

**Can other guests see my photos?**
Not through the guest link. Each guest sees only the photos they are in. The hosts can see the full gallery.

**How long is my selfie kept?**
It is deleted after the period the studio set. You can delete your data at any time from the page.

**Some of my photos are missing.**
New photos keep arriving while the event is live. Photos taken from the side or far away may not match. Check again later, or ask the studio for the full gallery.

**It says no face was found in my selfie.**
Take the selfie again in good light, facing the camera. Remove sunglasses or a mask, and keep only your face in the frame.

**I'm not on WhatsApp.**
Ask the studio. They can send you a link that needs no number.

**Can I download my photos?**
If the studio turned on downloads, yes. Select the photos and tap **Download** to get a ZIP of up to 200 photos. The studio decides whether you get the full-size photo or a smaller copy.

**Can I send my photos to family?**
Yes. Select the photos and tap **Share**. The link shows up to 50 photos and works for 7 days.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The page opens but shows no photos | The event is still a draft, or no photo matches yet. | Publish the event. Ask the guest to check again later. |
| **Verify with WhatsApp** doesn't open WhatsApp | WhatsApp isn't installed on that phone. | Ask the guest to use the 6-digit code, or send them the **VIP** link. |
| No face found in the selfie | The light is poor, or the face is covered or turned away. | Take the selfie again in good light, facing the camera. |
| The guest stopped getting messages | They replied **STOP**, or reached 6 messages for the day. | Their photos still show on the page. Ask them to open it from the QR code. |
| **You can make 3 ZIPs an hour.** | The guest made 3 ZIPs in the last hour. | Wait an hour, or download photos one by one. |
| A shared link reads **This link has expired** | Share links work for 7 days. | Select the photos again and tap **Share** for a new link. |
| **The studio has turned off downloads for this gallery.** | **Downloads** is **Off** in the event's settings. | Pick **Originals** or **Web size**. See [Change event settings](event-settings.md#downloads). |
