# Set up auto-reply

Have Ohanvi draft a reply to each new Google review. You choose whether every reply waits for your approval, or whether replies to good reviews post on their own after a wait. You approve, edit or skip replies in the queue. Setup takes about 5 minutes.

## Before you start

- Google Business Profile is connected. See [Connect social accounts](../social/connect-social-accounts.md).
- You can see **Google Reviews** under **More** on the left rail.

!!! warning "Waiting for Google"
    Auto-reply works only after Google approves Business Profile access for Ohanvi and your profile is connected. Until then the screen shows **Waiting for Google to approve Business Profile access. Replies will start once it's connected.** You can save your settings now, but no reply is drafted or posted until then.

## Steps

### Choose how replies work

1. Click **More** on the left rail, then select **Google Reviews** → **Auto-reply**. The line at the top reads **Draft a reply to every new Google review. Post it yourself after a look, or let it post on its own.**
2. If you have several branches, pick the branch in **Branch:**. Each branch has its own settings. See [Switch between branches](manage-branches.md#switch-between-branches).
3. Under **Reply settings**, pick a **Reply mode**:
    - **Off** — No replies are drafted. This is the default.
    - **Ask me first** — A reply is drafted for each new review. Nothing posts until you approve it.
    - **Automatic** — Replies to reviews with enough stars post on their own after the wait. Other reviews wait for you.
4. For **Automatic**, pick **Wait before posting**: **Right away**, **15 minutes**, **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, **2 hours**, **4 hours**, **12 hours** or **24 hours**. The default is **1 hour**. The wait gives you time to change or skip a reply before it goes up.
5. For **Automatic**, pick **Minimum stars**: **5 stars only**, **4 stars and up**, **3 stars and up**, **2 stars and up** or **All ratings**. The default is **4 stars and up**. Lower ratings wait for you in the queue.
6. Click **Save settings**. The message **Auto-reply settings saved** appears.

To drop unsaved changes, click **Undo changes**.

!!! tip
    Start with **Ask me first**. Read a week of drafts, then switch to **Automatic** for 4 and 5 star reviews. Answer low ratings yourself.

### Work through the queue

The **Reply queue** card lists the replies drafted for new Google reviews. Each item shows the reviewer's name, their stars, the review, and **Your reply**.

1. Pick a tab: **Pending**, **Scheduled**, **Posted**, **Skipped** or **Failed**.
2. On a pending reply, do one of these:
    - Click **Approve**. The message **Reply approved** appears, and the reply posts right away.
    - Click **Edit**. Change the text in **Edit reply**, then click **Save reply**. The message **Reply updated** appears. Approve it when it is ready.
    - Click **Skip** to leave this review without a reply. The message **Reply skipped** appears.
3. Under **Scheduled**, read the replies waiting for their time. Each shows **Posts** followed by the date and time. You can still click **Edit** or **Skip**.
4. Click **Show more** at the bottom to see older items.

| Tab | What it lists |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending** | Replies that need your OK. |
| **Scheduled** | Approved replies, and **Automatic** replies waiting for their time. |
| **Posted** | Replies posted to Google, with **Posted** followed by the date and time. |
| **Skipped** | Replies you skipped, and replies Ohanvi dropped because the review already had a reply or was removed from Google. |
| **Failed** | Replies Google didn't accept, with the reason. Click **Approve** to try again. |

Ohanvi never replies to a review that already has a reply, and never posts a reply twice.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Waiting for Google to approve Business Profile access. Replies will start once it's connected.** | Google hasn't approved Business Profile access for Ohanvi yet, or your profile isn't connected. | Connect Google Business Profile when it is available. Your saved settings start working then. |
| The queue is empty and reads **Auto-reply is off** | **Reply mode** is **Off**. | Pick **Ask me first** or **Automatic**, then click **Save settings**. |
| **Wait before posting** and **Minimum stars** can't be changed | They apply only in **Automatic** mode. | Pick **Automatic** first. |
| A reply is under **Failed** with **Google Business Profile isn't connected.** | The connection to Google ended. | Reconnect Google Business Profile, then click **Approve** on the reply. |
| A reply is under **Failed** with **Couldn't post the reply to Google.** | Google didn't accept the reply. | Click **Edit** to check the text, then click **Approve**. |
| **Write the reply in 2 to 4000 characters.** | The edited reply is empty or too long. | Shorten or finish the reply, then click **Save reply**. |
| **This reply was already posted.** or **This reply was skipped.** | Someone else acted on it first, or it posted while you edited. | Refresh the queue. |
