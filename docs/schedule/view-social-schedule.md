# See your scheduled social posts

Use the **Social** view to see every scheduled post in one calendar or list. At the end you can check when each post goes live on each network and move or stop it.

## Before you start

- You can see **Schedule** in the left rail. By default only the workspace's Super Admin role has it. Ask your admin if it is missing.
- A post has a scheduled time. See [Schedule posts and manage the queue](../social/schedule-social-posts.md).

## Steps

### Open the Social schedule

1. Open **Schedule** in the left rail, then select **Social**. The page title reads **Social schedule**.
2. The page opens in **Calendar** view for the current month. The channel chips are hidden, because the page is already limited to Social.
3. Click a day to see its items. Click **List** to see them by time under day headings.
4. Click an item. Its details open on the right: **When**, **Recurrence**, **Audience / with**, **Owner**, **Channel** and **Type**.

### Read what each type means

The **Type** shows what kind of item it is.

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Post** | A social post scheduled to publish on a connected account. |

The **Audience / with** line shows the social network the post goes to, for example Instagram.

### Read the status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | The post is saved as a draft with a time. |
| **Scheduled** | The post is **Scheduled** or **Queued** and waits for its time. |
| **Live** | The post is publishing now. |
| **Sent** | The post is published, or published on some networks only. |
| **Failed** | Publishing did not go through. |
| **Cancelled** | Called off. It is not published. |

### Change an item

1. Click **Reschedule** to pick a new date and time. See [Reschedule, cancel or delete a scheduled item](reschedule-cancel-delete.md).
2. Click **Edit** to open the Social **Publish** screen. See [Schedule posts and manage the queue](../social/schedule-social-posts.md).
3. Click **Cancel** to call it off. The scheduled post is cancelled and is not published.
4. Click **Delete** to remove it from Social.

### Narrow the view

1. Click **Everyone's schedule** and choose **My schedule** to see only items you own.
2. Click **Any status** and choose **Scheduled**, **Draft**, **Sent** or **Cancelled**.

### Add something new

1. Click **New schedule**, then choose **New in Social**. Ohanvi opens the Social **Publish** screen, where you create the item.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nothing scheduled here** | No post has a scheduled time, or a filter hides it. | Schedule a post in Social **Publish**. |
| A post shows **Sent** but is missing on one network | The post was published on some networks only. | Open the post in Social **Publish** to see which network failed. |
| **Could not cancel.** | The post is already published or your role cannot change it. | Check the status. Ask your admin for Social access. |
