# Invite teammates with a link

Share an invite link by WhatsApp, email or QR code. Your teammate signs up with it and joins your workspace straight away, with the role you picked. They choose their own username and password, so you never handle it.

## Before you start

- You are a **Super Admin** in your workspace. See [Manage team members, roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions.md).
- Your plan has a free seat. When every seat is used, **Invite teammate** is hidden.

## Steps

### Create and share the link

1. Click your initials in the bottom-left corner, then click **Settings**.
2. Click your name at the top of the Settings list.
3. Under **Team & access**, click **App User**. The **App User Management** list opens.
4. Click the add-person icon (**Invite teammate**) at the top of the list. The **Invite teammate** window opens.
5. Under **JOINS AS**, click **Executive** or **Manager**. The link updates for that role.
6. Under **SHARE VIA**, click how to send it:
    - **WhatsApp** opens WhatsApp with the invite message filled in.
    - **Email** opens your email app with the subject and message filled in.
    - **Copy** copies the link. The message **Invite link copied to clipboard.** appears.
    - **More** opens your device's share menu.
7. To invite someone next to you, let them scan the QR code (**Scan to join as <role>**).

The link expires in 7 days. Each role keeps its own link, so reopening the window shows the same link until it expires.

An invite link can only add a **Manager** or an **Executive**. To add another **Super Admin**, use **Add** instead. See [Manage team members, roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions.md).

### What your teammate does

1. They open the link. The page shows **You're joining <workspace> team**.
2. They fill in their name, username, password, email and mobile number, then click **Join team**. They can also click **Continue with Google**.
3. They land in your workspace. The message **Account created — welcome to Ohanvi!** appears. With Google, it reads **Welcome to <workspace> team!**

Their password must meet the same rules as yours. See [Update your profile and password](update-your-profile.md).

### Invite someone to 1 module only

Some module team screens, such as the WhatsApp team screen, have their own invite action. That window is titled **Invite to <Module>**. Whoever joins with that link sees only that module.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Invite teammate** is missing | You are not a Super Admin, or every seat on your plan is used. | Ask the workspace owner, or add seats in the [Billing section](../billing/index.md). |
| Your teammate sees **This invite link is invalid or has expired.** | The link is older than 7 days, or was mistyped. | Open **Invite teammate** again and send the new link. |
| Your teammate sees **This team is full.** | Every seat on your plan is used. | Add seats or remove someone, then send the link again. |
| **This account has reached its … -user limit for your plan. Add seats to invite more teammates.** | The seat limit was reached while they signed up. | Add seats, then ask them to try again. |
| **Failed to generate invite link.** | The link could not be created. | Click **Retry** in the window. If it fails again, contact support. |
| **Could not open WhatsApp. The link has been copied instead.** | WhatsApp or your email app is not available on this device. | Paste the copied link into any chat or email. |
