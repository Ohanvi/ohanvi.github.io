# Appointments

Appointments lets customers book your services over WhatsApp and gives your team one place to confirm, move and close those bookings.

## Where to find it

Open **Appts** in the left rail (hover title **Appointments**). The panel lists **Appointments**, **Services**, **Classes** and **Providers**.

Booking rules and reminders are not in the panel: click your initials bottom-left, then **Settings** → **Appointments** → **Booking Rules**.

## Before you start

- Your role has Appointments access.
- Your WhatsApp number is connected. Bookings, working hours, reminders and customer messages all use it.
- You have at least 1 service and 1 provider. No times can be offered until both exist.
