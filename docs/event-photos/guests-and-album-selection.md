# Work with guests and album selection

The **Guests** tab lists everyone who verified their WhatsApp number for the event, with how many photos they were found in. The **Selection** tab shows the photos the couple hearted for the album, ready to export for Lightroom or as a CSV.

## Before you start

- The event is shared, and guests have scanned the guest QR code. See [Share the gallery](share-the-gallery.md).
- For album picks, the couple has the **Album selection** link.

## Steps

### See who verified

1. Click **Photos** on the left rail, select **Events**, and open the event.
2. Go to the **Guests** tab. The count above the table reads, for example, **128 guests**.
3. To find one guest, type in **Search guests by name or number**.
4. Read the guest's row.

| Column | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Guest** | The guest's name. |
| **WhatsApp** | The number they verified. |
| **Joined** | When they registered for the event. |
| **Verified via** | How they verified: the ready-made WhatsApp message, or the 6-digit code. |
| **Photos found** | How many photos they were found in. |
| **Last message** | The status of the last WhatsApp message they were sent. |

A guest marked **Pending** gave a number but has not verified it yet. A guest marked **Stopped** asked for no more WhatsApp messages about this event.

### Find guests in your CRM

Each guest who verifies becomes a CRM contact, tagged with the event. Use the tag to find them later, or to send a broadcast to everyone from the event.

1. Open your contacts. See [Manage contacts](../crm/manage-contacts.md).
2. Filter by the event's tag.

!!! note "Guests who replied STOP"
    A guest who replies STOP gets no more WhatsApp messages about the event. Respect it in your own broadcasts too. See [Manage opt-in and opt-out words](../whatsapp/manage-opt-in-and-opt-out-words.md).

### Read the couple's album picks

The couple opens the **Album selection** link and hearts the photos they want in the album. Their picks appear on the **Selection** tab.

1. Open the event and go to the **Selection** tab.
2. Read the photos the couple hearted.
3. Export the picks for Lightroom, or as a CSV file.

Send the couple the **Album selection** link from the **Share** tab. See [Share the gallery](share-the-gallery.md#send-a-link-on-whatsapp).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The tab reads **No guests yet — share the QR at the venue** | No guest has scanned and verified yet. | Put the guest QR code up at the venue. See [Share the gallery](share-the-gallery.md). |
| A guest shows **Pending** | They gave a number but didn't finish verifying. | Ask them to tap **Verify with WhatsApp** and send the ready-made message. |
| A guest shows **Photos found** as 0 | No photo matches their selfie yet, or the matching photos are held. | Check the photos are uploaded and released. If the selfie was dark or blurred, ask them to take a new one. See [Fix Event Photos problems](troubleshooting.md). |
| A guest shows **Stopped** | They replied STOP. | No fix needed. They get no more WhatsApp messages about this event. |
| The **Selection** tab is empty | The couple hasn't hearted any photos yet. | Send them the **Album selection** link. |
