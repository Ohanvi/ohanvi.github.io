# Create and manage forms

Forms collect answers from customers, in chat, on the web or through a shared link. At the end, you have a form you can find, publish and keep tidy.

## Before you start

- You can see **Forms** in the left rail. If not, ask your admin.

## Steps

### Open your forms

1. Open **Forms** in the left rail, then select **Forms**.
2. Read the line under the title: **Whatever your customers fill in — chat, web, or a shared link.**
3. Click **All**, **Draft**, **Active** or **Archived**. Each shows a count.
4. Use the **Any source** picker to narrow by where the form came from. Use the search box to find a name.

### Start a new form

1. Click **New form**. **Start a new form** opens.
2. Pick a template chip that fits your business, or click **Start blank**.
3. The form builder opens. See [Build a form](forms-build-a-form.md).

### Read a form row

1. A badge **From <source>** shows where it came from.
2. **Live in chat** means a chatbot is using it.
3. **Public link** means anyone with the link can open it.
4. Click the row to open it in the builder.

### Use the row menu

1. Open the **⋮** menu on a row.
2. Choose **Responses** to read answers. See [Read form responses](forms-read-responses.md).
3. Choose **Share link and QR**. See [Preview and share a form](forms-share-and-preview.md).
4. Choose **Publish to WhatsApp**, or **Republish to WhatsApp** after changes.
5. Choose **Duplicate**. It says **Duplicated as a draft**.
6. Choose **Pause**. It says **Paused — the link no longer opens**.
7. Choose **Archive** to hide it without deleting.
8. Choose **Delete**. Confirm **Delete <name>?** Submissions already received are kept.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No forms yet** | You have not made a form. | Click **New form**. |
| **No forms match** | A filter or search hides them. | Click **Clear filters**. |
| The public link does not open. | The form is paused or still a draft. | Set the status to **Active**. |
