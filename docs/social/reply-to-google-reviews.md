# Reply to Google reviews

Reviews on your Google listing sync into Ohanvi. You can reply in public, change or delete a reply, and mark a review for follow-up with a private note. It takes about 5 minutes.

## Before you start

- Google Business is connected. See [Connect social accounts](connect-social-accounts.md).
- You can see **Social** in the left rail. Reviews sync from Google in the background, so a new review may take a little while to show.

## Steps

### Open the reviews

1. Open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Home**.
2. Under **NEEDS ATTENTION**, find **Google reviews** and click **Reply →**. The reviews page opens.
3. The top of the page shows how many reviews there are, for example **12 review(s)**.

### Filter the reviews

1. Open the rating list. It starts on **All ratings**.
2. Pick **1 star**, **2 stars**, **3 stars**, **4 stars** or **5 stars**.
3. Switch on **Unreplied only** to see reviews you have not answered.
4. Click the refresh icon to sync the latest.
5. Use the arrows (**Previous page** and **Next page**) to move through the list. The line **Page N of M** shows where you are.

If the filters hide everything, the page says **No reviews match these filters**. Click **Clear filters** to see all reviews again.

If nothing has synced yet, it says **No reviews synced yet**. Click **Go to dashboard**, which opens **Insights**, and look for **Sync from Google**. [VERIFY: where Sync from Google sits on Insights]

### Reply to a review

1. On a review, click **Reply**. The **Reply to …** window opens. It shows the review text.
2. Type your answer in **Public reply**.
3. To start from saved text, pick one in **Insert template**.
4. To get a suggested answer, click **Suggest reply**. Edit it before you send.
5. Click **Send Reply**. The message **Reply posted to Google** appears.
6. Read the line **Posted publicly on Google.** Everyone can see your reply.

If the box is empty, it says **Reply text is required**.

### Change a reply

1. Under your reply, click **Edit**.
2. Change the text.
3. Click **Update Reply**.

### Delete a reply

1. Under your reply, click **Delete**. The **Delete reply?** window opens.
2. Click **Delete**. The message **Reply deleted** appears.

!!! warning "This cannot be undone"
    Deleting removes your public reply from Google.

### Mark a review for follow-up

1. On a review, click the flag icon (**Flag for follow-up**).
2. Click the person icon (**Assign / note**). The **Assign / internal note** window opens.
3. In **Assigned to**, type the teammate's name.
4. In **Internal note**, type a note. Nobody outside your team can see it, and it does not show on Google.
5. Click **Save**.
6. The review now shows **Assigned:** and **Note:** lines.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No reviews synced yet** | Google Business was just connected, or nothing has synced. | Click **Go to dashboard**, run **Sync from Google**, then click the refresh icon. |
| **No reviews match these filters** | The rating or **Unreplied only** hides them. | Click **Clear filters**. |
| **Reply text is required** | The reply box is empty. | Type a reply, or click **Suggest reply**. |
| The reviews page is empty after reconnecting | The Google connection has expired. | Reconnect it. See [Connect social accounts](connect-social-accounts.md). |
