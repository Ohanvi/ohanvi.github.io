# Billing and credits

Ohanvi runs on prepaid credits: you recharge, and paid actions such as WhatsApp messages, email and AI take from the balance. These guides cover the balance, usage, invoices and your plan.

## Where to find it

- The **Credits** box at the top-right shows your balance. Click it to open the **Credits** page.
- Click your initials bottom-left for **Current usage**.
- Click your initials, then **Settings** → **Billing & credits** for **Plans & Pricing**, **Billing**, **Invoice Details**, **Current Usage** and **Credits**.

## Before you start

- You are the workspace's **Super Admin**. Plans, billing and invoice details are not shown to other roles.
- If your workspace is billed through Shopify, **Plans & Pricing**, **Billing** and **Invoice Details** are hidden. **Credits** and **Current Usage** still show.
