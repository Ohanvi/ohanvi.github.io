# Preview and share a form

Preview shows exactly what a customer sees. Sharing sends it out. At the end, people can open and submit your form.

## Before you start

- Your form has at least 1 question. See [Build a form](forms-build-a-form.md).

## Steps

### Preview

1. Open the form and click **Preview**.
2. Fill it in. The page says **Preview only — submitting here records nothing**.
3. Click **Preview again** to restart. Required questions are marked **\* Indicates required question**.

### Share by link or QR

1. Click **Share link and QR**. **Share this form** opens.
2. Copy the link, or click **Download QR**.
3. Anyone with this link can open and submit the form while it is **Active**.
4. Click **Copy link** in the top bar for a quick copy.

### Embed or use in chat

1. Open the **More** menu.
2. Click **Embed HTML**. It says **Embed snippet copied**. Paste it into your page.
3. Click **Use in your flow** to ask this form inside a chatbot flow.
4. Click **Publish to WhatsApp**, or **Republish to WhatsApp** after you edit.

### Pause or retire a form

1. In **More**, click **Pause** to stop the link opening.
2. Click **Archive** to hide it, or **Delete** to remove it.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The link says the form is closed. | It is paused or a draft. | Set **Status** to **Active**. |
| Preview answers are not in Responses. | Preview never records. | Submit from the real link to test. |
