Build a lead follow-up workflow

Trigger automatic WhatsApp, email, or task actions when a lead enters a segment.

Intermediate15 minutesWorkflows

Steps

  1. Open Workflows and select Create workflow.
  2. Choose a trigger such as new lead, form submitted, or tag added.
  3. Add conditions for source, location, score, or campaign.
  4. Add actions such as WhatsApp message, email, sales task, or delay.
  5. Test with a sample contact and activate the workflow.

Example flow

  • Trigger: Lead created from Facebook Ads.
  • Action: Send WhatsApp welcome template.
  • Delay: Wait 1 day.
  • Condition: If no reply, assign sales follow-up task.
