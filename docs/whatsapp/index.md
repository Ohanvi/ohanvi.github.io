# WhatsApp

WhatsApp is where you connect your business number, answer customers in a shared inbox, and send approved template messages and broadcasts. It also runs your product catalog, Click-to-WhatsApp ads and an AI agent that replies for you.

## Where to find it

Open **WhatsApp** in the left rail. The panel is one list, split by dividers into 3 bands:

| Band | Panel items |
| --- | --- |
| Talk and send | **Dashboard**, **Inbox**, **Campaign**, **Ads Manager** |
| Build | **Template**, **Flows** |
| Manage | **Contact**, **AI Agent**, **Catalog**, **Manage** |

The setup screens are not in the panel. Click your initials bottom-left, then **Settings** → **WhatsApp**. The **Setup** group holds **Configuration**, **Business Profile**, **Webhooks**, **Developer API**, **BUSY** and **Migrate to Ohanvi**.

**Manage** opens its own list, including **Opt-in Management**, **Live Chat Settings**, **Canned Messages**, **Agents**, **Tags**, **Scheduler** and **Delivery Logs**.

A user with the WhatsApp Executive role sees only **Inbox**.

## Before you start

- You have a WhatsApp Business number and admin access to your Meta Business Manager. Connect it from the web app — the Facebook window does not open on mobile.
- Your role has WhatsApp access. Each panel item has its own permission.
- You have credits. Template messages and AI replies use credits. See [Credits and billing](../credits-and-billing.md).
