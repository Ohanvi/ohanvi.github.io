# Use post templates

Save post text you reuse as a template, then open it in the composer with one click. You can browse the ready-made library, write your own templates with fill-in blanks, or let AI draft one from a topic or a trend.

## Before you start

- You can see **Social** in the left rail, with **Templates** in its panel.
- To publish from a template, at least one network is connected. See [Connect social accounts](connect-social-accounts.md).

## Steps

### Find a template

1. Open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Templates**.
2. On the left, pick a view:
    - **Discover** — the ready-made library, with **Recommended for you** and **More templates**.
    - **My Templates** — templates you created.
    - **AI Generated** — templates AI drafted for you.
    - **Saved** — templates you marked with the heart icon.
    - **Recently Used** — templates you opened in the composer.
3. Under **CATEGORIES**, pick **All Templates** or a category, such as **Marketing**, **Announcement**, **Event**, **Review** or **General**.
4. Under **Filter by platform**, pick a network to see only templates made for it, or **All platforms**.

### Use a template

1. On a template card, click **Use Template**. The **Create Post** window opens with the template text and image.
2. Replace each `[bracketed]` blank with your own words.
3. Pick your networks and publish or schedule the post. See [Create and publish a social post](create-social-post.md).

You can also pick a template from inside the composer: click **Templates** at the top, then choose one in **Start from a template**.

### Create a template

1. Click **Create Template**. The **New Template** window opens.
2. In **Template Name**, type a name.
3. In **Category**, pick a category.
4. Optionally, under **Thumbnail**, click **Add image**. The image must be under 500 KB.
5. In **Content**, type the post text. Wrap any word in `[brackets]` to make it a fill-in blank, for example `It helps [Target Audience] save time.`
6. Under **Platforms**, pick where the template can be used.
7. Check the **Live Preview**, then click **Save Template**. The message **Template saved.** appears.

To let AI fill this form, click **Draft with AI** at the top of the window and describe the post.

### Generate a template with AI

1. Click **Generate with AI**. The **Generate a template** window opens.
2. Pick a trending topic under **Trending in**, or type a topic under **Or write your own topic**, for example `Independence Day sale`.
3. Click **Generate**. Ohanvi writes a caption and finds an image. This can take up to a minute.
4. In **Review your template**, check **Template name**, **Category**, **Content** and **Platforms**. Change anything you want.
5. Click **Save template**. The message **Saved "…" to your templates** appears. Click **Use now** to open it in the composer.

### Edit or delete your template

1. On a template you created, click the **More** (⋯) menu.
2. Click **Edit** to change it, or **Delete** to remove it. For **Delete**, confirm in the **Delete template?** window.

You can only edit or delete templates you created.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Image too large — pick one under 500 KB.** | The thumbnail is over the size limit. | Compress the image and add it again. |
| **Name is required** or **Description is required** | **Template Name** or **Content** is empty. | Fill in both fields. |
| **No templates match here** | Your category, platform or search filter hides every template. | Pick **All Templates** and **All platforms**. |
| **Trending topics aren't available right now — type your own below.** | The trends feed returned nothing. | Type your own topic. |
| **Could not generate a caption right now. Please try again.** | The AI request failed. | Wait a moment and click **Generate** again. |
