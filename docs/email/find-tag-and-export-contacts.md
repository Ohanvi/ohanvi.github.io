# Find, tag and export contacts

The **Emails** tab in Audience lists every email contact. Use it to find people, tag them, change their status and export your audience. At the end, your contacts are labelled so you can target them.

## Before you start

- You can see **Audience** in the **Email** panel. If not, ask your admin.
- You have contacts. See [Add email contacts, lists and segments](manage-email-contacts.md).

## Steps

### Find contacts

1. Open **Email** in the left rail, then select **Audience**.
2. Open the **Emails** tab.
3. Click **All**, **Subscribed** or **Unsubscribed** to switch between **All Contacts**, **Subscribed Contacts** and **Unsubscribed Contacts**.
4. Narrow the list with the filters: **Status**, **Filter by Owner**, **Filter by Tags** and **Active in last N days** (Last 7, 30 or 90 days, or **Any time**).
5. Click **Clear all** to remove every filter.

### Tag contacts

1. Open a contact's tags to **Manage tags**. Under **Add or remove tags**, type in **Search or create a tag**.
2. Click **Create** for a new tag. Pick from **Popular tags** to reuse one.
3. Click **Add tags** or **Remove tags**.
4. To tag many people, paste their addresses into **Bulk Tag by Email**, or use **Upload File**. Name the tag when asked: **What should we name this tag?**
5. Or click **Manage audience**, then **Bulk select & actions**. Use **Select All**, then **Tag Contacts** or **Delete Selected Contacts**.

### Change a contact's status

1. Click the **More actions** menu on a row.
2. Choose **Reactivate** to mark them **Subscribed** again, or **Unsubscribe** to stop marketing email.
3. Choose **Copy to List** to add them to a list.

### Export your audience

1. Click **Export**. The **Export Contacts** dialog opens.
2. Set **Export Type**, **File Name**, **Format** and **Fields to Include**.
3. Optional: set **Filters** and **Date Range**. Then export.

### Other tools under Manage audience

1. Click **Manage audience** to open **Import history**, **Export audience**, **Find duplicates**, **Copy to list** and **Bulk select & actions**.

### Handle a data request

1. Click **GDPR Data Export** on the row to export all data held for that person.
2. Click **GDPR Erase** to erase their data. This cannot be undone.
3. Click **Delete Contact** to remove the contact without a data request.

!!! warning
    **GDPR Erase** is permanent. Export first if you may need a record.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A contact is missing. | A filter is hiding them. | Click **Clear all**. |
| A bulk tag skipped some rows. | The address is not in **Contacts**. | Add the contact first, then tag again. |
| **Reactivate** does nothing for a bounce. | Hard bounces and complaints are permanent. | See [Block addresses with the Suppression List](use-the-suppression-list.md). |
