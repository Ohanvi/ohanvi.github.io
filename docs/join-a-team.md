# Join a team

Use the invite link your team owner sent you to create an account inside their workspace. You do not choose a role or an organisation: the link already carries both. Takes about 2 minutes.

## Before you start

- You have an invite link from the team owner or an admin. See [Invite teammates](settings/invite-teammates.md).
- The link is still valid. Links can expire.
- The team has a free seat. A full team cannot take new members.

## Steps

### Open the invite

1. Open the invite link in your browser.
2. Read the heading. It says **You're joining** your team's name **team**. A chip below shows your role, for example **as Sales**.

### Create your account

1. In **Enter your name**, type your full name.
2. In **Enter your username**, type the username you will sign in with.
3. In **Enter your password**, type a password. In **Confirm your password**, type it again.
4. In **Enter your email**, type your work email.
5. In **Enter your mobile number**, type your number. The country code starts as India (IN).
6. Click **Join team**. The message **Account created — welcome to Ohanvi!** appears and **Home** opens.

### Join with Google instead

1. Click **Continue with Google** and choose your Google account.
2. The message **Welcome to** your team's name **team!** appears and **Home** opens.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **This invite link is invalid or has expired.** | The link is old or was already used. | Ask the owner to send a new invite. You can still click **Create an account** to start your own workspace. |
| **This team is full.** | The team's plan has no free seat. | Ask the owner to upgrade the plan, as the page says. |
| You already have an account. | You signed up earlier. | Click **I already have an account** and sign in. See [Sign in to Ohanvi](sign-in.md). |
