# Insights

Insights shows reactions, comments, followers and posts for all your channels. You can open one channel to see its posts and metrics. Instagram has its own view with views, reach, stories, best times to post and audience.

**Where to find it:** open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Insights**.

## Guides

| Guide | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| [Read social insights](../view-social-insights.md) | You want the summary figures, the top posts and a post-by-post table. |
| [Explore Insights charts and one channel](../explore-insights-charts.md) | You want the follower and post charts, the metrics tabs and the filters. |
| [Read Instagram insights](../read-instagram-insights.md) | You want your Instagram account's views, reach, top posts and reels, stories, best times to post and audience. |

## Before you start

- At least one network is connected and has published posts.
