# Use the Social sidebar

The **Social** panel on the left has more than menu rows. It has a **New** button and a list of your connected channels. You can start a post, jump to one channel's Publish or Insights, and connect more networks. It takes about 2 minutes.

## Before you start

- You can see **Social** in the left rail. If not, ask your admin to give your role Social access. See the [Settings overview](../settings/index.md).

## Steps

### Start something new

1. Open **Social** in the left rail.
2. Click **New** at the top of the panel. A menu opens.
3. Click **Post** to open the **Create Post** window. See [Create and publish a social post](create-social-post.md).
4. Click **Connect a new channel** to open **Connect** → **Social Connections**.
5. **Idea** is greyed out. It is not built yet.

### Read the Channels list

1. Scroll the panel to **CHANNELS**. Each connected account has a row with its icon and name.
2. Click a row to open it.
3. Under it you see **Publish** and **Insights**.
4. Click **Publish** to see that channel's posts only. See [Use Publish filters, overview and post details](use-publish-filters-and-extras.md).
5. Click **Insights** to see that channel's results only. See [Explore Insights charts and one channel](explore-insights-charts.md).
6. The channel you opened is highlighted. If two accounts use the same network, both show as active together.

### Connect more channels

1. Use the three icons at the top right of **CHANNELS**: search, settings and the plus sign.
2. Click the settings icon (**Settings**) to open **Connect** → **Social Connections**.
3. Click the plus sign (**Connect a new channel**) to open the same screen.
4. Under **Connect more channels**, click a network logo to open its connect wizard.
5. Click the plus tile to see all networks.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CHANNELS** is empty | No account is connected. | Click the plus sign and connect one. |
| **Idea** is greyed out | It is not available yet. | Use **Post** instead. |
| A channel row does not open | The panel is collapsed. | Click the group name to open it. |
