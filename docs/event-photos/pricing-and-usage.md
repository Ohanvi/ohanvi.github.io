# Events pricing and usage

Event Photos is charged by use, on top of your plan fee. You pay for the storage your photos take up and for face recognition, once per photo. Both come out of your prepaid credits, at your plan's rates.

!!! note "Charges show as a preview for now"
    Until Events billing is switched on, **Plan & usage** shows **Preview: these charges aren't being taken yet.** Your usage is measured and priced, but nothing comes off your credits, and face recognition never pauses. Usage from the preview is not charged later.

## What you pay for

- **Your plan fee.** You pay it as you do today. See [Compare plans and pricing](../billing/plans-and-pricing.md).
- **Events usage.** Storage and face recognition, charged from your credits at your plan's rates, on top of the plan fee.

For example, on **Starter** you pay the ₹599 plan fee. If your Events usage that month comes to ₹200, you pay ₹799 in all.

The app calls your credit balance the **wallet** on the Events pages. It is the same balance. See [Credits and billing in Ohanvi](../credits-and-billing.md).

## How storage is charged

- Storage is counted on each photo's original size. Web-size copies and thumbnails are not counted.
- Every photo in your events counts: photos you upload, photos from Beam and photos guests add.
- Storage is billed every day: the GB you store × your rate ÷ the days in that month.
- Ohanvi measures each day just after midnight, at 00:30 India time, for the day before. It counts the photos you had at the end of that day.
- A photo is **active** for its first 90 days. The upload day is day 1. From day 91 it is **archive**, at the lower rate.
- Archive is only a price. The photo doesn't move. It stays in the event, and you and your guests can still view and download it instantly.
- Photos in an expired event, an unpublished event or an event in the Bin still count. They stop counting when you delete them, or when the event is deleted for good.

### Minimum storage period

Each upload is billed for at least your plan's minimum storage period, counting the upload day. If you delete a photo sooner, its storage is still billed until that period ends.

For example, on **Starter** the period is 60 days. A photo uploaded on 1 November and deleted on 10 November is billed until 30 December.

The same applies when you delete an event, or when an event leaves the Bin for good.

## How face recognition is charged

- Face recognition is charged once per photo, when Ohanvi looks for the faces in it. It is charged even when the photo has **No faces**.
- A photo is never charged twice for face recognition.
- A photo that shows **Failed** is not charged.
- Guests' selfies are not charged.
- Face recognition is charged only for photos checked after Events pricing started. Photos you already had still count toward storage.

## When your credits run out

Only face recognition pauses. Everything else carries on:

- You can still upload photos, and Beam still sends them.
- Each new photo is stored and shows in the full gallery, with the status **Waiting for credit**.
- Guests can't find a waiting photo with their selfie until its faces are found.
- **Plan & usage** shows **Face recognition is paused: your wallet is empty.**, with the number of photos waiting.

Recharge, and within about 15 minutes the waiting photos are face-matched on their own. Guests then find them as usual. See [Add funds to your account](../add-funds.md).

Waiting photos in an expired event, or in an event in the Bin, keep waiting. Photos in an event you restore from the Bin carry on after you recharge.

## Free trial and Shopify billing

- **Free trial.** Events usage is free during your free trial. Your statement still lists each use, at ₹0, marked free during trial.
- **Billed through Shopify.** A workspace billed through Shopify is not charged for Events at the moment. See [Manage a plan billed through Shopify](../billing/shopify-billed-accounts.md).

## A month on the Starter plan

A studio on **Starter** in November (30 days):

- It stores 30 GB of photos less than 90 days old all month.
- It stores 10 GB of older photos, which are in archive.
- On 15 November it uploads a wedding: 2,500 photos, 20 GB. The wedding is stored for 16 days in November.

| Charge | How it is worked out | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active storage, 30 GB all month | 30 GB × ₹4 | ₹120.00 |
| Active storage, the 20 GB wedding | 20 GB × ₹4 × 16 ÷ 30 days | ₹42.67 |
| Archive storage, 10 GB all month | 10 GB × ₹3 | ₹30.00 |
| Face recognition, the wedding's photos | 2,500 photos × 6 paise | ₹150.00 |
| **Events usage, from your credits** | | **₹342.67** |
| **Plan fee** | | **₹599.00** |
| **Total for November** | | **₹941.67** |

From the wedding photos' day 91, they are billed at the archive rate. Its 20 GB then costs ₹3 per GB-month instead of ₹4: ₹60 a month.

## FAQ

**Does my plan fee cover any Events usage?**
No. All Events usage is charged from your credits, on top of the plan fee.

**Do my photos get slower to open after 90 days?**
No. Archive is only a lower price. Photos don't move, and open and download as before.

**How do I lower my storage cost?**
Delete photos and events you no longer need. They stop counting once the minimum storage period has passed. An event in the Bin still counts until it is deleted for good.

**Does a bigger plan cost less for Events?**
Growth, Scale and Pro have lower rates and a 30-day minimum storage period. Compare them on **Plan & usage** under **Compare plans**.

**Are guest selfies charged?**
No. Only the photos in your events are charged.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Photos show **Waiting for credit** | Your credits are empty, so face recognition is paused. | Recharge. Within about 15 minutes the photos are face-matched on their own. See [Add funds to your account](../add-funds.md). |
| Photos still show **Waiting for credit** 15 minutes after a recharge | The event is expired or in the Bin, or the balance can't pay for one photo. | Restore the event from the Bin, or recharge more. |
| **Plan & usage** shows charges, but your credits didn't go down | Charges show as a preview until Events billing is switched on. | No fix needed. Nothing is taken during the preview. |
| A photo you deleted is still billed for storage | It is inside your plan's minimum storage period. | No fix needed. Billing stops when the period ends. |
| An event in the Bin is still billed for storage | Photos count until the event is deleted for good. | On the **Bin** tab of **Events**, open the event's menu and click **Delete forever**. The minimum storage period still applies. See [Create an event](create-an-event.md#move-an-event-to-the-bin). |
