# Add products

Add the things you sell to the CRM. At the end, a deal can carry product lines, and its amount is the sum of the lines. Adding one product takes about a minute.

## Before you start

- You are an admin. CRM settings are admin only.
- You know each product's code, name and price.

## Steps

### Open Products

1. Click your initials in the bottom-left corner, then click **Settings**.
2. Select **CRM**, then click **Products**.

You can also press **Ctrl K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), type **Products**, and select it.

### Add a product

1. Click **Add**. The **Add Products** form opens.
2. Type a **Code**. It must be unique.
3. Type a **Name**.
4. Type the **Unit Price**.
5. Check **Currency**. It defaults to **INR**.
6. Keep **Active** ticked so the product can be picked on a deal.
7. Click **Submit**. The message **Product created** appears.

Both **Code** and **Name** are required.

### Use a product on a deal

1. Open a deal. See [Create and manage deals](manage-deals.md).
2. Add a product line and pick the product.
3. The deal's amount becomes the sum of its lines.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Product code '…' already exists.** | Another product uses that code. | Give the product a unique **Code**. |
| The product is missing on a deal | **Active** was not ticked. | Edit the product and tick **Active**. |
| **Products** is missing from CRM settings | Your role is not an admin. | Ask your admin. |
