# Deactivate your account

Pause your whole Ohanvi account. Everyone is signed out and nobody can sign in, but nothing is deleted. You confirm with your password and a one-time code sent to your email. Takes about 2 minutes. You can bring the account back by signing in again.

## Before you start

- You are the **account owner**, the person who created the account. Other admins do not see **Deactivate account**.
- You know your password.
- You can open the email address on your account. Ohanvi sends a one-time code there.
- You understand that this acts on the **whole** account, every user in it, not just you.

## What deactivating does

| What happens | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Everyone is signed out straight away. | Nobody in the account can sign in. |
| Nothing is deleted. | Your contacts, chats, settings and invoices stay. |
| Your data is kept for at least the number of days shown on the screen. | The screen text says **at least 90 days** by default. |
| You can come back. | Sign in again yourself within that time. The account is reactivated automatically. |
| After that time Ohanvi may delete it. | Deleting is done by the Ohanvi team, not from this screen. |

!!! warning "Deactivating signs out your whole team"
    Tell your teammates before you start. Their work in progress is not saved when they are signed out.

## Steps

### Open the screen

1. Click your initials in the bottom-left corner, then click **Settings**.
2. In the left list, click your name. The settings for your account open.
3. Find **Deactivate account** under **More**, and click it. The page title reads **Deactivate account**. Only the account owner sees this row.
4. Read the line under the title. It names your account and says it asks for your password and a code sent to your email.

### Deactivate the account

1. Click **Deactivate…** on the **Deactivate account** card. The **Deactivate your account** window opens.
2. Read the warning at the top of the window.
3. Under **Step 1 of 2 — confirm it is you**, type your password in **Your password**. Click the eye icon to show or hide it.
4. Click **Continue**. Ohanvi emails a one-time code to your registered address.
5. Under **Step 2 of 2 — enter the code we emailed you**, read the banner. It shows the masked email address and how long the code is valid.
6. Type the code in **Verification code**.
7. Click **Deactivate account**. Everyone is signed out. The sign-in screen shows Ohanvi's message about the account.

If the code does not arrive, click **Resend code**.

### Bring the account back

1. Open the Ohanvi sign-in page. See [Sign in to Ohanvi](../sign-in.md).
2. Sign in as the owner, within the days shown on the **Deactivate account** screen.
3. The account is reactivated automatically. Tell your teammates they can sign in again.

### Delete the account for good

You cannot delete the account from Settings. The screen says: contact Ohanvi support at info@ohanvi.com. The Ohanvi team does the deletion, and it cannot be undone.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Deactivate account** is not in the list. | You are not the account owner. | Ask the owner to deactivate, or contact Ohanvi support. |
| The page says **Only the account owner — the person who created this account — can deactivate it.** | Your user is not the founding owner. | Sign in as the owner. |
| **Enter your password.** | The password box is empty. | Type your password and click **Continue**. |
| **Could not send the code.** | The code email did not go out. | Check your connection and click **Continue** again. |
| **Enter the code from your email.** | The code box is empty. | Type the code from the email. |
| **The request failed. Nothing was changed.** | The code is wrong or expired. | Click **Resend code**, then enter the new code. |
