# Set sales quotas

Give each salesperson a monthly sales target, then check how close they are. At the end, the forecast shows each owner's attainment against their quota. Adding one quota takes about a minute.

## Before you start

- You can see **CRM** in the left rail with **Forecast** in its panel.
- You know the owner's username, the month and the target amount.

## Steps

### Open Sales Quotas

1. Open **CRM** in the left rail, then select **Forecast**.
2. Click **Manage quotas**. The **Sales Quotas** list opens.

You can also press **Ctrl K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), type **Sales Quotas**, and select it.

### Add a quota

1. Click **Add**. The **Add Sales Quotas** form opens.
2. Fill **Owner Username**. It is the person the target belongs to.
3. Optional: fill **Owner Display Name**.
4. Pick the **Year**.
5. Pick the **Month**.
6. Type the **Target Amount**.
7. Check **Currency**. It defaults to **INR**.
8. Click **Submit**. The message **Quota created** appears.

Each owner can have 1 quota per month.

### Check progress against a quota

1. In the **Sales Quotas** list, find the row.
2. Click **View** in the **Achievement** column.
3. The **Forecast vs Quota** window opens for that month.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A quota for this owner and period already exists.** | That owner already has a quota for that month. | Edit the existing quota instead. |
| **Target amount must be positive.** | **Target Amount** is 0 or less. | Enter an amount above 0. |
| **Month must be between 1 and 12.** | The month is out of range. | Pick a month from 1 to 12. |
| **Forecast vs Quota** shows the whole team's figures | The report cannot filter by owner yet. | Compare the owner's quota with **Won by owner** on the **Dashboard**. |
