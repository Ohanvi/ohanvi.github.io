# Review posts before they publish

Send a draft to a teammate for review before it goes live. The reviewer approves it or asks for changes. Once it is approved, you can schedule or publish it. Review is optional: a draft you never submit can be published directly.

## Before you start

- You can see **Social** in the left rail, with **Publish** in its panel.
- The post is saved as a draft. See [Create and publish a social post](create-social-post.md).
- To approve posts, your role has approval access for social posts. If **Approve** returns an error, ask your admin. See the [Settings overview](../settings/index.md).

## Steps

### Submit a draft for review

1. Open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Publish**.
2. Click the **Drafts** tab.
3. On the post, click **Submit**. The message **Post submitted for approval.** appears.

The post moves to the **Approvals** tab with the status **Pending approval**. Admins in your workspace get an **Approval requested** notification.

### Approve a post

1. Open **Social** → **Publish**, then click the **Approvals** tab.
2. Read the post. The card shows **Submitted by** and who sent it.
3. Click **Approve**. The message **Post approved — it can now be scheduled.** appears.

The author gets a **Post approved** notification and can now schedule or publish the post.

### Request changes

1. In the **Approvals** tab, click **Request Changes** on the post. The **Request changes?** window opens.
2. In **What needs changing?**, type your feedback. This is optional.
3. Click **Request Changes**. The message **Changes requested — the post went back to the creator.** appears.

### Resubmit after changes

1. In the **Drafts** tab, find the post marked **Changes requested**. Your reviewer's note shows under it.
2. Click the **Edit** icon, make the changes, and click **Save Changes**.
3. Click **Resubmit**.

!!! note
    If you edit a post after it was approved, it goes back to **Pending approval**. It needs a fresh review before it can be published.

## Approval statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Pending approval** | Waiting for a reviewer. It cannot be scheduled or published yet. |
| **Approved** | A reviewer signed off. You can schedule or publish it. |
| **Changes requested** | A reviewer sent it back. Edit it and click **Resubmit**. |

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **You cannot approve your own post** | The author cannot approve their own post. | Ask another teammate to approve it. |
| **Post is awaiting approval and cannot be published until approved** | The post is still pending review. | Wait for approval, or ask a reviewer. |
| **Changes were requested; edit and resubmit for approval before publishing** | A reviewer asked for changes. | Edit the post, then click **Resubmit**. |
| **Only draft posts can be submitted for approval; current status is …** | The post is already scheduled or sent. | Only drafts can go to review. |
