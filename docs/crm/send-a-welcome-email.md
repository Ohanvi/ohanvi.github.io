# Send a welcome email to a contact

Send a single email to one contact without leaving Ohanvi. At the end, the contact has the email in their inbox with the brochures you chose, and the email shows in their activity history. It takes about a minute.

## Before you start

- You can see **Contacts** in the left rail with **All contacts** in its panel.
- The contact has an **Email**. See [Manage contacts](manage-contacts.md).
- To change the default message, see [Set up the welcome email](set-up-the-welcome-email.md).

## Two ways to send

| Mode | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **Compose + PDF** | You want to edit the subject and message and attach your own files. |
| **Ohanvi brochure** | You want a ready-made email that shows the brochure inside the message. The subject and body are made for you. |

## Steps

### Open the email window

1. Open **Contacts** in the left rail, then select **All contacts**.
2. Click the contact, then click **More actions** (the **…** button).
3. Click **Send Welcome Email**. The email window opens.
4. Read the **To** box. It shows the contact's email and you cannot change it here.
5. To enlarge the window, click the maximise button in its header.

### Send with Compose + PDF

1. Click **Compose + PDF** at the top of the window.
2. Optional: under **Email Template**, pick a saved email template. The box reads **Select a template (optional)**. The subject and body fill in.
3. Check the **Subject**. The window starts with your saved welcome subject.
4. Check the **Body**. Edit it for this contact if you want. Your change affects only this email.
5. Under **Attach brochures**, tick each file you want to attach. The size shows under each name.
6. Click **Send**. The message **Welcome email sent to …** appears.

If the **Subject** is empty, the window says **Add a subject before sending**. If the **Body** is empty, it says **Add a message before sending**.

### Send the Ohanvi brochure email

1. Click **Ohanvi brochure** at the top of the window.
2. Read the box that explains the email. It is a ready-made message with the brochure shown inside it. You do not write the subject or body.
3. Optional: under **Add images to the email**, click **Click to add images** or drop files on it. Use PNG, JPG, GIF or WEBP files, up to 5 MB each. The counter shows how many you added, for example **2/6**.
4. Under **Attach brochures**, tick the brochures to include.
5. Click **Send**.

### Add a brochure from this window

1. Next to **Attach brochures**, click **Manage**. The **Manage Brochures** window opens.
2. Click **Upload brochure** and pick a file. See [Set up the welcome email](set-up-the-welcome-email.md) for file rules.
3. Close the window. The new file is in the list. Tick it.

If you have no files yet, the list says **No brochures yet. Tap Manage to upload the booklet.**

### Check that it was logged

1. Select the contact and open **Notes & Activities** from **More actions**. Or open the **Email** tab in the middle pane.
2. Find the email you sent. Each welcome email is saved as an email activity on the contact.
3. Go back to the **All contacts** list. The email count for that contact has gone up by 1.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Could not send the email. Please retry.** | The email did not send. | Click **Send** again. Check the contact's **Email**. |
| The **To** box is empty | The contact has no email. | Add an **Email** in the **Identity** block, then click **Update contact**. |
| An attached file is missing in the list | It was not uploaded, or it was deleted. | Click **Manage** and upload it again. |
| **Email Opt-out** is ticked on the contact | The contact asked not to get emails. | Respect the choice. See [Track contact consent](consent-and-compliance.md). |
