# Read private notes

**Private notes** is your follow-up inbox for customers who gave 1 to 3 stars and sent you a note from your review page. Read each note, contact the customer if they asked you to, and set a status so your team knows where it stands. It takes about 2 minutes a note.

A private note is sent as well as a Google review, never instead of one. Every customer saw the same Google button.

## Before you start

- Your role can open **Private notes**. It needs its own permission. If you cannot see it, ask your admin. See [Team, roles and permissions](../settings/roles-and-permissions.md).
- **Private note for 1–3 stars** is on in the **Review page** settings. It is on by default.

## Steps

### Read the notes

1. Click **More** on the left rail, then select **Google Reviews** → **Private notes**. You can also click **Read notes** on the unread alert on **Review page**, or open the alert in your notifications.
2. Read the line at the top: **Notes from customers who gave 1–3 stars. They saw the same Google button as everyone else.**
3. Click **Unread** to see only the notes you have not read, or **All** for every note. When there are unread notes, the tab shows the count, for example **Unread (3)**. The newest note is first.
4. To see notes at one stage of follow-up, pick a status: **New**, **Contacted**, **Resolved** or **Unreachable**. A status tab shows its count only when it has notes, for example **New (2)**.
5. Read a note. It shows:
    - The star rating, the date and time, and the page language the customer used.
    - The customer's message.
    - Under **Answers**, the answers they tapped, each after its question, for example **How was the wait? → Too long**.
    - The **Staff**, **Services** and **Products** they picked, if any.
    - Their name and phone, if they left them, with **Customer agreed to be contacted** when they ticked the box. If they left neither, the note says **No name or phone left**.
    - **Unread** on a note you have not read.
6. Click **Show more** at the bottom to load older notes.

The answers show the question as the customer saw it, so they still make sense after you edit your questions.

### Contact the customer

A customer's name and phone are kept only when they ticked **Yes, {your business} may contact me about this** on the review page. The page tells them their number is used only to contact them about this note, including on WhatsApp.

1. On a note with a phone the customer agreed to share, click **WhatsApp**. WhatsApp opens a chat with the customer's number. You type and send the message yourself.
2. Or call them on the phone shown.
3. Set the status to **Contacted**. See [Set the follow-up status](#set-the-follow-up-status).

The **WhatsApp** button shows only on notes with a phone the customer agreed to share. Notes from before this release have no **WhatsApp** button, even with a phone, because the customer's agreement wasn't recorded then. Call them on the phone shown instead.

!!! warning "Don't ask for a review change"
    Fix the problem the customer raised. Don't ask them to change or remove their Google review, and don't offer anything for one. Use their number only for this note, as the page promised them.

### Set the follow-up status

Every new note starts as **New**. Move it as you follow up:

| Status | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| **New** | Nobody has acted on the note yet. |
| **Contacted** | You called or messaged the customer. |
| **Resolved** | The problem is sorted out, or the note needs nothing more. |
| **Unreachable** | You tried, and could not reach the customer. |

1. On the note, click its status and pick the new one.
2. Below the comment box, the note shows the status, who changed it and the date.

### Add your comment

Keep a short record of what you did, for example **Called back, offered a replacement**. Only your team sees it. The customer never does.

1. On the note, type in the box **Your comment (only your team sees it)**, up to 1000 characters.
2. Click **Save**. It shows only after you change the text. The message **Comment saved** appears.

To remove a comment, clear the box and save.

### Mark a note read

1. On the note, click **Mark as read**. The count on the **Unread** pill goes down by 1.
2. To bring it back to the unread list, click **Mark as unread**.

### Delete contact details

Delete the customer's name and phone once you no longer need them, for example after the problem is resolved.

1. On the note, click **Delete contact details**.
2. Confirm. The message **Contact details deleted** appears.

The name and phone are gone for good. You can't get them back. The note then reads **Contact details deleted on** followed by the date. The message, rating, answers, status and your comment stay.

!!! note "Contact details are deleted after 90 days"
    Ohanvi deletes the name and phone on every note 90 days after the customer sent it, even if you don't. The message, rating and answers stay, so your notes and stats keep their history.

### Get an alert for each new note

Super Admins and System Admins get a notification and an email for each new note, such as **New private note: 2★ at Sunrise Dental – Indiranagar** (business – branch). The alert shows the branch, the stars and the start of the message. It never contains the customer's name or phone. Open **Private notes** to see those.

Each person's **Social** notification settings decide the notification. It comes while either **In-app** or **Push** is on, so turn both off to stop it for yourself. The email follows the workspace's email setting for **Social**, not yours. A Super Admin can turn it off for the whole workspace. See [Choose how notifications reach you](../settings/notification-preferences.md).

To get alerts somewhere else, build a flow that starts on the event **Private note from the review page**. For example, send yourself a WhatsApp message with the note. See [Build an automation flow](../flows/build-automation-flow.md).

### Turn private notes on or off

1. Open **Review page** and go to **Settings**.
2. Click **More settings**, then turn **Private note for 1–3 stars** on or off.
3. Click **Save settings**.

When it is off, customers don't see **Also tell the owner privately**, and **Private notes** reads **Private notes are off. Turn on "Private note for 1–3 stars" in the Review page settings.** Notes already received stay in the list.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No private notes yet** | No 1 to 3 star customer has sent a note. | No fix needed. When one does, it shows up here. |
| **Private notes are off. Turn on "Private note for 1–3 stars" in the Review page settings.** | Private notes are turned off. | Turn on **Private note for 1–3 stars** and click **Save settings**. |
| **Private notes** is missing under **Google Reviews** | Your role has no access to private notes. | Ask your admin to give your role access. |
| A note says **No name or phone left** | The customer didn't tick **Yes, … may contact me about this**, or left no name or phone. Without the tick, the page doesn't send them. | No fix needed. The note is anonymous. |
| A note's name and phone are gone | Someone clicked **Delete contact details**, or the note is more than 90 days old. | No fix needed. They can't be brought back. |
| A note has no **WhatsApp** button | The customer left no phone, didn't agree to be contacted, or their contact details were deleted. A note from before this release has no **WhatsApp** button, because the customer's agreement wasn't recorded then. | No fix needed. If a phone shows, call them. |
| **Keep the comment under 1000 characters.** | Your comment is too long. | Shorten it, then save again. |
| No notification for a new note | Alerts go to Super Admins and System Admins only, or both **In-app** and **Push** are off for **Social**. | Check your role, and turn on **In-app** or **Push** for **Social** in [your notification settings](../settings/notification-preferences.md). |
| No email for a new note | Alerts go to Super Admins and System Admins only, or email for **Social** is off for the workspace. | Check your role, or ask a Super Admin to turn on email for **Social**. |
