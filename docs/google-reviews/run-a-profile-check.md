# Run a profile check

Get a score out of 100 for your Google listing, check it for missing details, read suggestions to fix them, and see where you rank among similar businesses within about 2 km. The check reads your listing from Google Maps. It takes about 2 minutes.

## Before you start

- Your review page is linked to a confirmed Google listing. A pasted review link is not enough. See [Confirm your business on Google](get-more-google-reviews.md#confirm-your-business-on-google).
- You can see **Google Reviews** under **More** on the left rail.

You do not need to connect Google Business Profile. The check uses the public listing on Google Maps. If you connect it, the check also reads your own description, your replies to reviews and how many people found you. See [Checks from your Business Profile](#checks-from-your-business-profile).

## Steps

### Run the check

1. Click **More** on the left rail, then select **Google Reviews** → **Profile check**. The line at the top reads **See what your Google listing is missing and how it compares with similar businesses nearby.**
2. If you have several branches, pick the branch in **Branch:**. See [Switch between branches](manage-branches.md#switch-between-branches).
3. On the **Your Google listing** card, click **Run check**. The button reads **Checking…** while it runs.
4. Wait for the message **Profile check updated**. The card then reads **Last checked** followed by the date and time.

Before the first run, the card reads **Not checked yet. You can run the check 3 times a day.**

### Read your score

The **Profile score** card shows your score, such as **72 / 100**, with a badge: **Strong** (80 or more), **Room to improve** (50 to 79) or **Needs work** (under 50).

Each check is worth points. The more important a check, the more it is worth:

| Points | Checks |
| --- | --- |
| 12 | **Has photos**, **Google rating** |
| 10 | **Opening hours**, **Google's summary of your business** or **Description**, **Business category**, **Number of reviews**, **Replies to recent reviews** |
| 8 | **Website**, **Phone number** |
| 5 | **Unanswered reviews** |

Your score is the share of points you earned among the checks that ran. A check that didn't run, or wasn't compared, neither adds nor takes away. So a listing without Google Business Profile connected is scored on its listing checks only, and can still reach 100.

Results from before the score existed show no score card. Run the check again to get one.

### Read the checklist

The **Checklist** card groups the checks under **Your listing** and, with Google Business Profile connected, **Reviews**. Each line shows **Looks good** or **Fix this**, with a short reason. The card's note reads **Everything we check looks good.**, or how many lines you have to fix.

| Check | Looks good when | Fix this reads, for example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Opening hours** | **Your opening hours are listed.** | **No opening hours on your listing. Add them so people know when you're open.** |
| **Website** | **A website is linked.** | **No website on your listing.** |
| **Phone number** | **A phone number is listed.** | **No phone number on your listing.** |
| **Google's summary of your business** | **Google shows a short summary of your business. Google writes it, not you.** | **Google shows no summary of your business yet.** |
| **Has photos (Google shows up to 10 here)** | **10 or more photos on your listing.** | **6 photos found. Add more photos.** |
| **Business category** | **Listed as** followed by your category, such as **Listed as Beauty salon.** | **No main category on your listing.** |
| **Google rating** | **Rated 4.6, at or above 4.3.** | **Rated 4.1. Aim for 4.3 or more.**, or **No Google rating yet.** |
| **Number of reviews** | You have at least the median number of reviews of similar businesses nearby, meaning the middle one when they are lined up by count. | **12 reviews; similar businesses nearby have 40 (median).** |

**Google's summary of your business** is the short blurb Google writes about you on Maps. You can't edit it. Google writes it when your listing is complete and has regular reviews. With Google Business Profile connected, this line is replaced by **Description**, your own description. See [Checks from your Business Profile](#checks-from-your-business-profile).

**Has photos** counts at most 10, because Google shows only up to 10 photos to the check. **10 or more photos on your listing.** means at least 10. Google's own count may be higher.

**Number of reviews** shows **Looks good** and isn't counted in your score when there is nothing to compare with. The line then ends with one of:

- **No similar businesses nearby to compare with.**
- **Too few similar businesses nearby to compare with.** Fewer than 3 nearby businesses have reviews.
- **Pick your industry on the Review page to compare with similar businesses.** See [Compare with businesses nearby](#compare-with-businesses-nearby).

Fix missing details in Google Business Profile, or in Ohanvi if Google Business Profile is connected. See [Manage your Google Business Profile](../social/manage-google-business-profile.md).

#### Category suggestion

When at least 3 of the nearby businesses list the same main category and yours is different, a note under **Your listing** reads, for example, **3 of 5 similar businesses nearby list Software company; you're listed as Service.** The first suggestion then asks you to check your primary category in Google Business Profile.

Change the category only if theirs describes your business better. A more exact category helps Google show you to the right customers.

### Checks from your Business Profile

When Google Business Profile is connected for this branch's listing, the check adds the lines below. Each is marked **From your Business Profile**.

| Check | Group | What it reads |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Description** | **Your listing** | Your own description. **Fix this** when there is none, or when it is under 250 characters, such as **Your description is 120 characters. Aim for at least 250.** |
| **Replies to recent reviews** | **Reviews** | How many reviews from the last 90 days have your reply, such as **You replied to 11 of 12 reviews from the last 90 days (91%). Typical reply time: 5 hours.** **Fix this** under 80%. |
| **Unanswered reviews** | **Reviews** | Reviews with no reply yet. **Looks good** reads **Every review has a reply.** |

The **Activity on Google** card shows 4 tiles: **Searches**, **Calls**, **Directions** and **Website clicks**. Each shows the count for the last 28 days and the change against the 28 days before, such as **↑ 13%** or **↓ 20%**. **No change** means the same count. When the 28 days before had none, the tile shows **↑ from 0**. The card's note reads **Last 28 days vs previous 28**, followed by the dates.

The reply lines appear once your reviews have synced, and the **Activity on Google** card once Google has sent your profile's numbers. Until then, they're left out and your score doesn't count them.

When your Business Profile isn't connected, the **Checklist** card ends with **Connect Google Business Profile on the Review page to add reply-rate and activity checks.** The line shows only when Ohanvi can connect Google Business Profile. See [Manage your Google Business Profile](../social/manage-google-business-profile.md).

### Read the suggestions

The **Suggestions** card lists 3 to 6 numbered ideas, most important first, under the line **Ideas based on the checklist and the businesses nearby.**

Ohanvi's AI writes them only from your checklist and the nearby businesses' numbers. They don't add facts about your business or promise a result. If the AI is not available, the check still runs and the card is left out.

### Compare with businesses nearby

The **Businesses nearby** card lists up to 5 similar businesses within about 2 km, from Google Maps.

Ohanvi finds them by your Google category. When your category is too general, such as **Service**, it searches by the **Industry** you picked on the Review page instead, such as **IT / Software**. Public places, such as police stations, government offices, schools, places of worship, ATMs, parking and bus or train stations, are left out.

If your Google category is too general and **Industry** is **Other business**, no search runs. The card reads **Google found no similar businesses nearby.** and **Number of reviews** ends **Pick your industry on the Review page to compare with similar businesses.** See [Choose the questions customers answer](get-more-google-reviews.md#choose-the-questions-customers-answer).

1. Read the badges at the top, such as **#2 of 6 by rating** and **#3 of 6 by number of reviews**. They show your rank among these businesses and yours. #1 is the best. Businesses with the same rating or review count share a rank, and a business with no rating ranks below any rating.
2. Read the table: **Business**, **Rating** and **Reviews**. The best-rated business is first.
3. Find your row. It is highlighted and marked **You**.

If Google has no similar business near you, the card reads **Google found no similar businesses nearby.**

### Limits

- You can run the check 3 times a day for each branch.
- A result is kept for 30 days, because Google allows Maps data to be kept only that long. After that the screen reads **No check yet**. Run the check again.
- The screen is marked **Data from Google Maps**.

!!! tip
    Run the check again after you fix your listing. Google can take a few days to show a change on Maps.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Confirm your Google listing first** | The branch has no confirmed Google listing. | Click **Go to Review page** and confirm the listing. |
| **Couldn't run the check** with **You can run the check 3 times a day.** | You ran it 3 times today for this branch. | Run it again tomorrow. |
| **A check is already running. Try again in a moment.** | A teammate, or another tab, started a check for this branch. | Wait a moment, then click **Refresh**. |
| **Couldn't read your listing from Google. Try again.** | Google didn't answer. | Click **Run check** again in a minute. |
| **No check yet** after you ran it before | The last result is more than 30 days old. | Click **Run check**. |
| **Google found no similar businesses nearby.** | Google has no business of the same kind within about 2 km. | No fix needed. The checklist still applies. |
| **Pick your industry on the Review page to compare with similar businesses.** | Your Google category is too general, and **Industry** is **Other business**. | On the Review page, pick your industry under **Industry**, then click **Run check**. |
| A government office, temple or bus stop showed up nearby in an older check | Older checks didn't leave out public places. | Click **Run check**. |
| No score card | The result is from before scores, or no check could be scored. | Click **Run check**. |
| **Google's summary of your business** shows **Fix this** | Google hasn't written a summary for your listing. You can't add one yourself. | Keep your listing complete and ask for reviews. To have your own description checked instead, connect Google Business Profile. |
| **Has photos** shows 10 but you have more | Google shows the check up to 10 photos. | No fix needed. 10 means 10 or more. |
| No **Replies to recent reviews** or **Activity on Google** with Google Business Profile connected | Your reviews or numbers haven't synced yet, the connected profile is another branch's listing, or no review came in during the last 90 days. | Wait a day, then click **Run check**. |
| A line still shows **Fix this** after you fixed it | Google Maps hasn't updated the listing yet. | Run the check again in a few days. |
