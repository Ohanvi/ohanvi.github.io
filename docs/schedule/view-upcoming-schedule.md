# See what is coming up

Use the **Upcoming** view to plan ahead. It lists items from today onward that are still waiting or running, grouped by day. At the end you will know what goes out in the next days and can move or stop anything.

## Before you start

- You can see **Schedule** in the left rail. By default only the workspace's Super Admin role has it. Ask your admin if it is missing.
- Something is scheduled for today or a later day in at least one module.

## Steps

### Open Upcoming

1. Open **Schedule** in the left rail, then select **Upcoming**. The page title reads **Upcoming** and the page opens in **List** view.
2. Read the day headings. They read **Today**, **Tomorrow**, then a date such as **Fri, 3 Oct**.
3. Click a row to open its details on the right.

### Know what Upcoming includes

Upcoming shows an item only when both of these are true:

- Its date is today or later.
- Its status is **Scheduled** or **Live**.

!!! note
    Items that are **Draft**, **Sent**, **Paused**, **Cancelled** or **Failed** are not in **Upcoming**. A **Paused** item still shows under **All schedules** when the status is **Scheduled**.

### Narrow the list

1. Click a channel chip to show one channel. Click more chips to add channels back.
2. Click **Everyone's schedule** and choose **My schedule** to see only your items.
3. Click **Any status** to narrow further. The status you pick works together with the **Upcoming** rule.

### Move or stop an upcoming item

1. Click the item.
2. Click **Reschedule** to pick a new date and time, or **Cancel** to call it off. See [Reschedule, cancel or delete a scheduled item](reschedule-cancel-delete.md).

### See a longer range

1. Click **Calendar** to see the month with the same filter.
2. Use the arrows next to the month name to move to the next month. Click **Today** to jump back.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nothing scheduled here** | Nothing is waiting from today onward, or a filter hides it. | Click another channel chip, choose **Everyone's schedule**, or set the status to **Any status**. |
| A paused broadcast is missing | **Upcoming** shows only **Scheduled** and **Live** items. | Open **All schedules** and set the status to **Scheduled**. |
| A draft is missing | Drafts have their own view. | Open **Drafts**. See [Find and finish drafts](find-and-finish-drafts.md). |
| Something that went out earlier today is missing | It is **Sent** now. | Open **Today** to see it. |
