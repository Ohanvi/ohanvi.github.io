    Recommended article structure¶

    1. Goal: Explain what the user will complete.
    2. Before you start: Mention permissions, connected accounts, and required data.
    3. Steps: Use short numbered actions with screenshots after important clicks.
    4. Video: Embed YouTube, Vimeo, Loom, or portal demo links where helpful.
    5. Troubleshooting: List common errors and fixes at the bottom.
