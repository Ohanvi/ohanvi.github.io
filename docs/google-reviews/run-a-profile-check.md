# Run a profile check

Check your Google listing for missing details, read suggestions to fix them, and see where you rank among similar businesses within about 2 km. The check reads your listing from Google Maps. It takes about 2 minutes.

## Before you start

- Your review page is linked to a confirmed Google listing. A pasted review link is not enough. See [Confirm your business on Google](get-more-google-reviews.md#confirm-your-business-on-google).
- You can see **Google Reviews** under **More** on the left rail.

You do not need to connect Google Business Profile. The check uses the public listing on Google Maps.

## Steps

### Run the check

1. Click **More** on the left rail, then select **Google Reviews** → **Profile check**. The line at the top reads **See what your Google listing is missing and how it compares with similar businesses nearby.**
2. If you have several branches, pick the branch in **Branch:**. See [Switch between branches](manage-branches.md#switch-between-branches).
3. On the **Your Google listing** card, click **Run check**. The button reads **Checking…** while it runs.
4. Wait for the message **Profile check updated**. The card then reads **Last checked** followed by the date and time.

Before the first run, the card reads **Not checked yet. You can run the check 3 times a day.**

### Read the checklist

The **Checklist** card lists 8 checks on your listing. Each line shows **Looks good** or **Fix this**, with a short reason. The card's note reads **Everything we check looks good.**, or how many lines you have to fix.

| Check | Looks good when | Fix this reads, for example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Opening hours** | **Your opening hours are listed.** | **No opening hours on your listing. Add them so people know when you're open.** |
| **Website** | **A website is linked.** | **No website on your listing.** |
| **Phone number** | **A phone number is listed.** | **No phone number on your listing.** |
| **Description** | **Google shows a description of your business.** | **Google shows no description for your business.** |
| **Photos** | **At least 10 photos on your listing.** | **6 photos on your listing. Aim for at least 10.** |
| **Business category** | **Listed as** followed by your category, such as **Listed as Beauty salon.** | **No main category on your listing.** |
| **Google rating** | **Rated 4.6, at or above 4.3.** | **Rated 4.1. Aim for 4.3 or more.**, or **No Google rating yet.** |
| **Number of reviews** | You have at least the median number of reviews of similar businesses nearby. | **12 reviews; similar businesses nearby have 40 (median).** |

With no similar businesses nearby, **Number of reviews** shows **Looks good** and the line ends **No similar businesses nearby to compare with.**

Fix missing details in Google Business Profile, or in Ohanvi if Google Business Profile is connected. See [Manage your Google Business Profile](../social/manage-google-business-profile.md).

### Read the suggestions

The **Suggestions** card lists 3 to 6 numbered ideas, most important first, under the line **Ideas based on the checklist and the businesses nearby.**

Ohanvi's AI writes them only from your checklist and the nearby businesses' numbers. They don't add facts about your business or promise a result. If the AI is not available, the check still runs and the card is left out.

### Compare with businesses nearby

The **Businesses nearby** card lists up to 5 popular businesses of the same category as yours, within about 2 km, from Google Maps.

1. Read the badges at the top, such as **#2 of 6 by rating** and **#3 of 6 by number of reviews**. They show your rank among these businesses and yours. #1 is the best. Businesses with the same rating or review count share a rank, and a business with no rating ranks below any rating.
2. Read the table: **Business**, **Rating** and **Reviews**. The best-rated business is first.
3. Find your row. It is highlighted and marked **You**.

If Google has no similar business near you, the card reads **Google found no similar businesses nearby.**

### Limits

- You can run the check 3 times a day for each branch.
- A result is kept for 30 days, because Google allows Maps data to be kept only that long. After that the screen reads **No check yet**. Run the check again.
- The screen is marked **Data from Google Maps**.

!!! tip
    Run the check again after you fix your listing. Google can take a few days to show a change on Maps.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Confirm your Google listing first** | The branch has no confirmed Google listing. | Click **Go to Review page** and confirm the listing. |
| **Couldn't run the check** with **You can run the check 3 times a day.** | You ran it 3 times today for this branch. | Run it again tomorrow. |
| **A check is already running. Try again in a moment.** | A teammate, or another tab, started a check for this branch. | Wait a moment, then click **Refresh**. |
| **Couldn't read your listing from Google. Try again.** | Google didn't answer. | Click **Run check** again in a minute. |
| **No check yet** after you ran it before | The last result is more than 30 days old. | Click **Run check**. |
| **Google found no similar businesses nearby.** | Google has no business of the same kind within about 2 km. | No fix needed. The checklist still applies. |
| A line still shows **Fix this** after you fixed it | Google Maps hasn't updated the listing yet. | Run the check again in a few days. |
