# Choose your own or Ohanvi's managed services

For WhatsApp, AI and email, choose whether Ohanvi uses your own provider account or its managed service. At the end, each channel shows which option is active and how its usage is billed.

## Before you start

- You can see **Connectors** under your name in **Settings**. If not, ask your workspace owner.
- For the WhatsApp choice, your WhatsApp number is already connected. If not, click **Set up WhatsApp** on the **WhatsApp (Meta)** card.
- To use your own AI or email provider, you have an API key or login from that provider.

## How billing differs

Managed usage is billed from your credits. Your own account is billed by the provider. See [Credits and billing in Ohanvi](../credits-and-billing.md).

| Channel | Ohanvi managed | Your own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp (Meta)** | **Pay through Ohanvi** — your credits pay Meta's rate plus Ohanvi's commission. | **Pay Meta directly** — you pay Meta; your credits pay only Ohanvi's usage commission. |
| **AI** | **Ohanvi’s AI key** — Ohanvi's shared AI key. | **Your key · connected** — your own Gemini, OpenAI or Anthropic key. |
| **Email** | **Ohanvi’s shared sender** | **Your provider · connected** — your own email provider. |

## Steps

### Open Connectors

1. Click your initials in the bottom-left corner, then click **Settings**.
2. Click your name at the top of the Settings list.
3. Under **Billing & credits**, click **Connectors**. The **Connectors** page opens with 3 cards.

### Choose how WhatsApp is billed

1. On the **WhatsApp (Meta)** card, click **Pay through Ohanvi** or **Pay Meta directly**.
2. The message **WhatsApp billing updated.** appears.

If the card shows **Not set up**, click **Set up WhatsApp** and connect your number first.

### Use your own AI key

1. On the **AI** card, click **Connect your own AI key**. The **AI Connectors** page opens.
2. Pick your provider and paste your key. Follow the steps on that page [VERIFY: exact field labels on AI Connectors].
3. Go back to **Connectors** and click **Refresh**. The card shows **Your key · connected**.

To change it later, click **Manage AI provider**.

### Use your own email provider

1. On the **Email** card, click **Connect your own provider**. The email provider page opens.
2. Add your provider's details and save. See the [Email section](../email/index.md) for provider setup.
3. Go back to **Connectors** and click **Refresh**. The card shows **Your provider · connected**.

To change it later, click **Manage email providers**.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Could not update WhatsApp billing.** | The change did not save. | Try again. If it keeps failing, contact support. |
| **WhatsApp (Meta)** shows **Not set up** | No WhatsApp number is connected. | Click **Set up WhatsApp** and add your WhatsApp configuration. |
| The AI or Email card still shows Ohanvi's service | The page has not reloaded since you added your key. | Click **Refresh**. |
| **Connectors** is missing under your name | Your role cannot see it. | Ask your workspace owner. |
