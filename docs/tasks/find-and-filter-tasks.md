# Find, filter and view tasks

Find the work that matters to you right now, from any screen, and look at the whole project in the view that suits the question. At the end, you can check what is due today, narrow the board to one person, type or priority, and read the project's progress at a glance.

## Before you start

- Your workspace has a task project with tasks in it. See [Set up a task project](set-up-task-project.md).

## Steps

### See your own tasks in the Tasks panel

The **Tasks** panel works on any screen, so you can check your work without leaving what you are doing.

1. Click **Tasks** in the right-hand rail. The **Tasks** panel opens.
2. Pick a tab at the top of the list:

    | Tab | Shows |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Today** | Tasks due today or assigned to you today. Tasks due today come first. |
    | **Assigned to me** | Every task where you are the assignee. |
    | **Created by me** | Every task you reported. |

3. Read the tags on each row: **Due today** (amber) or **Overdue** (red).
4. Click a row to open the task, or change its status from the dropdown under the summary.

To close the panel, click **Tasks** in the right-hand rail again, or press Esc.

### Filter the Tasks panel by status

1. In the **Tasks** panel, click the filter icon at the right end of the tabs (tooltip **Filter by status**).
2. In **Filter by status**, tick the statuses you want to see.
3. Click **Done**. The filter icon is highlighted while a filter is on.

To show all statuses again, open the filter and click **Clear**.

### Search the board

1. In the **Tasks** panel, click **Board**. The board opens on the same project.
2. Type in **Search board**. The board shows only tasks whose key or summary contains your text.
3. Click the cross (tooltip **Clear search**) to show all tasks again.

Type a task key, such as OHANVI-12, to jump straight to that task.

### Filter the board by type, priority or person

- **Type:** click **Type** and tick **Epic**, **Story**, **Task**, **Bug** or **Sub-task**.
- **Priority:** click **Priority** and tick one or more of **Highest**, **High**, **Medium**, **Low** and **Lowest**.
- **Person:** click a member's avatar at the right end of the toolbar to show only their tasks. Click **Unassigned** for tasks with no owner. Click more avatars to add people, or click **More people** for the full list.

Filters combine. For example, **Bug** plus **Highest** plus one person shows that person's most urgent bugs. Click a selected option again to remove it.

When a filter shows **Filtered by epic**, click it to remove the epic filter.

### Group the board into swimlanes

1. Click **Group** (tooltip **Group by (swimlanes)**).
2. Pick **Assignee** or **Epic**. The board splits into one row per person or per epic. Tasks without one go in **Unassigned** or **No epic**.
3. To go back to a single board, pick **No swimlanes**.

### Switch the board view

Pick a view from the switcher at the top of the board:

| View | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| **Summary** | See the project at a glance: **Status overview**, **Priority breakdown**, **Types of work**, **Team workload**, **Epic progress** and **Recent activity**. |
| **Timeline** | See tasks on a calendar by start and due date. Switch the scale with **Weeks**, **Months** or **Quarters**, and click **Today** to jump to today. |
| **Backlog** | Plan work into sprints. See [Plan work in sprints](plan-sprints.md). |
| **Board** | Move cards between status columns. See [Move tasks across the board](move-tasks-on-board.md). |
| **List** | See tasks as a table with **Work**, **Assignee**, **Reporter**, **Priority**, **Status**, **Created**, **Updated** and **Due date**, and change many tasks at once. |

The **Timeline** only shows tasks that have a start or due date.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nothing due or assigned today.** | No task is due today and none was assigned to you today. | Open **Assigned to me** to see all your tasks. |
| **No tasks are assigned to you.** | No task names you as assignee. | Check **Created by me**, or ask the task owner to assign you. |
| **Could not load your tasks.** | The list did not load. | Click **Retry**. |
| **Could not identify the signed-in user.** | Your session is incomplete. | Sign out and sign in again. See [Sign in to Ohanvi](../sign-in.md). |
| **No matching tasks** in every column | A search or filter hides all tasks. | Clear **Search board** and untick the **Type**, **Priority** and person filters. |
| **No members assigned to this project.** in the person filter | The project has no members yet. | Click **Add members** on the board. See [Set up a task project](set-up-task-project.md). |
