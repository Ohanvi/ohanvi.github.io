# Change event settings

Each event has its own settings. They decide how closely a face must match a guest's selfie, when photos reach guests, whether and how often WhatsApp tells guests about new photos, whether guests can add their own photos, what guests can download, and how long selfies are kept.

## Before you start

- The event exists. See [Create an event](create-an-event.md).

## Steps

1. Click **Photos** on the left rail, select **Events**, and open the event.
2. Go to the **Settings** tab. It reads **Choose who can open the gallery, when photos reach guests, and how long the photos are kept.**
3. Pick a group: **General**, **Privacy**, **Download** or **Design**.
4. Change the settings below. Each change saves on its own.

### Face match strictness

How closely a face in a photo must match the guest's selfie.

| Option | Match score | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Strict** | 95 | Guests should see fewer look-alikes. Some of their photos may be missed. |
| **Balanced** | 85 | Most events. |
| **Loose** | 80 | Guests miss photos taken from the side or far away. Look-alikes may show up. |

### Release photos

When new photos reach guests.

- **Straight away** — guests see a photo as soon as their face is found in it.
- **Review first** — new photos are **Held**. Guests see them only after you release them on the **Photos** tab. See [Upload and organise photos](upload-and-organise-photos.md#release-held-photos).

### WhatsApp messages

In **General**. Whether guests get WhatsApp messages about their photos: a link when their photos are ready, and a message when new ones are found.

- **On** — the default. The setting reads **Each message is charged from your WhatsApp wallet.** See [Credits and billing](../credits-and-billing.md).
- **Off** — no photo messages are sent for this event, and **Send photos again** on the **Guests** tab is turned off. Guests still verify on WhatsApp and see their photos in the gallery.

When you turn it back **On** on a published event, guests get a message with the photos found while it was off.

!!! note "Reels have their own switch"
    **WhatsApp messages** covers photo messages only. Reels are sent on WhatsApp by **Send on WhatsApp when ready** in the reel settings. See [Make reels for guests](reels.md#choose-the-look-and-delivery).

### WhatsApp window

In **General**. While the event is live, Ohanvi tells guests on WhatsApp when new photos of them arrive. Matches found within the window are grouped into one message.

- **30 s**, **90 s** or **5 min**.

A longer window sends fewer, bigger messages. A guest gets at most 6 of these live messages a day. You can't change the window while **WhatsApp messages** is **Off**.

### Guest uploads

In **Privacy**. Pick **On** to let guests who verified add their own photos, up to 50 each. You approve each photo before anyone else sees it. It is **Off** for a new event. See [Let guests add their photos](guest-uploads.md).

### Downloads

What guests can save from the gallery.

| Option | Guests can download |
| --- | --- |
| **Originals** | The full-size photo. |
| **Web size** | A smaller copy, sized for screens. |
| **Off** | Nothing. They can only view the photos. |

When the event sells photos, guests download only the web-size copy of photos they haven't paid for, and the original of photos they bought. See [Sell photos to guests](selling.md).

### Watermark

Turn the watermark on to show it on the photos guests see.

### Selfie retention

Pick how long guests' selfies are kept. Selfies are deleted after this period. Guests can delete their own data at any time from the guest page.

!!! note "What a selfie is used for"
    A selfie is used only to find that guest's photos at this event. A search never crosses events.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Guests see photos of look-alikes | **Face match strictness** is too loose. | Pick **Balanced** or **Strict**. |
| Guests miss photos they are in | **Face match strictness** is too strict, or the photos are side-on or far away. | Pick **Balanced** or **Loose**. |
| Guests see no new photos | **Release photos** is **Review first**, and the photos are **Held**. | Release the photos, or pick **Straight away**. |
| Guests get no WhatsApp messages about their photos | **WhatsApp messages** is **Off**. | Pick **On**. Guests get the photos found while it was off. |
| **WhatsApp window** can't be changed | **WhatsApp messages** is **Off**. | Pick **On** first. |
| Guests say they get too many WhatsApp messages | The **WhatsApp window** is short. | Pick **5 min**. Matches are grouped into fewer messages. |
| A guest stops getting WhatsApp messages during the event | They reached 6 live messages for the day, or replied STOP. | No fix needed. Their new photos still show on the guest page. |
| Guests can't download photos | **Downloads** is **Off**. | Pick **Originals** or **Web size**. |
