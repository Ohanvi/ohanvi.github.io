# Settings

Settings holds your own account, your team and every module's configuration. What you see depends on your role: team, billing and invite settings are for the **Super Admin**.

## Where to find it

Settings is not in the left rail. Click your initials at the bottom-left of the screen, then click **Settings**.

The Settings screen lists:

| Group | Rows |
| --- | --- |
| Your name and email | **Team & access** · **Billing & credits** · **Preferences** · **Developer** |
| **Workspace** | **Security**, **Integrations** |
| **Modules** | **WhatsApp**, **CRM**, **Email Marketing**, **Appointments** (only the modules your workspace has) |

The same profile menu also has **My Account** (click your name), **Switch role** (when you hold more than 1 role), **Current usage** and **Sign out**.

## Before you start

- You are signed in. See [Sign in to Ohanvi](../sign-in.md).
- For team, billing and invite settings, your role is **Super Admin**.
