Sample guide

Plan and review an advertisement campaign

Create campaign notes, attach creatives, define budget, and monitor campaign performance.

Beginner9 minutesAdvertisements

Steps

  1. Open Advertisements and choose New campaign.
  2. Add objective, target audience, dates, and budget.
  3. Upload creatives or paste campaign asset links.
  4. Connect the campaign to lead capture or reporting workflows.
  5. Review spend, leads, conversion rate, and campaign notes.

Metrics to explain

  • Spend and remaining budget.
  • Lead count and cost per lead.
  • Conversion rate after WhatsApp or email follow-up.
