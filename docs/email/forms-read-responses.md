# Read form responses

The **Responses** tab inside a form shows its answers. Use it to see trends and read single replies.

## Before you start

- Your form has received at least 1 answer.

## Steps

1. Open **Forms** in the left rail, then select **Forms**.
2. Open the form and select the **Responses** tab. The tab shows the count.
3. Read the summary. It shows each question, **Question N of M**. Rating questions show **Average x of max**. NPS shows the score.
4. Open **Individual** to read 1 response at a time.
5. Read **What happened next** to see what the form did with the answers.
6. Look for **Partly done** or **Not accepted: …**. They say which step did not finish and why.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The count is 0. | Nobody has submitted. | Share the link. |
| A response is **Partly done**. | One outcome failed, such as booking. | Open **Submissions** and click **Retry**. |
