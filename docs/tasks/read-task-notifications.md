# Read task notifications

The notification bell collects alerts in one panel. Its **Tasks** tab shows changes to your tasks in three lists: **Created by me**, **New created** and **Watcher**. A red dot on the bell means something is unread.

## Before you start

- You can see the **Tasks** icon in the right-hand rail. Without task access, the **Tasks** tab is hidden.
- You created, or watch, at least 1 task. See [Comment, mention and attach files](collaborate-on-tasks.md).

## Steps

### Open the notifications panel

1. Find the bell in the right-hand rail.
2. Look for a small red dot on it. The dot means unread task alerts.
3. Click the bell. The **Notifications** panel opens.
4. To close it, click the cross, tooltip **Close**, or click outside the panel.

### Switch between General and Tasks

1. Look at the two tabs under the title: **General** and **Tasks**.
2. Click **General** for alerts from other modules.
3. Click **Tasks** for task alerts. A number beside the tab shows how many are unread.

### Read the task lists

1. On the **Tasks** tab, find the three tabs: **Created by me**, **New created** and **Watcher**.
2. Click **Created by me** for changes to tasks you reported.
3. Click **New created** for tasks that were just created.
4. Click **Watcher** for changes to tasks you watch. See [Comment, mention and attach files](collaborate-on-tasks.md).
5. Read each row. Unread alerts show a dot.
6. If a list is empty, it reads **No notifications here**.

### Reload a list that failed

1. If a list shows an error, find the **Retry** button.
2. Click **Retry**. The list loads again.

### Choose how alerts reach you

1. To turn task alerts on or off, open **Settings**.
2. Follow [Choose how notifications reach you](../settings/notification-preferences.md).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The **Tasks** tab is missing. | Your role has no task access. | Ask your admin. See [Manage team members, roles and permissions](../settings/roles-and-permissions.md). |
| **No notifications here** | Nothing has changed in that list. | Watch a task, or create one. |
| **Failed to load notifications** | The list could not load. | Click **Retry**. |
| The red dot stays on the bell. | Some alerts are unread. | Open each list and read the rows. [VERIFY: how alerts are marked as read] |
