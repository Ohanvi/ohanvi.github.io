# Get more Google reviews with Review Booster

Review Booster gives your business a review page, a QR code and a link. Customers open it, write a few words, and post the review on Google themselves. It takes about 10 minutes to set up.

## What it does

1. A customer scans your QR code, or taps the link you shared.
2. Your review page opens on their phone, in the language you picked. It shows your business name and logo.
3. They pick 1 to 5 stars and type a few words.
4. They can tap **Polish with AI**. The AI tidies only what they wrote. They can edit the result.
5. They tap **Copy & post on Google**. The text is copied, and Google's own review form opens.
6. They paste the text and tap **Post** on Google.

!!! note "Customers post the review themselves"
    Ohanvi never posts a review for a customer. Google has no way to do that. The review goes up only when the customer taps **Post** on Google.

## Before you start

- You can see **Social** in the left rail, with **Review Booster** in its panel. If not, ask your admin to give your role access. See the [Settings overview](../settings/index.md).
- Your page needs a Google review link. Pick one of these:
    - Connect Google Business. Ohanvi then finds your review link by itself. See [Connect social accounts](connect-social-accounts.md).
    - Or paste your review link by hand. See [Find your Google review link](#find-your-google-review-link) below.

### Find your Google review link

1. Open [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com) and sign in with the account that manages your listing.
2. Open your business. You can also search your business name on Google while signed in.
3. Click **Ask for reviews** (on some screens it is **Get more reviews** or **Share review form**).
4. Copy the link Google shows. It starts with `https://g.page/` or `https://search.google.com/`.

## Steps

### Set up your review page

1. Open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Review Booster**.
2. The first time, click **Create review page**.
3. Check the **Google review link** card at the top:
    - **From your Google profile** — the review link comes from your Google Business Profile.
    - **Your own link** — the review link is the one you entered.
    - **No review link yet** — click **Connect Google Business Profile**, or enter your link in **Manual review link**. The page stays closed until a review link is set.
4. To use your own link, enter it in **Manual review link** and click **Save link**. It is used instead of the link from your profile. Click **Remove manual link** to go back to the profile link.
5. Under **Settings**, set the **Business name** customers see. Leave it empty to use your Google listing name.
6. Pick the **Page language**: **English**, **Hindi** or **Hinglish**.
7. Optional: enter a **Logo URL** and pick an **Accent colour**.
8. Turn **AI polish** on or off, and set the **Daily AI limit**.
9. Make sure **Page is live** is on.
10. Click **Save settings**. The message **Settings saved** appears.

| Setting | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | Up to 120 characters. |
| Manual review link | Must start with `https://`, and be a Google link (`g.page`, `search.google.com`, `google.com`, `goo.gl`). |
| Accent colour | A colour code like `#1A73E8`. |
| Logo URL | An image link that starts with `https://`. |
| Daily AI limit | 1 to 1000 polishes a day. The default is 200. |

### Share the QR code or link

1. On **Review Booster**, find your QR code and page link.
2. Click **Copy link** to copy the link. Share it on WhatsApp, SMS, email or your bill.
3. Click **Download QR** to save the QR code as a PNG image.
4. Click **Download poster** to save a ready-to-print poster with the QR code.
5. Print the poster for your counter, table or reception.

!!! tip
    Put the QR code where customers wait or pay. Ask every customer, at the same moment in their visit.

### See what customers see

1. Open your page link on your phone.
2. Pick a star rating. Every rating, from 1 to 5, shows the same Google button.
3. Type a few words about the visit.
4. Tap **Polish with AI**. The text is rewritten into a clear review. Edit it if you like.
5. Tap **Copy & post on Google**. Google's review form opens.
6. Paste the text in Google's form and tap **Post**.

The AI uses only the customer's own words. It adds no facts, products, staff names or claims, and it never picks a rating. If **AI polish** is off in your settings, the daily AI limit is reached, or the AI can't answer, the page asks the customer to post their own words. **Copy & post on Google** always works, even on a phone with JavaScript turned off.

### Read the stats

Review Booster shows the last 30 days, with today's numbers and a daily chart.

| Number | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| Scans | Times your review page was opened, from the QR code or the link. |
| AI polishes | Times a customer used **Polish with AI**. |
| Google opens | Times a customer tapped **Copy & post on Google** and Google's review form opened. |

!!! note "Google opens are not posted reviews"
    Google does not tell Ohanvi whether a customer posted. A Google open means the customer reached Google's form. Your real review count is on Google, and in [Reply to Google reviews](reply-to-google-reviews.md).

### Make a new QR code

Make a new code if your link was shared somewhere you don't want it.

1. On **Review Booster**, under **Settings**, click **Generate new QR code**.
2. Click **Generate new code** to confirm. A new link and QR code appear.
3. Download and print the new QR code or poster.

!!! warning "Old QR codes stop working"
    The old link and every printed QR code stop working at once. Replace your printed posters and update any link you shared, including the review link in your WhatsApp **Review request** automation: it keeps the link it was created with.

### Ask for reviews on WhatsApp

The **Review request** store automation sends customers a WhatsApp message 3 days after delivery. If your review page is live when the store automations are first set up, Ohanvi fills the message's review link with your page link by itself. If the automation was set up earlier, or you made a new QR code since, paste your page link into it yourself (click **Copy link** on **Review Booster**).

See [Automate store messages](../flows/store-journeys.md).

## Google's rules

Google removes reviews, and can restrict your listing, when you break its review rules. Review Booster is built to follow them. Keep to them in what you say and print too.

- **Don't offer rewards.** No discounts, freebies, cashback or contest entries for a review. Don't mention a reward on your poster or message.
- **Ask every customer.** Don't ask only happy customers. Every customer sees the same **Copy & post on Google** button, whatever rating they pick.
- **The customer's own words.** Don't write reviews for customers, and don't post from staff or family accounts. The AI polishes only what the customer typed, and the customer can edit it.

## FAQ

**Can I send unhappy customers to a private form instead?**
No. Every customer sees the same Google button. Google does not allow review gating.

**Does Ohanvi see which customer posted a review?**
No. The page does not ask for a name or number. New reviews show up in [Reply to Google reviews](reply-to-google-reviews.md) after the next sync.

**Who pays for Polish with AI?**
Each polish is charged to your wallet, like other AI features. The daily AI limit caps how many run in a day.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Use your Google review link (it starts with https://g.page/ or https://search.google.com/)** | The pasted link is not a Google link, or starts with `http://`. | Copy the link again from **Ask for reviews** in Google Business Profile. |
| The page says **This review link is not active** | The page is turned off, the code was regenerated, or no review link is set. | Turn the page on, use the latest link, or add a review link. |
| The page asks the customer to wait and try again | Many people opened the same page from one network in a few minutes. | Wait 5 minutes. Customers on their own mobile data are not affected. |
| No review link after connecting Google | Google has not shared your listing's review link yet. | Paste your review link by hand. |
| **Polish with AI** does not work for customers | It is turned off, the daily AI limit is reached, the AI is busy, or your wallet is empty. | Turn it on, raise the daily AI limit, or [add funds](../add-funds.md). Customers can still post their own words. |
| Google opens are high but few new reviews | Some customers open Google and don't post. | No fix needed. Keep asking every customer. |
| A printed QR code stopped working | The code was regenerated. | Print the current QR code or poster. |
