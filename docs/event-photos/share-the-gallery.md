# Share the gallery

Each event has 4 links: one for guests, one for the full gallery, one for the couple's album selection, and one for VIPs. You copy a link, show its QR code, protect it with a PIN, or send it to a CRM list on WhatsApp.

## Before you start

- The event has photos. See [Upload and organise photos](upload-and-organise-photos.md).
- The event is published, or will be before guests look for photos. Guests can register on a draft, but see no photos until you publish. See [Create an event](create-an-event.md#publish-the-event).
- To send a link on WhatsApp, your WhatsApp number is connected and you have an approved template with a variable for the link. See [Connect your WhatsApp number](../whatsapp/connect-whatsapp-number.md).

## The 4 links

| Link | Who it's for | What they see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Guest link** | Every guest. Put its QR code up at the venue. | Only the photos they are in, after they verify on WhatsApp and take a selfie. |
| **Full gallery** | The hosts. | Every published photo. Optional: **Require registration**. |
| **Album selection** | The couple. | The event's photos. They heart the photos they want in the album. Their picks appear on the **Selection** tab. |
| **VIP** | Parents and elders who don't want to type a number. | Their photos after a selfie. No WhatsApp number is asked. |

!!! warning "Never print the VIP link"
    The VIP link skips WhatsApp verification. Send it only to the people it is for. Don't print its QR code or post it anywhere public.

## Steps

### Share the guest QR code

1. Click **Photos** on the left rail, select **Events**, and open the event.
2. Go to the **Share** tab. Each link has its own card with its QR code and address.
3. On the **Guest link** card, check the badge reads **On**. If it reads **Off**, turn on the switch.
4. Click **Copy link** to copy the address. The message **Link copied** appears.
5. Put the QR code up at the venue: on the welcome board, a table stand or a screen.

### Turn a link on or off

1. On the **Share** tab, find the link's card.
2. Turn the switch off. The message, for example **Full gallery link turned off**, appears, and the badge reads **Off**.

A link that is off doesn't open. Turn the switch on again to open it.

### Ask for a PIN

1. On the link's card, turn on **Ask for a PIN**. The **Set a PIN** window opens.
2. In **PIN**, type 4 to 6 digits.
3. Click **Set PIN**. The message **PIN set** appears.
4. Share the PIN with the people who should open the link.

Visitors type the PIN before they see anything. The PIN is stored scrambled and cannot be shown again, so note it down. To pick a new one, click **Change**.

To remove the PIN, turn **Ask for a PIN** off and click **Remove PIN**. Anyone with the link can then open it without a PIN.

### Require registration on the full gallery

1. On the **Full gallery** card, turn on **Require registration**. The message **Registration required** appears.

Visitors then verify a WhatsApp number first, so you know who opened the full gallery.

### Send a link on WhatsApp

1. On the link's card, click **Send on WhatsApp**. The link is copied, the message **Link copied. Paste it into your broadcast message.** appears, and the broadcast composer opens.
2. Under **Contact groups**, tick the CRM list to send to, for example the guest list the couple shared.
3. Choose an approved template that has a variable for the link.
4. Paste the link into that variable, then test and send the campaign. See [Send a broadcast campaign](../whatsapp/send-broadcast-campaign.md).

!!! tip
    Send the **Album selection** link to the couple alone, not to a list.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Use 4 to 6 digits.** | The PIN is too short, too long, or has letters. | Type 4 to 6 digits. |
| You forgot the PIN | The PIN cannot be shown again. | Click **Change** and set a new PIN. Share it again. |
| A visitor says the link doesn't open | The link is turned off. | Turn on the link's switch. |
| Guests open the link but see no photos | The event is still a draft. | Click **Publish** on the event. |
| **Send on WhatsApp** is greyed out | The link is turned off. | Turn on the link's switch. |
| No template fits the link | Broadcasts send only approved templates. | Create a template with a variable for the link, and wait for **Approved**. See [Create a message template](../whatsapp/create-message-template.md). |
