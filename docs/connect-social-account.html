Step-by-step sample

Connect a social media account

Authorize a brand channel so posts can be drafted, approved, and scheduled from Ohanvi.

Admin8 minutesSocial

Before you start

  • You are signed in as an Ohanvi admin.
  • You have admin access to the social page or channel.
  • Pop-ups are allowed in your browser for the authorization window.

Connect the account

  1. Open Social from the left navigation.
  2. Select Connected accounts.
  3. Choose the channel you want to connect.
  4. Click Connect and sign in to the channel account.
  5. Approve the requested permissions.
  6. Return to Ohanvi after the success message appears.

Verify the connection

Check account status

The account should show as Connected with the page name and profile image.

Send a test draft

Create a short draft post and confirm the connected page appears in the channel picker.

Next steps

  • Create a post calendar.
  • Invite reviewers for approval.
  • Schedule the first post from the composer.
