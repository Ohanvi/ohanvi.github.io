# Read Instagram insights

Pick Instagram on **Insights** to see how your account performed: views, reach, interactions and follower change against the period before, your best posts and reels, your stories, the best times to post, and who your followers are. Ohanvi keeps these numbers, so you can look back further than Instagram's own 90 days.

## Before you start

- Instagram is connected with **Direct Instagram login**. An account connected **Via linked Facebook Page** shows publishing only, not insights. See [Connect social accounts](connect-social-accounts.md).
- The account is a professional account (**Business** or **Creator**).
- You allowed insights when you connected. If you connected Instagram before insights were added, reconnect it once. See [Reconnect a network](connect-social-accounts.md#reconnect-a-network).
- Some sections need a minimum: **Audience** needs 100 followers, **Best time to post** needs 10 posts, and a story needs 5 viewers.

## Steps

### Open the Instagram view

1. Open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Insights**.
2. Click **Channels** and pick your Instagram account. You can also click its row under **Performance**.
3. Pick a period at the top: **7d**, **28d**, **30d**, **90d** or **MTD**. Every period ends yesterday, because Instagram's figures can arrive up to 48 hours late.
4. Read the line under the account name: the handle, the follower count, and **Last synced** with the time Ohanvi last collected the numbers.
5. The first time, the page says **Collecting your Instagram insights**. Come back in a few minutes.

### Read the overview

1. Read the 6 tiles under **Overview**: **Views**, **Reach**, **Engaged**, **Interactions**, **Eng. rate** and **Net followers**. Hover over a tile to see what it counts.
2. Read the change under each tile. It compares the period with the one just before it. **Eng. rate** changes in points (**pt**), the others in percent.
3. Under **Trend**, click **Reach**, **Views** or **Followers**. The solid line is this period; the dashed line is the period before.

### Find your best posts and reels

1. Scroll to **Top posts & reels**.
2. Click **All**, **Posts**, **Carousels** or **Reels** to pick what to rank.
3. Click **Sort** and pick **Views**, **Reach**, **Interactions**, **Eng. rate** or **Newest**.
4. Click a row to open the post on Instagram. **via Ohanvi** marks posts you published from Ohanvi.
5. Click **Show all posts** to page through every post in the period. Click **Show top 5** to go back.

### Read your stories

1. Scroll to **Stories**. The heading shows how many stories were captured and how many are **live**.
2. Read **Avg views**, **Avg reach** and **Completion**.
3. Read each story's views, the share of viewers who left on it (**exits**) and its replies. A check mark means the story has expired and its numbers are final.

!!! note "How stories are captured"
    Instagram deletes a story's numbers 24 hours after it is posted. Ohanvi reads live stories every 4 hours and keeps the last reading, so a story's final numbers can be up to 4 hours older than its expiry.

### Pick the best time to post

1. Scroll to **Best time to post**.
2. Click **Engagement** to rank hours by engagement rate, or **Reach** to rank them by reach.
3. Read the grid: one row per day, one column per hour. Darker cells did better.
4. Read **Top:** for the 3 best day-and-hour slots. Times are in your organisation's time zone.

### See who follows you

1. Scroll to **Audience**. **Snapshot** shows the date of the latest weekly reading.
2. Read **Age**, **Gender**, **Top countries** and **Top cities**. Each bar shows its share of followers.

### Refresh the numbers

1. Click **Refresh** in the top bar. Ohanvi asks Instagram for new numbers in the background.
2. The page says **Sync queued. New numbers appear in a few minutes.**
3. Ohanvi allows one refresh every 15 minutes. Inside that time the page says **Next sync allowed at** and a time.

### Ask Ohanvi about the numbers

1. Click **Ask Ohanvi** beside **Overview**. **AI Assistant** → **Agents** opens with a question about this period filled in.
2. Ohanvi picks an agent that has the Instagram tools. Click **Run** to get the answer.
3. If no agent has them, a message says so. Open the agent, tick the Instagram tools under **Edit** → **What it can do**, then run it.

### Get the numbers every week

Ohanvi adds a ready-made draft flow, **Weekly Instagram summary**, to your flows list. Every Monday at 09:00 it sends last week's views, reach, interactions, engagement rate and follower change on WhatsApp, compared with the week before. Open it in **Flows**, fill in who receives it and which WhatsApp template to use, then publish it. See [Build an automation flow](../flows/build-automation-flow.md).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Insights need Instagram connected directly** | The account is connected through a Facebook Page. | Click **Connect directly** and connect with **Direct Instagram login**. |
| **Reconnect Instagram and allow insights** | Your connection was made before insights were added, or you turned insights off. | Click **Reconnect**, sign in, and keep insights allowed. |
| **Switch to a professional account** | The account is a personal Instagram account. | In the Instagram app, switch to a **Business** or **Creator** account, then reconnect. |
| **Instagram needs reconnecting** above the numbers | Instagram stopped accepting Ohanvi's access, for example after a password change. | Click **Reconnect**. The numbers shown are the last ones collected. |
| **Shown from 100 followers (you have N).** | Instagram shares audience data only from 100 followers. | No action needed. The section fills in once you pass 100 followers. |
| **Post N more times to see your best times.** | The best-time grid needs 10 posts in the last 90 days. | Keep posting. The grid appears after 10 posts. |
| **Instagram hides insights under 5 viewers** on a story | Instagram does not report stories with fewer than 5 viewers. | No action needed. |
| Yesterday's numbers are missing | Instagram reports with up to 48 hours of delay. | Check again the next day. |
