# Design a review flyer

Design a printable flyer that carries your review page's QR code, from a ready template or your own photo, then download it as a PNG image or a PDF in A6, A5, A4 or as a table tent. Customers scan it at your counter and post the review on Google themselves. It takes about 5 minutes.

## Before you start

- Your review page is set up and linked to your Google listing. See [Get more Google reviews](get-more-google-reviews.md).
- Your review page is turned on.
- To print your name, **Business name** is set in the **Review page** settings, or your Google listing has a name.
- To print your logo, have it ready as a JPG or PNG file. You can upload it on the flyer.
- With several branches, each branch has its own flyer and QR code. Pick the branch in **Branch:** at the top. See [Manage branches](manage-branches.md).

## Steps

### Open the flyer

1. Click **More** on the left rail, then select **Google Reviews** → **Flyer**.
2. Read the line at the top: **Print this flyer for your counter or reception. Customers scan the code, pick what they liked and post the review on Google themselves.**
3. If a warning shows under it, click **Go to Review page** and fix it before you print:
    - **Your review page is off, so this QR code opens a "not found" page. Turn the page on before you print.**
    - **Your review page isn't linked to your Google listing yet, so it stays closed. Finish setup before you print.**

If you have no review page yet, the screen says **Set up your review page first**. Click **Go to Review page**.

### Pick a template

The **Design** panel is on the left. The **Preview** shows the flyer as you change it. On a wide screen it stays in view while you scroll. On a phone it is below the **Design** panel.

1. Under **Template**, pick one:

    | Template | What it looks like | Suits |
    | --- | --- | --- |
    | **Classic** | A plain flyer with a headline, your QR code and the steps to post. | Any business. |
    | **Tech** | A blue header, a row of service icons and photos at the side. | IT, electronics, repair. |
    | **Studio** | Black and gold, with photo frames on both sides. | Photo studios, events, salons. |
    | **Herbal** | Green and gold, a serif name and products at the side. | Ayurveda, food, organic products. |
    | **Your photo + QR** | Your own finished design or photo, with the QR code placed on it. | A flyer made in Canva or by a designer, or a photo of your shop. |

2. Follow the steps for your template below, then [download and print](#download-and-print) it.

### Design a Classic flyer

1. Under **Theme**, pick **Classic**, **Fresh green**, **Warm amber** or **Dark**.
2. In **Headline**, type a short line above the QR code, up to 60 characters. The counter shows how many you have used. The default is `Loved your visit? Tell Google.`
3. Turn **Show business name** on to print your business name above the headline.
4. Turn **Show logo** on to print your logo. With no logo set, your initials are shown.
5. Drag **QR code size** between 100 and 240. A bigger code is easier to scan from further away.
6. Turn **Logo in QR code** on to put your logo in the middle of the code. Phones still read it. With no logo on the review page, the code prints without one.
7. Under **QR code colour**, pick a colour swatch, or type a colour code such as `#1E3A8A`. Dark colours scan best. The default is `#000000`, black.
8. Under **Paper size**, pick **A6 (105 × 148 mm)**, **A5 (148 × 210 mm)**, **A4 (210 × 297 mm)** or **Table tent (A4, folded)**. The default is A5.
9. Check the **Preview**. Under the QR code it shows the steps **1 Scan**, **2 Pick**, **3 Post** and the line **Takes under a minute · Opens Google reviews**.
10. Click **Save flyer**. The message **Flyer saved** appears. Your design opens the same way next time.

To drop your changes, click **Undo changes**. It brings back the last saved design.

!!! warning "Keep the QR code dark"
    Phones need strong contrast to read a QR code. A light colour shows **Pick a darker QR colour so phones can scan it.** You cannot save or download the flyer until you pick a darker one.

!!! warning "No rewards in the headline"
    Google doesn't allow offers in exchange for reviews. A headline with words such as discount, offer, free, cashback, coupon, voucher, gift, prize, reward or "% off" shows **Remove the reward. Google doesn't allow offers in exchange for reviews.** You cannot save or download the flyer until you remove it.

### Design a Tech, Studio or Herbal flyer

These flyers have 3 parts: a header with your logo, name, tagline and service icons; the middle with photos, **Review us on Google**, your rating, a large QR code and **Scan to review**; and a footer.

1. Under **Template**, pick **Tech**, **Studio** or **Herbal**.
2. Add the photos under **Background photos**. Do one of these:
    - Click **Generate with AI**. The prompt box below is **Filled from your industry and services. Edit it and generate again.** Change the words if you like, then generate again. You can generate 3 backgrounds a day for each review page.
    - Click **Upload photo** and pick a JPG or PNG file, up to 5 MB.
3. Under **Logo**, click **Upload logo** and pick your logo file. It replaces the **Logo URL** in the **Review page** settings, so your review page shows the same logo.
4. In **Tagline**, type a short line under your name, such as `Ayurvedic · Wellness · Herbal Products`.
5. Under **Service icons**, check the icons. Each has an icon and a label. Click a label to change it, remove the ones you don't want, or click **+ Add** to add one. Use up to 7.
6. Turn **Show Google rating** on or off. It shows your real rating from your Google listing, such as **4.8**.
7. Turn **Show "Powered by Ohanvi"** on or off.
8. Under **Paper size**, pick **A6**, **A5**, **A4** or **Table tent**.
9. Check the **Preview**. Its title shows the paper size, such as **A5 · 148 × 210 mm**, and it **Updates as you edit**.
10. Click **Save flyer**.

Your industry's icons and background prompt are filled in for you the first time. See [Choose the questions customers answer](get-more-google-reviews.md#choose-the-questions-customers-answer) to change your industry.

!!! note "What the AI draws and what it doesn't"
    The AI makes only the background photos, and leaves the middle plain for the QR code. Ohanvi prints the QR code, your name, tagline, icons, rating and **Scan to review** itself, so the code always scans and the spelling is right. The AI never writes text or logos into the photo.

!!! note "Your rating, only when it is real"
    The rating comes from your confirmed Google listing. If your listing has no rating yet, or you pasted a review link instead of confirming a listing, no rating or stars are shown.

### Use your own photo with the QR code

Use this when you already have a finished design or a photo. You upload it and place your QR code on it.

1. Under **Template**, pick **Your photo + QR**.
2. Click **Upload your photo** and pick a JPG or PNG file, up to 10 MB. The **Your photo** preview shows it with the QR code on top. The line above reads **Drag the QR code to place it. Drag the blue corner or use the slider to resize it.**
3. Drag the QR code to where it should print.
4. To change its size, drag the blue corner of the QR code, or move the **Size** slider between 8% and 70% of the photo's width. The label shows the size on paper, such as **28% of width · about 4.1 cm on A5**.
5. Optional: under **Position**, click **Top left**, **Top right**, **Centre**, **Bottom left** or **Bottom right** to snap the QR code there.
6. Under **Style**, pick **White box + "Scan to review"** to put the code on a white box with the words under it, or **QR only**.
7. Under **Paper size**, pick **A6**, **A5**, **A4** or **Table tent**.
8. Click **Save flyer**. The QR code's place and size are saved as a share of the photo, so it lands in the same spot on every paper size.

!!! warning "Keep the QR code big enough"
    A QR code smaller than about 2.5 cm on paper, or one on a busy photo without the white box, may not scan. The app warns you before you download. Make it bigger, pick a larger **Paper size**, or turn on the white box.

### Download and print

1. Under the preview, click **Download PNG** or **Download PDF**. The button reads **Preparing…** while the file is made.
2. The file saves as `google-review-flyer.png` or `google-review-flyer.pdf`. The message **Flyer downloaded** or **Flyer PDF downloaded** appears. When the app saves to a folder, it reads **Flyer saved to** or **Flyer PDF saved to**, followed by the file's location.
3. Print it and put it where customers wait or pay.

Downloads use the design shown here, saved or not. With **Your photo + QR**, the QR code is printed on top of your photo at full resolution. The PDF prints at full paper size:

| Paper size | The PDF |
| --- | --- |
| A6, A5 or A4 | One page of that size. |
| Table tent | One A4 landscape page with the flyer twice. The top copy is upside down. |

### Fold a table tent

1. Pick **Table tent (A4, folded)** and download the PDF.
2. Print it on A4 at full size.
3. Fold along the dashed line.
4. Stand it on a table. Both sides show the flyer.

!!! tip
    Scan the printed flyer with your own phone before you put it out. It should open your review page.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Remove the reward. Google doesn't allow offers in exchange for reviews.** | The headline mentions a reward. | Rewrite the headline without the offer. |
| **Keep the headline to 60 characters.** | The headline is too long. | Shorten it. |
| **Download PNG** and **Download PDF** do nothing, and the preview says **Remove the reward from the headline to download the flyer.** | The headline has a reward word. | Remove the reward from the headline. |
| The printed QR code opens a "not found" page | The review page is off, a new QR code was made, or the page is not linked to Google. | Click **Go to Review page**, fix the page, then download and print again. |
| The flyer shows initials instead of a logo | No logo is set on the review page. | Add a logo on **Review page**, or turn **Show logo** off. |
| Customers can't scan the code from a distance | The QR code is small. | Move **QR code size** up, or pick a bigger **Paper size**, then download again. |
| **Pick a darker QR colour so phones can scan it.** | The QR code colour is too light. | Pick one of the swatches, or a darker colour code. |
| **Use a colour code like #1A2B3C.** | The colour code is not 6 characters after `#`. | Type a full code, such as `#1E3A8A`. |
| The preview says **Fix the QR code colour to download the flyer.** | The QR code colour can't be used. | Fix the colour under **QR code colour**. |
| The QR code prints without your logo | No logo is set on the review page. | Add a **Logo URL** on **Review page**, then download again. |
| **Generate with AI** stops working for the day | You generated 3 backgrounds today for this review page. | Use one of the backgrounds you made, click **Upload photo**, or try again tomorrow. |
| The AI background doesn't fit your business | The prompt doesn't describe your business well. | Edit the prompt under **Background photos** with your products or services, then generate again. |
| An upload is refused | The file is not JPG or PNG, or it is too large. Background photos can be up to 5 MB and your own photo up to 10 MB. | Save the image as JPG or PNG and make it smaller, then upload it again. |
| The flyer shows no rating | Your Google listing has no rating yet, it is not confirmed, or **Show Google rating** is off. | Confirm your business on **Review page**, or turn **Show Google rating** on. |
| A warning says the QR code may not scan | With **Your photo + QR**, the code is smaller than about 2.5 cm on paper. | Drag the blue corner or the **Size** slider to make it bigger, or pick a larger **Paper size**. |
| The QR code is hard to scan on your photo | The photo is busy behind the code. | Under **Style**, pick **White box + "Scan to review"**, or move the code to a plain part of the photo. |
| The table tent's sides don't line up | The PDF was printed scaled to fit. | Print at full size, without scaling. |
