# Review Booster is now Google Reviews

Review Booster has moved out of **Social**. It is now its own **Google Reviews** section under **More** on the left rail, with **Review page**, **Flyer**, **Stats** and **Private notes**. Your review page, link and printed QR codes keep working.

## Where to go now

| You want to | Read |
| --- | --- |
| Set up your review page and share the QR code | [Get more Google reviews](../google-reviews/get-more-google-reviews.md) |
| Print a flyer with your QR code | [Design a review flyer](../google-reviews/design-a-review-flyer.md) |
| See scans, drafts made and Google opens | [Read Google review stats](../google-reviews/read-google-review-stats.md) |
| Read notes from 1 to 3 star customers | [Read private notes](../google-reviews/read-private-notes.md) |
| Ask for a review after each appointment | [Ask for a Google review after an appointment](../google-reviews/ask-for-a-review-after-an-appointment.md) |
| Reply to reviews inside Ohanvi | [Reply to Google reviews](reply-to-google-reviews.md) |

## What changed

- **No link to paste.** Ohanvi finds your business on Google, and you confirm it with one click.
- **Tags and drafts.** Customers tap what stood out and get up to 3 AI drafts, in place of **Polish with AI**.
- **Flyer designer.** Themes, headline, name, logo and QR code size, with PNG and PDF downloads, in place of the single poster.
- **Private notes.** Customers who give 1 to 3 stars can also send you a private note. The Google button stays on their screen.
- **Google rating and review count** on **Stats**.
