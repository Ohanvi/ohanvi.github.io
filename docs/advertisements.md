# Plan and review an advertisement campaign

Create campaign notes, attach creatives, define budget, and monitor campaign performance.

## Steps

1. Open **Advertisements**.
2. Choose **New campaign**.
3. Add objective, target audience, dates, and budget.
4. Upload creatives or paste campaign asset links.
5. Connect the campaign to lead capture or reporting workflows.
6. Review spend, leads, conversion rate, and campaign notes.

## Metrics to explain

| Metric | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Spend | Amount used from the campaign budget. |
| Leads | Number of contacts captured from the campaign. |
| Cost per lead | Spend divided by leads. |
| Conversion rate | Leads that became replies, bookings, orders, or customers. |
