# Use Publish filters, overview and post details

This page covers the smaller parts of **Publish**: the channel filter, the side panel, the menus on each post, and the details window. You can find any post, copy its link on the network, see which channel failed, and copy a post to reuse it. It takes about 5 minutes.

## Before you start

- You can see **Social** in the left rail, with **Publish** in its panel.
- At least one network is connected, and you have created at least one post. See [Create and publish a social post](create-social-post.md).

## Steps

### Read the tabs and counts

1. Open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Publish**.
2. Look at the tabs: **Queue**, **Drafts**, **Approvals** and **Sent**. Each tab shows how many posts it holds.
3. Click **List** or **Calendar** to switch the view. See [Schedule posts and manage the queue](schedule-social-posts.md) for the calendar.
4. If a tab is empty, it says **No … posts yet.**
5. Click the refresh icon to reload the posts.

### Start for the first time

If no channel is connected, **Publish** shows **Welcome to Publish**.

1. Click **Connect a Channel** to connect your first network. See [Connect social accounts](connect-social-accounts.md).
2. Or click **Invite your Team** to add teammates first.

### Filter by channel

1. Click **Channels** in the filter row. On a narrow screen, click **Filters** first.
2. Type in **Search channels** to find a channel.
3. Tick the channels you want. Click **Select all** to tick every channel.
4. If no channel matches, the list says **No channels found**. Click **Connect a Channel** to add one.
5. The list and the counts now show only those channels.

### Check the Tags and Timezone filters

1. Click **Tags**. It says **No tags** and **Use tags to organise your content**. Tags are not available to pick yet.
2. Click **Timezone**. It says **Based on this device — per-user timezones are coming soon.** Post times follow your device.

### Find drafts with no date

1. In **Calendar** view, click **No Date**.
2. Read **Undated drafts**. It lists drafts and approvals that have no scheduled date.
3. If there are none, it says **No Undated Drafts**.

### Read the Publishing Overview

1. Look at the panel on the right of **Publish**.
2. Under **Publishing Overview**, read **This Week**. It counts **Queued**, **Drafts**, **Published** and **Failed**.
3. Read the line that says what percent of your posts published successfully this week.

### Use Quick Actions

1. In the same panel, find **Quick Actions**.
2. Click **Create Post** to open the composer.
3. Click **Create from Template** to open **Templates**. See [Use post templates](use-post-templates.md).
4. Click **View Calendar** to switch to the calendar.
5. **Bulk Schedule** and **Media Library** show a message that they are coming soon.

### Use the menu on a post

1. On a post, click the **⋯** menu.
2. On a **Queue** post, you can click **Publish Now** or **Cancel schedule**.
3. On any post, click **Duplicate** to make a copy you can edit and send again.
4. Click **Delete** to remove it.
5. On a **Sent** post, you can also click **Post Details** or **Copy link**.
6. Click **Copy link** to copy the post's link on the network. The message **Link copied.** appears. If the post has no link yet, it says **No published link available yet.**

### Open the post details

1. On a **Sent** post, click **⋯**, then **Post Details**.
2. The title reads **Published on** and the date.
3. Read the channel and the post text. Media shows as a strip below.
4. Under **Channels**, read which network published and which failed. A failed one shows the network's error.
5. Read the numbers row for the post.
6. Click **Go to post** to open the post on the network. If it has no link yet, it says **No published link available yet.**

### See the analytics of one post

1. On a **Sent** post that did not fail, click **View Analytics**. A failed post shows **Retry** instead.
2. The **Post Analytics** window opens with a row for each network.
3. If there are no figures yet, it says **No published targets for this post yet.**
4. Click **Close** when you are done.

### Cancel a scheduled post

1. On a **Queue** post, click **⋯**, then **Cancel schedule**.
2. In **Cancel this scheduled post?**, click **Cancel schedule** to confirm, or **Keep scheduled** to leave it.

### Send feedback

1. Click **Share Feedback** at the top of **Publish**.
2. Type your thoughts and click **Submit**, or **Close** to leave.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Welcome to Publish** shows | No channel is connected. | Click **Connect a Channel**. |
| **No channels found** | Your search matches nothing, or nothing is connected. | Clear the search, or connect a channel. |
| **No published link available yet.** | The network has not returned the link. | Wait a minute and try again. |
| **Bulk Schedule** or **Media Library** only shows a message | They are not built yet. | Schedule posts one by one. |
| **Tags** says **No tags** | Tags cannot be added yet. | No fix. |
