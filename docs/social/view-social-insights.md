# Read social insights

Use **Home** to see what needs action today, and **Insights** to see how your posts perform. You can compare networks side by side, find your top posts, and open the numbers for any single post you published.

## Before you start

- You can see **Social** in the left rail, with **Home** and **Insights** in its panel. If not, ask your admin to give your role Social access. See the [Settings overview](../settings/index.md).
- At least one network is connected, and you have published a post. See [Create and publish a social post](create-social-post.md).

## Steps

### Check Social Home

1. Open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Home**.
2. To see one network only, click **All platforms** and pick a network.
3. Read **NEEDS ATTENTION**. It lists items to act on, each with a button:
    - **Google reviews** that need a response — click **Reply**. See [Manage your Google Business Profile](manage-google-business-profile.md).
    - **Failed posts** that need a retry — click **Retry**.
    - A **connection** that needs attention — reconnect it in **Connect** → **Social Connections**.
4. Read **TODAY** for posts going out today. Click **Calendar** to open the full calendar.
5. Under **Performance**, pick **This Week**, **Last 30 Days** or **Last 90 Days**. The card shows **Reach**, **Engagement**, **Followers** and **Posts**.
6. Scroll to **RECENT ACTIVITY** and **RECENT POSTS**. Click a post, then **Open in Publish** to manage it.

If nothing is connected yet, Home shows **Connect your first social account**. Click **Connect Platforms** to start.

### Compare performance on Insights

1. Open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Insights**.
2. To narrow to one network, click **All Channels** and pick a channel.
3. Pick a date range: **7 days**, **30 days** or **Month to date**. Each figure is compared with the period before.
4. Read the **Summary**: **Posts**, **Reactions**, **Comments**, **Eng. Rate**, **Impressions** and **Shares**. Hover over a figure to see what it counts.
5. Under **Performance**, compare each channel's **Posts**, **Reactions**, **Comments** and **Eng. Rate**.
6. Under **Top 5 Posts**, switch between **Reactions** and **Comments** to rank your best posts.
7. Under **Performance per Post**, see the numbers for every post published in the period.

### See the numbers for one post

1. Open **Social** → **Publish**, then click the **Sent** tab.
2. Each post shows its **Reach**, **Engagement**, **Likes** and **Comments**.
3. Click **View Analytics** on the post. The **Post Analytics** window opens with the numbers for each network.
4. Click **Close** when you are done.

!!! note "What the networks report"
    Not every network shares every number. Follower counts, impressions and reach are not reported by the connected networks yet, so **Total Followers** and the **Followers** chart can stay empty. **Shares** come from Facebook only. **Eng. Rate** needs impressions, so it is often blank.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No published posts in this period.** | Nothing was published in the date range you picked. | Pick a longer range, or publish a post. |
| **No channels connected** | No network is connected. | Connect one in **Connect** → **Social Connections**. |
| **No published targets for this post yet.** | The post has not gone out to any network yet. | Wait until the post is **Published**, then open **View Analytics** again. |
| **Analytics not available** for a network | That network does not share post numbers with Ohanvi. | Check the numbers on the network itself. |
| Numbers look out of date | Figures refresh in the background. | Click **Refresh**. |
