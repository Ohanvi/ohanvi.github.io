# Read the sales forecast

See how much your open deals are likely to bring in, and how that compares with your targets. At the end, you can answer "Will we make this month?" from one screen. Reading the forecast takes about 5 minutes.

## Before you start

- You can see **CRM** in the left rail with **Forecast** in its panel. It needs access to the CRM reports. If you do not see it, ask your admin.
- Your deals have stages, amounts and expected close dates. The figures come from them. See [Create and manage deals](manage-deals.md).
- For the quota figures, set quotas first. See [Set sales quotas](set-sales-quotas.md).

## Steps

### Open the forecast

1. Open **CRM** in the left rail, then select **Forecast**.

### Read the three figures

1. Read **Weighted forecast**. It is each open deal's value multiplied by its chance of winning.
2. Read **Open pipeline**. It is the best case if every open deal closes.
3. Read **Committed**. It is the weighted value of deals in stages at 80% or higher.

### Read the charts

1. Read **Weighted pipeline by stage** to see which stage holds the money.
2. Read **Quota attainment** to see progress against the month's quota.
3. Read **3-month forecast**. In this chart, **Committed** is already won, and **Upside** is the weighted value of open deals.

### Download the forecast

1. Click **Export**.
2. Ohanvi downloads the forecast file.

### Open the quotas

1. Click **Manage quotas**. The **Sales Quotas** list opens.
2. To add a target, see [Set sales quotas](set-sales-quotas.md).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The figures are 0 | Your open deals have no amounts or close dates. | Fill in **Amount** and **Expected close** on your deals. |
| **Quota attainment** is empty | No quota is set for the period. | Add a quota. See [Set sales quotas](set-sales-quotas.md). |
| **Forecast** is missing from the **CRM** panel | Your role has no access to CRM reports. | Ask your admin. |
