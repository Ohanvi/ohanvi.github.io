# Use the AI Assistant in the composer

The **AI Assistant** writes a caption and finds an image for your post. It takes over the preview side of the **Create Post** window until you go back. You can then rewrite the caption or swap the image. It takes about 3 minutes.

## Before you start

- You have opened the **Create Post** window and picked at least one channel. See [Create and publish a social post](create-social-post.md).
- You know what the post is about, or you want to start from a trending topic.

## Steps

### Open the assistant

1. In **Create Post**, click **AI Assistant** at the top. The label changes to **Close AI Assistant**.
2. Read the line **Writing for …**. It names the channels you picked. The assistant writes for the tightest of them.
3. To leave the assistant, click the back arrow (**Back to preview**).

### Describe the post

1. Under **What should this post be about?**, type your topic. For example: `Diwali sale on ethnic wear`.
2. If the composer already has a caption, this box reads **Write something new**.
3. Tick **Caption only, no image** if you do not want an image.
4. Click **Generate post**. The button reads **Generating…** and the pane says **Writing your caption and finding an image…**.
5. When it finishes, the caption and image appear in the composer.

### Start from a trending topic

1. Under **Or start from what's trending**, pick a topic area in the list at the right.
2. Click a topic chip. The assistant shows up to 20 topics.
3. Click **Generate post**.
4. If the topic box already has text, your own text is used, not the chip.

If the pane says **Trending topics aren't available right now — type your own above.**, type your own topic. For a topic area with nothing trending, it says **No trending topics for this area right now — try another, or type your own above.**

### Rewrite the caption

1. After a caption exists, find **Refine your caption**.
2. Click one of the buttons:
    - **Rephrase** says the same thing in new words.
    - **Shorten to N** makes it fit the tightest limit among your channels. `N` is that limit.
    - **Expand** makes it longer.
    - **More casual** makes it friendlier.
    - **More formal** makes it more professional.
3. A spinner shows on the button while it works. The caption in the composer changes.

### Swap the image

1. Under **Image**, change the words in **Search words, e.g. diwali lights**. They start as the words the AI chose.
2. Click **Change image**. It reads **Finding…** while it searches.
3. The new image replaces the old one. The caption does not change.

### Finish the post

1. Click the back arrow (**Back to preview**) to see the post on each channel.
2. Click **Publish Now**, **Save Draft** or **Schedule**. See [Create and publish a social post](create-social-post.md).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Generate post** is greyed out | The topic box is empty and no trend is picked. | Type a topic, or click a trend chip. |
| The assistant shows an error in red | The AI could not finish. | Click **Generate post** again, or shorten your topic. |
| **Change image** finds a poor match | The search words are too general. | Type more specific words, then click **Change image** again. |
| The caption is too long for a channel | The assistant writes for the tightest limit, but you edited it. | Click **Shorten to N**. |
