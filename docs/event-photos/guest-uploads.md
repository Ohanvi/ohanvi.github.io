# Let guests add their photos

With Guest uploads on, guests who verified their WhatsApp number can add the photos they took at the event, up to 50 each. Every photo waits for you. Nobody else sees it until you approve it on the **Photos** tab. Approved photos go into the **Guest photos** folder, and guests who are in them find them with their selfie, like any other photo.

## Before you start

- The event exists. See [Create an event](create-an-event.md).
- Your WhatsApp number is connected. Guests must verify their number before they can add photos. See [Connect your WhatsApp number](../whatsapp/connect-whatsapp-number.md).

## Steps

### Turn on guest uploads

Guest uploads are off for a new event.

1. Click **Photos** on the left rail, select **Events**, and open the event.
2. Go to the **Settings** tab, and pick **Privacy**.
3. Under **Guest uploads**, pick **On**. The change saves on its own.

The setting now reads **Guests can add up to 50 photos each (JPEG or PNG, up to 25 MB). You approve each one on the Photos tab, under From guests, before anyone sees it.**

To stop taking photos, pick **Off**. Guests can't add any more. Photos they already sent stay where they are: approved ones stay in the gallery, and the rest still wait under **From guests**.

### Approve or delete guest photos

1. On the **Photos** tab, click **From guests**. The chip shows how many guest photos are waiting, for example **From guests · 3**.
2. Read the strip at the top, for example **3 guest photos waiting for approval**. Each photo is marked **To approve**, and shows who sent it, such as **From Neha**.
3. Double-click a photo to see it larger.
4. Select the photos. The bar reads, for example, **2 photos selected**. To select every photo on the list, click **Select all shown**.
5. Click **Approve** to show them in the gallery. Or click **Delete**, then **Delete** again in the **Delete 2 guest photos?** window.

When none are left, the list reads **No guest photos waiting**. Click **Show all photos** to go back.

An approved photo works like one you uploaded:

- It sits in the **Guest photos** folder.
- Ohanvi has already looked for faces in it, so guests who are in it find it with their selfie.
- Guests who are in it get a WhatsApp message, when the event's **WhatsApp messages** are on. See [Change event settings](event-settings.md#whatsapp-messages).

!!! note "Approve each photo yourself"
    There is no button to approve every guest photo at once. **Release all** on the **Photos** tab also leaves guest photos out. Look at each photo before you approve it.

!!! warning
    A deleted guest photo is removed from the event, and nobody else ever sees it. It still counts toward the guest's 50 photos.

## What guests see

Once a guest has verified, the top of their photos shows **Took photos at the event? Add them to the gallery.** with **Add your photos**. The same item is in the page menu.

1. Tap **Add your photos**. The page reads **The studio reviews photos before anyone sees them.**
2. Tap **Choose photos** and pick several photos.
3. Keep the page open. It reads, for example, **Sending 2 of 5… Keep this page open until your photos are sent.** When it is done: **5 photos sent. The studio will review them.**
4. Read **Your photos**. Each photo shows **Waiting for approval** or **Approved**.

- The page counts the photos, for example **You’ve added 12 of 50 photos.** and **You can add 38 more photos.**
- Each photo can be JPEG or PNG, up to 25 MB. An iPhone (HEIC) photo, or a larger one, is turned into a JPEG on the phone first, when the phone's browser can open it.
- A photo that fails shows the reason, with **Try again** and **Remove**.

See [What guests see](what-guests-see.md#add-your-photos).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A guest doesn't see **Add your photos** | **Guest uploads** is **Off**, or the guest hasn't verified their number. | Pick **On** under **Settings** → **Privacy** → **Guest uploads**. Ask the guest to tap **Find my photos** and verify. |
| A guest sees **The studio isn’t taking photos from guests right now.** | **Guest uploads** is **Off**. | Pick **On**, if you want their photos. |
| A guest sees **You’ve added as many photos as you can for this event.** | They sent 50 photos. Photos you deleted still count. | No fix needed. Ask them to send any others another way. |
| A guest sees **That photo is too large. The limit is 25 MB.** | The file is over 25 MB, and the phone could not shrink it. | Ask the guest to pick a smaller copy of the photo. |
| A guest sees **We couldn’t open that photo. Choose a JPEG or PNG.** | The photo isn't JPEG or PNG, and the phone's browser could not change it. Often an iPhone HEIC photo on a computer. | Ask the guest to pick a JPEG or PNG. On an iPhone, they can set **Settings** → **Camera** → **Formats** → **Most Compatible** for new photos. |
| A guest sees **Verify again to add photos.** | Their session on the page ended. | Ask them to verify their number again, then add the photos. |
| There is no **From guests** chip | **Guest uploads** is **Off**, and no guest photo is waiting. | Turn **Guest uploads** on. |
| An approved photo doesn't show for a guest who is in it | Ohanvi didn't match their face in it, for example a side-on face. | Check the event's match strictness. See [Change event settings](event-settings.md#face-match-strictness). |
