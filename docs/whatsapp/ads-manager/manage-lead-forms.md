# Manage lead forms

Open the **Forms** tab to see the Meta lead forms attached to your Page. At the end, your form list is synced from Meta, you can search it, and you can read each form's status, questions and leads. You can also create a new form with a **Lead form** ad. Takes about 2 minutes.

## Before you start

- Your Facebook account, Page and ad account are connected in **Setup**. See [Run Click-to-WhatsApp ads](../click-to-whatsapp-ads.md).
- You accepted the **Terms of Service for Lead Form Ads** in **Setup**.
- You launched at least 1 **Lead form** ad, or a lead form already exists on your Page. See [Create an ad](create-an-ad.md).

## What a lead form is

A lead form opens inside Facebook or Instagram when someone taps your ad. The person fills in details such as name and phone without leaving the app. Their answers arrive in **Leads**. You follow up on WhatsApp.

The **Forms** tab only lists and syncs forms. You create a form while you create a **Lead form** ad.

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **FORM NAME** | The name of the form. |
| **STATUS** | The form's status, for example **ACTIVE**. |
| **CREATED** | The date the form was created. |
| **QUESTIONS** | How many questions the form asks. |
| **LEADS** | How many leads the form has collected. |

## Steps

### Open the list

1. Open **WhatsApp** in the left rail, then select **Ads Manager**.
2. Click **Forms**. The form list opens, 25 rows at a time.

    

### Pull the latest forms from Meta

1. Click **Sync**. [VERIFY: the in-page button reads **Sync from Meta** when the ribbon is off]
2. Wait while the button reads **Syncing…**.
3. Check the table. Forms created on Meta appear in the list.

Hover over **Sync** to see its tooltip: **Pull the latest lead forms from the Meta ad account**.

### Find a form

1. Click the **Search forms** box.
2. Type part of the form name. The table filters as you type.
3. Clear the box to see all forms again.

### Check a form's results

1. Find the form in the table.
2. Read **STATUS**. **ACTIVE** means the form is in use.
3. Read **QUESTIONS** to see how many questions it asks.
4. Read **LEADS** to see how many people submitted it.
5. Open **Leads** to see the people. See [Manage ad leads](manage-ad-leads.md).

### Create a lead form

1. Click **Create Ad**.
2. Under **Ad type**, choose **Lead form**.
3. Under **Use an existing form**, choose **Create a new form**.
4. Under **What to ask for**, tick the details to collect. Name and phone are the usual choices.
5. Optional: type a **Custom survey question (optional)**, for example `Which course are you interested in?`.
6. Type your **Privacy policy URL**. Meta requires one on every lead form.
7. Finish the other sections of the ad.
8. Click **Launch Ad**.
9. Return to **Forms** and click **Sync**. The new form appears.

    

!!! tip
    To reuse a form on a new ad, pick it under **Use an existing form** in **Create Ad**. Leads then stay together in one form.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
|-------|-------|-----|
| **Connect your Meta ad account** with a **Set up now** button | Setup is not finished. | Click **Set up now** and finish **Setup**. |
| **No lead forms yet** | No lead form exists on the Page, or the list is not synced. | Click **Sync**. If the list is still empty, click **Launch Lead Form Ad** and create one. |
| **Could not load lead forms** | The list did not load. | Click **Retry**. |
| **Add your privacy policy URL — Meta requires one on every lead form.** | A new form has no privacy policy link. | Fill **Privacy policy URL** with a working https page. |
| A new form is missing from the list | The list was loaded before the form existed on Meta. | Click **Sync**. |
| **LEADS** shows 0 | Nobody has submitted the form yet, or the ad is still in review. | Check the ad status in **Ads Manager**. Meta reviews ads, usually within 24 hours. |
