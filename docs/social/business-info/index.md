# Business Info

Business Info edits your Google Business Profile details. You can also answer and track Google reviews.

**Where to find it:** open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Business Info**. Reviews open from **Home** under **NEEDS ATTENTION**.

## Guides

| Guide | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| [Manage your Google Business Profile](../manage-google-business-profile.md) | You edit details, hours, links or verification. |
| [Reply to Google reviews](../reply-to-google-reviews.md) | You answer or track reviews. |

## Before you start

- Google Business is connected. See [Connect social accounts](../connect-social-accounts.md).
