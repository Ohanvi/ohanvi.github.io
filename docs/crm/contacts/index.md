# Contacts

**Contacts** holds the people your business talks to. WhatsApp, Email, Commerce and CRM all read the same record, so a change here shows everywhere. Use these guides to add, find, call, email, group and protect your contacts.

**Where to find it:** open **Contacts** in the left rail.

| --- | --- | --- |
| **All contacts** | [Manage contacts](../manage-contacts.md) | You want to add, find, edit, tag, merge or export contacts. |
| **All contacts** | [Call contacts and review your calls](../call-contacts-and-review-calls.md) | You call a contact and want to record the outcome. |
| **All contacts** | [Call many contacts in a row with Auto Calling](../auto-call-contacts.md) | You have a list to call one after another. |

## Before you start

- Your workspace has the CRM module, and your role has CRM access. Otherwise the **Contacts** icon does not appear. Ask your admin.
- For contacts to arrive on their own from chats, your WhatsApp number is connected.
