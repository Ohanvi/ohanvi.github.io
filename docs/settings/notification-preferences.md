# Choose how notifications reach you

Pick, module by module, whether Ohanvi tells you in the app, by push notification or by email. Changes save the moment you flip a switch, so you are done as soon as the switches look right.

## Before you start

- You are signed in to Ohanvi. See [Sign in to Ohanvi](../sign-in.md).
- You have access to at least 1 module that sends notifications, such as **WhatsApp** or **CRM**.

## Steps

### Turn notifications on or off

1. Click your initials in the bottom-left corner, then click **Settings**.
2. Click your name at the top of the Settings list.
3. Under **Preferences**, click **Notifications**. The **Notifications** page opens with 1 row per module.
4. On a module's row, flip the switch under **In-app** or **Push**. **Saving…** shows briefly at the top.

Only modules you can open are listed. New modules appear when you get access to them.

### Fine-tune single events

1. On a module's row, click **Fine-tune per event**. That module's own notification settings open.
2. Turn single events on or off there.

Not every module has this link.

### Email notifications for the whole workspace

Super Admins also see an **Email** column. It turns email alerts for that module on or off for the whole workspace, not only for you. Everyone else sees **In-app** and **Push** only.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Couldn't save that change — please try again.** | The change did not reach the server. | Check your connection and flip the switch again. |
| **Couldn't save the email setting — please try again.** | The email switch did not save. | Try again. If it keeps failing, contact support. |
| **No notification settings for your current access yet — they appear as you get access to modules that send notifications.** | Your role has no module that sends notifications. | Ask your workspace owner for module access. |
| There is no **Email** column | Only Super Admins can change email alerts. | Ask your workspace owner. |
| Push notifications do not arrive | Your browser or phone blocks notifications from Ohanvi. | Allow notifications for app.ohanvi.com in your browser or phone settings [VERIFY: push delivery requirements]. |
