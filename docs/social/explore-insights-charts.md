# Explore Insights charts and one channel

Insights shows all your channels together. Click one channel to see its posts, weekly takeaways and metric charts. This page explains every chart, toggle and filter on **Insights**. It takes about 5 minutes.

## Before you start

- You can see **Social** in the left rail, with **Insights** in its panel.
- At least one network is connected and has published posts. See [Create and publish a social post](create-social-post.md).
- For the summary figures and top posts, see [Read social insights](view-social-insights.md).

## Steps

### Pick the period and the channel

1. Open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Insights**.
2. Pick **7 days**, **30 days** or **Month to date** at the top. Each figure is compared with the period before.
3. Click **Channels**.
4. Pick **All Channels**, or one channel. A filled dot marks your choice.
5. If no channel is connected, the list says **No channels connected**.
6. Click **Refresh** to reload the numbers.

### Use the Tags filter

1. Click **Tags**.
2. Read the list. Today it says **No tags available yet**.

!!! note
    Tags are on posts, but Insights does not roll them up yet. The filter is there, but it has nothing to pick.

### Read the Performance table for all channels

1. With **All Channels** selected, scroll to **Performance**.
2. Read each channel's **Posts**, **Reactions**, **Comments** and **Eng. Rate**.
3. Click a channel's row. Insights switches to that channel.
4. If a channel had no posts, the table says **No channel activity in this period.**

### Read the Followers chart

1. Scroll to **Followers**.
2. Click **Bar**, **Line** or **Growth** at the top right of the chart.
3. **Bar** compares channels. **Line** shows the trend. **Growth** shows how much followers changed.

### Read the Posts chart

1. Scroll to **Posts**.
2. Click **Bar** to compare how many posts each channel published.
3. Click **Stacked Bar** to stack the channels on top of each other.

### Look at one channel

1. Pick one channel in **Channels**, or click its row in **Performance**.
2. Read **WEEKLY TAKEAWAYS** at the top. Click the arrow beside it to fold it.
3. Read the two cards:
    - **Start Building Your Posting Habit** shows how many posts you published in the period. Click **Create a Post** to open **Publish**.
    - **Set a Posting Goal** is a reminder to set a goal. It has no button yet.
4. Read **Performance per Post**. It lists every post with its **Reactions**, **Comments** and **Eng. Rate**. The line **Posts · N** shows the count.
5. If you published nothing, it says **No published posts in this period.**

### Read the Metrics tabs

These appear when one channel is selected.

1. Scroll to **Metrics**.
2. Click **Content Impact** to see the posts chart.
3. Click **Audience Growth** to see the follower line chart.
4. Click **Visibility**. It says **Impressions and reach are not reported by the connected networks yet.**

### Rank your top posts

1. Under **Top 5 Posts**, click **Reactions** or **Comments** at the top right.
2. Read the five post cards in that order. Each card shows its channel, date and text.
3. The **Open post** icon on a card is greyed out today. To work on a post, find it in **Publish**.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No channels connected** in the list | No network is connected. | Connect one. See [Connect social accounts](connect-social-accounts.md). |
| **No channel activity in this period.** | The channel posted nothing in that period. | Pick a longer period. |
| **No published posts in this period.** | Nothing was published for this channel. | Pick a longer period, or publish a post. |
| **No tags available yet** | Insights has no tag rollup yet. | Use the other filters. |
| **Visibility** is empty | The networks do not send impressions or reach yet. | No fix. Use **Content Impact** instead. |
