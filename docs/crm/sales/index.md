# CRM

**CRM** tracks the companies, deals and sales work built on your contacts. It shows what to do today and how the month is going. Use these guides to set up your pipeline, work your deals and read your numbers.

**Where to find it:** open **CRM** in the left rail.

## Guides

| Tab | Guide | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dashboard** | [Read the CRM dashboard](../track-sales-performance.md) | You want your open pipeline, wins and deals going cold. |
| **Deals** | [Create and manage deals](../manage-deals.md) | You add a deal, move it through stages and close it. |
| **Accounts** | [Add and manage accounts](../manage-accounts.md) | You track the companies you sell to. |
| Activities, Calendar, Notes | [Log activities and notes](../log-activities-and-notes.md) | You plan calls and meetings or write notes. |
| **Campaigns** | [Track campaigns and campaign templates](../track-campaigns.md) | You record a campaign and its results. |
| **Automations** | [Manage CRM automations](../manage-automations.md) | You pause, resume or create rules. |
| **Funnel** | [Read the funnel and move leads on the board](../read-the-funnel.md) | You want to see where leads drop off. |
| **Forecast** | [Read the sales forecast](../read-the-forecast.md) | You compare the pipeline with your targets. |
| Sales reports | [Run the sales reports](../run-sales-reports.md) | You need a report as an Excel or PDF file. |
| Pipelines, Products, Lead Sources, Sales Quotas | [Configure the CRM](../configure/index.md) | You set up the pieces your CRM is measured in. |

Some screens are not in the panel: **Deal Board**, **Campaign Templates**, **Activities**, **Activity Calendar**, **Notes**, **Sales reports**, **Pipelines**, **Products**, **Sales Quotas** and **Lead Sources**. Each guide says how to open them.

## Configure

The setup screens are in **Settings** → **CRM**. They are for admins. See [Configure the CRM](../configure/index.md).

## Before you start

- Your workspace has the CRM module, and your role has CRM access. Otherwise the **CRM** icon does not appear. Ask your admin.
- **Dashboard**, **Funnel**, **Forecast** and **Sales reports** need access to the CRM reports. **Automations** needs automation access.
