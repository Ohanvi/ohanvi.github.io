# See your scheduled appointments

Use the **Appointments** view to see every booking on a calendar or in a list. At the end you can check who is booked when, and move or cancel a booking without leaving the Schedule.

## Before you start

- You can see **Schedule** in the left rail. By default only the workspace's Super Admin role has it. Ask your admin if it is missing.
- Bookings exist. See [Take bookings on WhatsApp](../appointments/take-bookings-on-whatsapp.md).

## Steps

### Open the Appointments schedule

1. Open **Schedule** in the left rail, then select **Appointments**. The page title reads **Appointments schedule**.
2. The page opens in **Calendar** view for the current month. The channel chips are hidden, because the page is already limited to Appointments.
3. Click a day to see its items. Click **List** to see them by time under day headings.
4. Click an item. Its details open on the right: **When**, **Recurrence**, **Audience / with**, **Owner**, **Channel** and **Type**.

### Read what each type means

The **Type** shows what kind of item it is.

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Booking** | One appointment booked with a provider. |

The reminders that go out before a booking show under the **WhatsApp** view as **Appointment reminder**.

The **Audience / with** line shows the customer's phone number.

### Read the status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Scheduled** | The booking is **Requested**, **Confirmed** or **Rescheduled**. |
| **Sent** | The booking is **Completed** or **No-show**. |
| **Cancelled** | The booking is **Cancelled** or **Rejected**. |

### Change an item

1. Click **Reschedule** to pick a new date and time. See [Reschedule, cancel or delete a scheduled item](reschedule-cancel-delete.md).
2. Click **Edit** to open the **Appointments** page. See [Manage appointments](../appointments/manage-appointments.md).
3. Click **Cancel** to call it off. The booking is set to **Cancelled** and its reminders are removed.
4. Click **Delete** to remove it from Appointments.

### Narrow the view

1. Click **Everyone's schedule** and choose **My schedule** to see only items you own.
2. Click **Any status** and choose **Scheduled**, **Draft**, **Sent** or **Cancelled**.

### Add something new

1. Click **New schedule**, then choose **New in Appointments**. Ohanvi opens the **Appointments** page, where you create the item.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nothing scheduled here** | There are no bookings, or a filter hides them. | Choose **Everyone's schedule** and **Any status**. |
| **Could not reschedule.** | The new time is taken, outside working hours, or outside your booking rules. | Pick another time, or use **Move** on the **Appointments** page, which offers free times. |
| **Delete** cancels the booking instead of removing it | Appointments are never deleted. | No action needed. The booking stays as **Cancelled**. |
