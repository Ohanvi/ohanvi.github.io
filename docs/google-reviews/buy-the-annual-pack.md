# Buy the Annual Pack for a branch

The **Google Reviews Annual Pack** covers one branch's review page for 1 year. It costs ₹1,000 + GST per branch, paid from your credits. Takes about 2 minutes.

## What you get

| | Without the pack | With the pack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **AI review drafts** | ₹5 + GST per request that returns drafts, up to 3 drafts. | Unlimited, free. |
| **Voice input** | ₹3 + GST per voice input (one tap of **Speak instead**). | ₹2 + GST per voice input. |
| **Daily AI limit**, and up to 100 voice inputs a day per branch | Apply. | Still apply. |

- The pack covers only the branch you buy it for. Each branch needs its own pack. See [Manage branches](manage-branches.md).
- Voice input is charged whether the customer's phone or our AI turns the speech into text.
- After the year, drafts go back to ₹5 + GST and voice to ₹3 + GST.

## Before you start

- Your credits cover ₹1,000. GST was paid when you added funds, so ₹1,000 comes off your credits. See [Add funds to your account](../add-funds.md).
- The branch's review page is set up. See [Get more Google reviews](get-more-google-reviews.md).
- Your workspace is not billed through Shopify. See [Manage a plan billed through Shopify](../billing/shopify-billed-accounts.md).

## Steps

### Buy it from the branch's Settings

1. Click **More** on the left rail, then select **Google Reviews** → **Review page**.
2. In **Branch:** at the top, pick the branch.
3. Go to **Settings**.
4. Click **Buy Annual Pack**. If the branch already has an active pack, the button reads **Extend by a year**.
5. Check the branch and the price, then click **Buy**, or **Extend**.

### Buy it from the Credits page

1. Open the **Credits** page. See [Credits and billing in Ohanvi](../credits-and-billing.md).
2. Under **Add-on packs**, on the **Google Reviews Annual Pack** card, click **Buy**.
3. If you have several branches, pick one under **Which branch?**.
4. Check the branch and the price, then click **Buy**, or **Extend** if the branch already has an active pack.

The price comes off your credits and **Settings** on the branch's **Review page** shows the date the pack ends.

## Extend the pack

Buy the pack again for the same branch.

- **While it is active:** it runs a year past its current end date. No days are lost.
- **After it has ended:** a new pack starts on the day you buy it and runs for 1 year.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Could not buy the pack** with **Not enough wallet balance. Add money to buy the Google Reviews Annual Pack.** | Your credits are lower than ₹1,000. | Click **Add funds** in the message, add funds, then buy again. See [Add funds](../add-funds.md). |
| **Buy** on the pack's card on **Credits** is grey, with **Add ₹… more to your credits first** | Your credits are lower than ₹1,000. | [Add funds](../add-funds.md) and open **Credits** again. |
| **No Google review page yet** when you buy on **Credits** | The pack is bought for one branch's review page, and none is set up yet. | Set up the branch's review page first. See [Get more Google reviews](get-more-google-reviews.md). |
| **Buy Annual Pack** is missing, or **Credits** shows no **Add-on packs** | Your workspace is billed through Shopify, or your billing status is still loading. | Wait a moment and open the page again. A workspace billed through Shopify can't buy the pack. See [Manage a plan billed through Shopify](../billing/shopify-billed-accounts.md). |
| Drafts still cost ₹5 on a branch | The pack was bought for another branch, or it has ended. | Check **Branch:** at the top, then check the end date under **Settings**. Buy the pack for this branch. |
| Customers see **Drafts aren't available right now.** with a pack | **AI review drafts** is off, or the **Daily AI limit** is reached. The pack doesn't lift the limit. | Turn on **AI review drafts**, or raise the **Daily AI limit**. |
| Customers don't see **Speak instead** with a pack | **Voice input** is off, your credits can't cover ₹2, or the branch already had 100 voice inputs today. Voice still costs ₹2 + GST with the pack. | Turn on **Voice input** under **More settings**, or [add funds](../add-funds.md). After 100 voice inputs, voice comes back the next day. |
