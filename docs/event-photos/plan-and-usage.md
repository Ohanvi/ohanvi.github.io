# Read your Events plan and usage

**Plan & usage** shows what Event Photos costs you this month: your plan's rates, your storage and face recognition, the cost of each day, and the events that use the most storage. You can also compare plans and recharge from it.

## Before you start

- You can see **Events** on the left rail. If not, ask your admin to give your role access. See [Team, roles and permissions](../settings/roles-and-permissions.md).
- You know how Events usage is charged. See [Events pricing and usage](pricing-and-usage.md).

## Steps

### Open Plan & usage

1. Click **Events** on the left rail, then select **Plan & usage**.
2. The page reads **What Events cost this month on your plan: storage and face recognition.**

The buttons at the top of the page:

- **Refresh** — load the latest figures.
- **Recharge** — open the **Recharge credits** page. See [Add funds to your account](../add-funds.md).

### Read your plan

The first card shows your plan's name, marked **Your plan**, and its Events rates. On **Starter** it reads **Storage ₹4 per GB-month for a photo's first 90 days, then ₹3. Face recognition 6 paise per photo. Each upload is billed for at least 60 days.**

Under it, the card reads **Events usage is charged from your wallet, on top of your plan fee.** and shows your **Wallet balance**. The wallet is your credit balance.

While Events billing is not switched on yet, the card also shows **Preview: these charges aren't being taken yet.** The figures are real prices, but nothing comes off your credits, and face recognition never pauses.

To move to another plan, click **Change plan**. **Plans & Pricing** opens. See [Manage your plan and users](../billing/manage-plan-and-users.md).

### Read this month's usage

The section is named after the month, for example **November 2026**.

| Tile | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Active storage** | The size of your photos in their first 90 days, right now, and what they cost this month so far. |
| **Archive storage** | The size of your photos from their day 91 on, right now, and what they cost this month so far. |
| **Face recognition** | The photos whose faces were found this month, deleted photos included, and what they cost. |
| **Charged from your wallet this month** | Storage plus face recognition this month. It reads **Storage and face recognition, on top of your plan fee**, or **Preview: not taken yet** while charges show as a preview. |

Storage is measured once a day, just after midnight. Today's storage shows up tomorrow morning.

### Read storage per day

1. Find the **Storage per day** card. It shows one bar for each day measured this month, with the total, for example **5 days · ₹33.33 in all**.
2. Point at a bar, or press and hold it on a phone. You see the day, the size stored and that day's cost.
3. To see the same days as a table, click **Show table**. The table lists **DAY**, **ACTIVE**, **ARCHIVE** and **COST**, newest first.
4. To go back to the bars, click **Show chart**.

Early in the month the card reads **No storage measured yet this month. The first day shows up the morning after.**

### Find the events that use the most storage

1. Scroll to **Storage by event**. It lists your 10 biggest events, biggest first, with each one's photo count and size.
2. Click an event to open it. To lower your storage, delete the photos you don't need. See [Upload and organise photos](upload-and-organise-photos.md#delete-photos).

With no photos stored yet, it reads **Nothing stored yet**.

### Compare plans

1. Scroll to **Compare plans**. The table shows each plan's **Plan fee**, **Minimum storage period**, **Active storage, per GB-month**, **Archive storage, per GB-month** and **Face recognition, per photo**.
2. Find your plan. Its column is highlighted and marked **Current**.
3. To change plan, click **Change plan** under the table. **Plans & Pricing** opens. See [Compare plans and pricing](../billing/plans-and-pricing.md).

On a phone, swipe the table sideways to see every plan.

### Restart face recognition after your credits run out

When your credits are empty, the page opens with **Face recognition is paused: your wallet is empty.** It shows how many photos wait, for example **12 photos are waiting. Recharge to continue.**

1. Click **Recharge** in the message. The **Recharge credits** page opens.
2. Recharge. See [Add funds to your account](../add-funds.md).
3. Wait about 15 minutes. The waiting photos are face-matched on their own, and their status changes from **Waiting for credit** to the number of faces.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Plan & usage** is not in the **Events** menu | Your role can't open events. | Ask your admin to give your role access to Events. See [Team, roles and permissions](../settings/roles-and-permissions.md). |
| **Couldn't load your Events usage** | The page could not reach Ohanvi. | Click **Retry**. |
| **Couldn't refresh** | The latest figures could not load. The page still shows the last ones. | Click **Try again**. |
| Today's storage is missing | Storage is measured once a day, just after midnight. | Check again tomorrow morning. |
| **Charged from your wallet this month** shows an amount, but your credits didn't go down | Charges show as a preview. The tile reads **Preview: not taken yet**. | No fix needed. Nothing is taken during the preview. |
| **Face recognition is paused: your wallet is empty.** | Your credits can't pay for face recognition. | Click **Recharge**. Waiting photos are face-matched within about 15 minutes. |
