# Find and finish drafts

Use the **Drafts** view to find items that are saved but not ready to send. At the end you will have found each draft, opened it in the module that owns it, and either finished it or removed it.

## Before you start

- You can see **Schedule** in the left rail. By default only the workspace's Super Admin role has it. Ask your admin if it is missing.
- You saved a draft with a date and time in Email or Social. A draft with no date and time does not appear on the Schedule.

## Steps

### Open Drafts

1. Open **Schedule** in the left rail, then select **Drafts**. The page title reads **Drafts** and the page opens in **List** view.
2. Read the list. Each row shows the title, the channel and a **Draft** pill.
3. Click a row. Its details open on the right.

### Know where drafts come from

| Channel | What shows as a draft |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | A campaign that is **Draft**, **Pending review** or **Rejected** and has a scheduled time. |
| **Social** | A post saved as a draft with a scheduled time. |

WhatsApp, CRM and Appointments items are never drafts. They are **Scheduled** as soon as they exist.

### Finish a draft

1. Click the draft.
2. Click **Edit**. The message **Opening Email…** or **Opening Social…** appears.
3. Ohanvi opens the module that owns the draft. Find the draft there and finish it. See [Send an email campaign](../email/send-email-campaign.md) or [Schedule posts and manage the queue](../social/schedule-social-posts.md).
4. Come back to **Drafts**. A finished item moves to **Upcoming**.

### Remove a draft

1. Click the draft.
2. Click **Cancel** to call it off and keep the record, or **Delete** to remove it from its module. See [Reschedule, cancel or delete a scheduled item](reschedule-cancel-delete.md).

### Narrow the list

1. Click a channel chip to show **Email** or **Social** drafts only.
2. Click **Everyone's schedule** and choose **My schedule** to see only your own drafts.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nothing scheduled here** | There are no drafts, or none has a date and time. | Open the draft in its module and give it a scheduled time. |
| A **Rejected** email campaign shows as a draft | **Rejected** campaigns are grouped with drafts. | Open it with **Edit**, fix what was rejected and send it for review again. |
| A draft is not in **Upcoming** | **Upcoming** shows only **Scheduled** and **Live** items. | Finish the draft first. |
