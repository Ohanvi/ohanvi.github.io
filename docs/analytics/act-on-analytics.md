# Act on your analytics

Turn what **Analytics** shows into your next step. At the end, you have cleaned up failed contacts, reused your best campaign, tuned your templates or cart-recovery journey, and timed your next broadcast to the hour your customers order most.

## Before you start

- You have read [Marketing Analytics](read-marketing-analytics.md) or [Store Analytics](read-store-analytics.md) for a window with data.
- Your role can open **WhatsApp** → **Campaign** and **Template**.

## Steps

### Follow a Do this next suggestion

**Do this next** on **Marketing Analytics** shows up to 4 suggestions, most important first. Each appears only when the window's numbers call for it.

| Suggestion | When it appears | Button opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Clean out … contacts that failed to deliver** | Failed messages are 2% or more of sent. | **Review failures** — **Delivery Logs**. |
| **Segment your sends — …% read rate is low** | Read rate is below 55%. | **Pick a segment** — **Campaign**. |
| **Sharpen the offer — only …% of readers acted** | Clicks and replies are below 8% of opens. | **See templates** — **Template**. |
| **Repeat what worked — "…" read best** | At least 1 campaign has a read rate above 0%. | **Open campaign** — **Campaign**. |
| **Connect a store to see revenue per campaign** | No store is connected. | **Connect store** — **WhatsApp** → **Manage**. |

1. Open **Analytics** in the left rail, then select **Marketing Analytics**.
2. Under **Do this next**, read the suggestion and its reason.
3. Click the suggestion's button. The linked screen opens.
4. Make the change there, for example remove failed numbers or pick a smaller audience.

### Reuse your best campaign

1. Under **Best campaigns**, note the campaign at the top.
2. Open **WhatsApp** in the left rail, then select **Campaign**.
3. Create a new broadcast with the same template and a similar send time.

### Tune cart recovery

1. Open **Analytics** in the left rail, then select **Store Analytics**.
2. In **Abandoned carts**, check the **Recovery rate**.
3. Click **Tune the cart-recovery journey**. The **Flows** screen opens.
4. Open the cart-recovery journey and adjust its messages or timing.

### Follow up with top customers

1. In **Top customers**, click a name shown in green. The **Inbox** opens.
2. Search for the customer's number, shown under their name.
3. Send a thank-you or a re-order message.

### Time your next broadcast

1. In **When orders come in**, read the peak hour in the caption.
2. Open **WhatsApp** in the left rail, then select **Campaign**.
3. Schedule your next broadcast shortly before that hour [VERIFY: scheduling option label in Campaign].

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Do this next** does not appear | No suggestion applies to this window's numbers. | Nothing to fix. Try a longer window for more data. |
| **Connect store** opens **Manage**, not the store screen | The button links to WhatsApp settings. | Open **Connect** → **Store Connections** instead. |
| **Pick a segment** opens the campaign list | The button does not pre-select an audience. | Create a campaign and choose a smaller audience yourself. |
| **Tune the cart-recovery journey** opens an empty list | No cart-recovery journey exists yet. | Connect a store first; see [Set up data for Analytics](set-up-analytics-data.md). |
