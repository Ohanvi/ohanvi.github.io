# Add holidays and closed days

Business Holidays lists national days, festivals and your own closed days. The Scheduler paints them on the month so you do not plan a send onto a day nobody is there to answer replies. At the end, your own days appear in the Scheduler.

## Before you start

- You can see **Manage** in the **Email** panel. If not, ask your admin.
- You know the dates you want to mark.

## Steps

### Open the holiday list

1. Open **Email** in the left rail, then select **Manage**.
2. Select **Business Holidays**. The page title reads **Holidays**.
3. Read the line under the title. It counts shipped holidays and your own.

You can also open the list from the Scheduler. Click **Holidays** at the top of the Scheduler.

### Read the list

1. Look at the **Date**, **Holiday**, **Type**, **Source**, **Note** and **Status** columns.
2. In **Source**, **Shipped** means the holiday comes with the product and is shared by every account. **Yours** means you added it.
3. In **Type**, read what each kind means for a send:

    | Type | Meaning |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Festival** | A good day to send. |
    | **National** | A good day to send. |
    | **Closed** | Your company is closed. Do not send. |
    | **Optional** | Some people will be away. |

### Add your own holiday

1. Click **Add**. The add form opens.
2. In **Date**, pick the day.
3. In **Holiday**, type a name, for example `Annual stock count`.
4. In **Type**, choose **Festival — a good day to send**, **National day — a good day to send**, **Company closed — do not send** or **Optional — some will be away**.
5. Optional: in **Note**, type a reminder for your team.
6. Save the form. The holiday appears with **Source** set to **Yours**.

### Edit or delete a holiday

1. Click **Edit** on one of your own rows. Change the fields and save.
2. To remove it, click **Delete** and confirm.

!!! warning
    You cannot edit or delete a shipped holiday. Its **Date (shipped holiday — read only)** field is locked, because one account renaming it would rename it for all accounts. The message says: **This holiday ships with the product and is shared by every account, so it cannot be deleted. Add your own instead.**

!!! note
    A holiday never blocks a send. On the Scheduler, moving a campaign onto a **Closed** day only asks you to confirm.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Delete** is greyed out. | The row is a **Shipped** holiday. | Leave it, or add your own holiday for the day. |
| The fields are locked. | The row is a **Shipped** holiday. | Add your own holiday instead. |
| A holiday does not show in the Scheduler. | The page was not refreshed. | Click **Refresh** in the Scheduler. |
