# Create subscription topics

A topic is a category of email, such as **Weekly offers** or **Product news**. People can opt out of one topic without leaving your whole list. At the end, you have topics that campaigns can use and contacts can opt out of.

## Before you start

- You can see **Manage** in the **Email** panel. If not, ask your admin.
- You know which kinds of email you send, for example offers, news and receipts.

## Steps

### Open the topic list

1. Open **Email** in the left rail, then select **Manage**.
2. Select **Topics**. The page title reads **Topics**.
3. With no topics, the page says: **Subscription categories contacts can opt out of individually on your preference centre.** Otherwise it counts your topics.
4. Read the **Topic Name**, **Description**, **Created On** and **Status** columns.

### Create a topic

1. Click **Add**. The add form opens.
2. In **Topic Name**, type a short name people will recognise, for example `Weekly offers`. It is required.
3. Optional: in **Description**, say what the topic sends.
4. Save the form. The topic appears in the list.

### See where a topic is used

1. Click **View Details** on a topic row. The **Topic Details** dialog opens. Click the maximize button to make it larger, and **Restore** to shrink it.
2. Read **Record Status**, **Used In** and **Opted Out**. **Used In** lists the campaigns that use the topic. It says **Not used by any campaign yet.** if none do.
3. Read **Record Info** for who created it and when.
4. Open **History** to see earlier changes. It says **No revision history available.** if there are none.

### Use a topic

1. Tag a campaign with the topic when you create it. A contact who has not opted out of a topic is subscribed to it by default.
2. To give a list a topic by default, open the list and set **Default Topic**. See [Use Email Groups](use-email-groups.md).

### Edit or delete a topic

1. Click **Edit** on the row, change the name or description, and save.
2. To remove a topic, click **Delete** and confirm.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Used In** shows an error. | The campaigns could not be loaded. | Close the dialog and open it again. |
| People still get a topic after opting out. | The campaign was not tagged with the topic. | Tag the campaign with the topic before you send. |
| You cannot find **Topics**. | It lives inside **Manage**, not in the main panel. | Open **Manage** first. |
