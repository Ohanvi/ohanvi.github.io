# Open Settings and find a setting

Open **Settings** from your profile menu and learn how it is laid out. You will know where your account, team, billing, notification and module settings live, and how to open each one without leaving the screen.

## Before you start

- You are signed in to Ohanvi. See [Sign in to Ohanvi](../sign-in.md).

## Steps

### Open Settings

1. Click your initials in the bottom-left corner. The profile menu opens.
2. Click **Settings**. The **Settings** screen opens.

On a phone, the initials button sits in the top-right corner instead.

### Read the layout

The left side of **Settings** lists what you can configure:

- **Your name and email** at the top. This holds your team, billing, notification and developer settings.
- **Workspace** — shared tools such as **Integrations**.
- **Modules** — one entry per module you can open, such as **WhatsApp**, **CRM**, **Email Marketing** and **Appointments**.

You only see modules your workspace and your role can open. The search box at the top of the list is not active yet.

### Open a setting

1. On the left, click your name, or a module such as **CRM**. Its settings list appears on the right, grouped under headings.
2. Click a row, for example **Notifications**. That setting opens on the right.
3. To go back to the list, click the back link at the top of the setting. It shows your name, or **<Module> settings** for a module.

A module with only 1 setting, such as **Appointments**, opens that setting straight away.

### What is under your name

Your name opens these groups. You see only the rows your role allows.

| Group | Rows |
| --- | --- |
| **Team & access** | **App User**, **User Group** |
| **Billing & credits** | **Plans & Pricing**, **Billing**, **Invoice Details**, **Credits**, **Current Usage**, **Connectors**, **Refer & Earn** |
| **Preferences** | **Notifications** |
| **Developer** | **Developer APIs** |

If the list shows **Nothing to manage with your current role.**, ask your workspace owner for access.

### Other items in the profile menu

- Click your name at the top of the profile menu to open **My Account**. See [Update your profile and password](update-your-profile.md).
- **Switch role** appears when you hold more than 1 role. Pick a role to see the app as that role.
- **Current usage** opens your itemised usage for this billing cycle.
- **Sign out** signs you out.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You cannot find **Settings** in the left rail | Settings lives in the profile menu, not the rail. | Click your initials bottom-left, then click **Settings**. |
| **Nothing to manage with your current role.** | Your role has no access to these settings. | Ask your workspace owner to change your role. See [Manage team members, roles and permissions](roles-and-permissions.md). |
| A module is missing from the list | Your workspace or role does not include that module. | Ask your workspace owner to give your role access to it. |
| Billing rows are missing | Only the workspace owner (Super Admin) sees billing. Workspaces billed through Shopify see plans in Shopify instead. | Ask your workspace owner, or open billing from your Shopify admin. |
| Typing in **Search settings** does nothing | Search across settings is not available yet. | Pick the module on the left, then scan its groups. |
