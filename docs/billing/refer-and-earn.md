# Refer and earn

Invite businesses to Ohanvi and earn a share of what each one pays, every month they keep paying. Your commission first covers your own plan. Anything beyond that you can withdraw. Takes about 5 minutes to set up payouts.

## Before you start

- You are signed in as an admin.
- To withdraw, you have a bank account or UPI ID in your own name.

## Steps

### Open Refer and earn

1. Click your initials in the bottom-left corner, then click **Settings**.
2. Under **Billing & credits**, click **Refer & Earn**. The **Refer & earn** page opens.
3. Read the **How it works** panel. A business signs up with your code and pays their first invoice, then you earn a share of their plan every month they keep paying.

### Share your code

1. Find **YOUR REFERRAL CODE**.
2. Click **Copy code**. The message **Referral code copied** appears.
3. Or click **Copy invite link**. The message **Invite link copied** appears. Send it to the business.
4. The business can also type your code in **Have a referral code?** when they sign up. See [Sign up for Ohanvi](../sign-up.md).

### Read your earnings

1. Read **Total referred**, **Lifetime earned**, **This month** and **Monthly commission**.
2. Read **Your plan this month**. When your commission covers your plan, you see **Commission covering your plan**, and the rest shows as **Balance beyond your next invoice**.
3. Under **Referred businesses**, check each business's status: **Signed up, not paid**, **Active & paying** or **Stopped paying**.

### Add payout details

1. Click **Add payout details**. If you already have details, click **Update payout details** or **Change**.
2. Choose **Bank** or **UPI ID**.
3. For a bank, fill in **Account holder name**, **Bank name**, **Account number** and **IFSC**, for example `HDFC0001234`. For UPI, type your **UPI ID**, for example `name@okhdfcbank`.
4. Click **Save**. Details are encrypted and never shown in full again. Saving replaces the earlier ones.

### Withdraw earnings

1. Under **Withdrawable**, check your balance.
2. Click **Withdraw** or **Request withdrawal**. The **Withdraw earnings** window opens.
3. Type the **Amount**. It must be at least the minimum shown and no more than your balance.
4. Click **Request withdrawal**. The message **Withdrawal requested — we will review it shortly** appears.
5. Track it under **Withdrawals**. The status moves from **Requested** to **Approved** and **Paid**, or shows **Declined**. A paid row shows the UTR.
6. Money reaches your account in 3–5 working days.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No payout account yet — add one to withdraw.** | No payout details are saved. | Click **Add payout details**. |
| **Enter a valid IFSC, e.g. HDFC0001234** | The IFSC format is wrong. | Check the code and retype it. |
| **Enter a valid UPI ID, e.g. name@okhdfcbank** | The UPI ID format is wrong. | Check it and retype. |
| **Minimum withdrawal is** an amount | Your amount is too low. | Enter at least the minimum. |
| **Could not request the withdrawal.** | The request failed. | Try again. |
