# Run the sales reports

Run a report on your funnel, pipeline, wins and losses, lead sources, activity, campaign return or quota progress. At the end, you have a file you can share. A report takes about 2 minutes.

## Before you start

- You have access to the CRM reports. If you do not, ask your admin.
- **Sales reports** is not listed in the **CRM** panel. You open it from the search box.

## Steps

### Open Sales reports

1. Press **Ctrl K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), or click the search box at the top.
2. Type **Sales reports**.
3. Select **Sales reports**. The screen opens.

### Pick a report

1. Click one of the 7 reports:
    - **Sales Funnel**
    - **Pipeline Value**
    - **Win / Loss**
    - **Lead Sources**
    - **Activity Productivity**
    - **Campaign ROI**
    - **Forecast vs Quota**
2. Wait for the report to load.

### Choose the period

1. Click the date range button.
2. Pick the period you want. The report reloads.

If the report says **Nothing in this period**, widen the date range.

### Download the report

1. Click **Export Excel** for a spreadsheet.
2. Or click **Export PDF** for a document you can print or send.

### Check the commerce strip

1. At the top of **Sales reports**, find the **Commerce Leads** strip.
2. Click it to open abandoned carts. See [Recover abandoned carts with Commerce leads](recover-abandoned-carts.md).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sales reports** does not appear in the search | Your role has no access to CRM reports. | Ask your admin. |
| **Nothing in this period** | No data falls in the date range. | Widen the date range. |
| **Forecast vs Quota** shows the whole team | The report cannot filter by owner yet. | Compare with **Won by owner** on the **Dashboard**. |
