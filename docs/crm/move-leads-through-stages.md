# Move leads through your stages

Leads no longer sit on a list or a board of stages that you drag between. They follow a plan of phases and steps on **CRM** → **Leads**. This page shows where each old task went and which status each choice sets.

## Where each task went

| You used to | Now |
| --- | --- |
| Add a lead on the list | Click **Add lead** on **Leads**. See [Work with leads](leads.md). |
| Drag a card to another stage | Choose on the lead's active step. See [Decide after the first call](leads.md#decide-after-the-first-call). |
| Convert a lead into a contact and a deal | Click **Mark converted**. See [Convert a lead](leads.md#convert-a-lead). |
| Import leads from a file | Click **Import** on **Leads**. See [Import leads and deals](import-leads-and-deals.md). |
| Open **Leads** from **Contacts** | Open **CRM** → **Leads**. The old address `/contacts/leads` opens it. |

## What each choice sets

| Choice | Lead status |
| --- | --- |
| **In** | Qualified |
| **Out** | Unqualified |
| **Lost: not interested** | Lost |
| **Mark converted** | Converted |

A lead reaches **Converted** only by being converted. Your stages and fields, and the funnel that counts leads by stage, still work. See [Set up stages and fields](set-up-stages-and-fields.md) and [Read the funnel and move leads on the board](read-the-funnel.md).
