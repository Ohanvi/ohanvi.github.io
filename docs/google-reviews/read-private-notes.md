# Read private notes

Read the notes that customers who gave 1 to 3 stars sent you from your review page, and mark each one read once you have followed up. It takes about 2 minutes.

A private note is sent as well as a Google review, never instead of one. Every customer saw the same Google button.

## Before you start

- Your role can open **Private notes**. It needs its own permission. If you cannot see it, ask your admin. See [Team, roles and permissions](../settings/roles-and-permissions.md).
- **Private note for 1–3 stars** is on in the **Review page** settings. It is on by default.

## Steps

### Read the notes

1. Click **More** on the left rail, then select **Google Reviews** → **Private notes**. You can also click **Read notes** on the unread alert on **Review page**.
2. Read the line at the top: **Notes from customers who gave 1–3 stars. They saw the same Google button as everyone else.**
3. Click **Unread** to see only the notes you have not read, or **All** for every note. The pill shows the count, for example **Unread (3)**. The newest note is first.
4. Read a note. It shows the star rating, the date and time, the tags the customer tapped and their message. A note you have not read has the badge **Unread**. If the customer left a name or phone, it shows under the message; if not, the note says **No name or phone left**.
5. Click **Show more** at the bottom to load older notes.

### Mark a note read

1. Follow up with the customer if they left a name or phone.
2. On the note, click **Mark as read**. The count on the **Unread** pill goes down by 1.
3. To bring it back to the unread list, click **Mark as unread**.

!!! warning "Don't ask for a review change"
    Fix the problem the customer raised. Don't ask them to change or remove their Google review, and don't offer anything for one.

### Turn private notes on or off

1. Open **Review page** and go to **Settings**.
2. Turn **Private note for 1–3 stars** on or off.
3. Click **Save settings**.

When it is off, customers don't see **Also tell the owner privately**, and **Private notes** reads **Private notes are off. Turn on "Private note for 1–3 stars" in the Review page settings.** Notes already received stay in the list.

### Get an alert for each new note

Build a flow that starts on the event **Private note from the review page**. For example, send yourself a WhatsApp message with the note. See [Build an automation flow](../flows/build-automation-flow.md).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No private notes yet** | No 1 to 3 star customer has sent a note. | No fix needed. When one does, it shows up here. |
| **Private notes are off. Turn on "Private note for 1–3 stars" in the Review page settings.** | Private notes are turned off. | Turn on **Private note for 1–3 stars** and click **Save settings**. |
| **Private notes** is missing under **Google Reviews** | Your role has no access to private notes. | Ask your admin to give your role access. |
| A note says **No name or phone left** | Name and phone are optional for the customer. | No fix needed. The note is anonymous. |
