# Compare plans and pricing

Plans & Pricing shows every plan with what it includes. You can pick a ready plan, build your own, apply a coupon and pay. Every plan includes all modules, billed monthly, in INR. Takes about 5 minutes.

## Before you start

- You are the workspace's admin. See [Manage your plan and users](manage-plan-and-users.md).
- Your workspace is not billed through Shopify. See [Manage a plan billed through Shopify](shopify-billed-accounts.md).
- Add your GSTIN first if you want input tax credit. See [Add your GSTIN and invoice details](add-gst-invoice-details.md).

## Steps

### Open Plans and Pricing

1. Click your initials in the bottom-left corner, then click **Settings**.
2. Under **Billing & credits**, click **Plans & Pricing**.
3. Read the banner at the top for your licence status.

### Compare plans

1. Read each plan card: its name, **Billed per user**, **Highlights** and **Everything in** the plan before it, **plus** what is new.
2. Check **Add-ons** such as **Extra user seat** and **Extra automated message**.
3. For custom needs, click **Contact us** or **Let's talk** on the Enterprise card. It opens an email to the sales team.

### Build your own plan

1. Click **Build your own plan** (**BUILD YOUR OWN**).
2. Pick the modules and users you need. The summary shows the total.

### Subscribe

1. Click **Choose this plan** on a plan card.
2. On **Complete your subscription**, type your **Billing email**. Invoices and receipts are sent here.
3. Under **Have a coupon?**, type the **Coupon code** and click **Apply**. The line **Coupon** and your code **applied** appears.
4. Check the **GST** line and **Amount due**. If credits cover it, the button reads **Confirm — nothing to pay**.
5. Complete the payment. The page shows **Confirming your payment…**. If you paid but it still waits, click **I have already paid** or **Check again**.

### Switch or downgrade

1. Click **Choose a different plan**, then **Confirm switch**.
2. A downgrade asks **Downgrade plan?**. It starts at your next renewal, and you keep your current plan until then.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Enter a valid billing email first.** | The billing email is empty or wrong. | Retype it. |
| **No plans are available for this account right now.** | No plan is published for you. | Click **Contact us**. |
