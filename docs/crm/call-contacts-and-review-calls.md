# Call contacts and review your calls

Call a contact from the **All contacts** screen and record what happened. At the end, each call has an outcome and an optional follow-up date, and your team can see it in the contact's history. The whole review takes about a minute per call.

## Before you start

- You can see **Contacts** in the left rail with **All contacts** in its panel. See [Manage contacts](manage-contacts.md).
- The contact has a **Phone** or **Mobile** number.
- For **WhatsApp Call**, your WhatsApp number is connected. See [Use WhatsApp Calling](../whatsapp/whatsapp-calling.md).

## Ways to call

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Call Now** | Starts a call from the device you are using. A review window opens when you come back to Ohanvi. |
| **Call on Mobile — send this number to your phone** | Sends the number to your phone so you can dial from there. Shown on a computer only. |
| **WhatsApp Call** | Starts a WhatsApp call to the contact. |

## Steps

### Call a contact

1. Open **Contacts** in the left rail, then select **All contacts**.
2. Click the contact in the list.
3. Click **More actions** (the **…** button) in the record header.
4. Click **Call Now**. Your device starts the call.
5. Talk to the contact. When the call ends, come back to Ohanvi. The **Call Review** window opens on its own.

On a phone, the row shows the same buttons. Tap the phone button on the row.

### Send the number to your phone

1. Select the contact, then click **More actions**.
2. Click **Call on Mobile — send this number to your phone**.
3. Open the message on your phone and dial the number.

This button is hidden on a phone, because the dialler is already in your hand.

### Review a call

The **Call Review — contact name** window shows how long the call lasted.

1. Under **Call Outcome**, pick one. You can also type your own. The box reads **Select or type an outcome**. The outcome is required.
2. Choose from the built-in outcomes:
    - **Interested**
    - **Interested but Busy**
    - **Not Interested**
    - **Call Back**
    - **Call Tomorrow**
    - **Call in Two Days**
    - **No Answer**
    - **Invalid Number**
    - **Left Organization**
    - **Already Used Other Vendor**
3. Look at the hint under the box. Ohanvi guesses an outcome from the call length. A very short call hints **Invalid Number**. A short call hints **Not Interested**. A longer call hints **Interested**. You can change it.
4. To reuse a custom outcome later, click **Save "your text" as a reusable option**.
5. If you chose **Already Used Other Vendor**, type the name in **Vendor Name**. The box reads **Which vendor is the contact using?** It is required.
6. In **Remarks**, type notes about the call. The box reads **Notes about the call…**. Click the microphone (**Dictate remarks (voice to text)**) to speak instead.
7. Set a **Follow-up Date**:
    - **Call Tomorrow** and **Call in Two Days** fill the date for you. The line **Follow-up scheduled for …** confirms it.
    - **Call Back** needs you to click **Select a date** and pick one.
8. Click **Save**. The call appears under the contact's **Calls** and **Activity** tabs.

!!! note
    The **Call Outcome** is required. If it is empty, the window says **Please select or type a call outcome.** For **Call Back**, a missing date gives **Please pick a follow-up date for Call Back.**

### Fix the contact's details during the review

1. In the review window, click **Update contact details**.
2. Read the prompt. It changes with the outcome:
    - **Update the contact details they shared:** for most outcomes.
    - **Wrong number? Enter the correct one:** for **Invalid Number**.
    - **They left — add the alternate details they gave you:** for **Left Organization**.
3. Change **Mobile**, **Phone**, **Email** or **Designation**.
4. For **Invalid Number**, tick **Mark as invalid — set Do Not Call** if you never want to call this number again.
5. Click **Save**.

### Call the last contact again

1. On the **All contacts** screen, click the **Last Called** icon in the top bar.
2. Read the name, the number and when you called, for example **Called 2 hours ago**.
3. Click **Call Again** to dial the same contact, or **Close**.

If you have not called anyone from this device yet, the message **No calls placed from this device yet** appears.

### Hide contacts you have already called

1. Click the filter icon in the top bar. Its tooltip is **Hide called contacts (already completed calls)**.
2. The list now shows only contacts without a completed call.
3. Click the same icon again. Its tooltip is now **Show all contacts**.

### See your follow-ups

1. Click the bell icon in the top bar. Its tooltip is **Follow-ups Due**.
2. Pick **Overdue**, **Upcoming** or **All**. Each shows its count, for example **Overdue (3)**.
3. Read each row. It shows the follow-up, the owner and the contact it belongs to.
4. Click **Refresh** to reload, or **Close** to leave.

If nothing is due, the list says **No follow-ups in this view.**

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Call Review** does not open after a call | You did not return to the Ohanvi tab or app. | Switch back to Ohanvi. The window opens when you return. |
| **Could not save the call review. Please retry.** | The save did not reach the server. | Check your connection and click **Save** again. |
| **Could not update the contact details. Please retry.** | The details you changed did not save. | Click **Save** again. |
| **Please enter the vendor name.** | You chose **Already Used Other Vendor** without a name. | Type the vendor in **Vendor Name**. |
| **Call on Mobile** is not shown | You are on a phone. | Use **Call Now**. |
