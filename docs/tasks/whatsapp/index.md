# WhatsApp tasks

WhatsApp tasks lets your staff work on the task board from WhatsApp. They get each new task as a message with buttons, type short commands to list or finish work, and send photos as proof. Every change lands on the same task board your office uses.

**Where to find it:** open **Tasks** in the sidebar, then click **WhatsApp tasks**. The page has the tabs **Today**, **Analytics**, **Recurring** and **Settings**.

## Before you start

- Your business has a WhatsApp number connected to Ohanvi.
- The people who will use it are added as staff or teammates with a WhatsApp number. See [Add staff](../../hr/add-staff.md).
