# Manage lead sources and see which ones convert

A lead source is the channel a lead came through, such as your website, a referral or Instagram. At the end, each lead has a source and you can see which sources bring people who qualify. Adding a source takes about a minute.

## Before you start

- You are an admin. CRM settings are admin only.
- You know the channels your enquiries come through.

## Steps

### Open Lead Sources

1. Click your initials in the bottom-left corner, then click **Settings**.
2. Select **CRM**, then click **Lead Sources**.

You can also press **Ctrl K** (**⌘K** on a Mac), type **Lead Sources**, and select it.

### Read the performance view

1. Read the heading **Lead Sources**. The line under it says **Where your N leads come from — and which convert best**.
2. Read **Performance by source**. The chip **Last 30 days** shows the period. Only leads created in the last 30 days count.
3. Read each row. It shows the source name, a bar for its share of leads, and the count, for example **48 leads**.
4. Under each row, read **N qualified · N% conv**. A lead counts as qualified when its status is **QUALIFIED** or **CONVERTED**.
5. Look for sources with many leads and a low rate. They fill your list but rarely qualify.

The busiest source is first.

### Add a source

1. Click **Add source**. The **Add Lead Source** form opens.
2. In **Name**, type the source, for example `Instagram`.
3. Optional: type a **Description**.
4. Optional: tick **Default Source** to preselect it on new leads.
5. Click **Submit**. The message **Lead source created** appears.

Ohanvi starts you with **Website**, **Referral**, **Cold Call**, **Trade Show**, **Social Media**, **Email Campaign**, **Partner** and **Other**.

### Use a source on a lead

1. Open a lead, or click **Add lead**. See [Work with leads](leads.md).
2. Pick the **Lead Source**.
3. Save the lead. It now counts in that source's row.

If you have none yet, the screen says **No lead sources yet**. Click **Add source**.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **No lead sources yet** | No source exists. | Click **Add source**. |
| A source shows 0 leads | No lead with that source was created in the last 30 days. | Check that leads carry the source. |
| **Lead Sources** is missing from CRM settings | Your role is not an admin. | Ask your admin. |
