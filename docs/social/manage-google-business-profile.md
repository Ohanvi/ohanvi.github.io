# Manage your Google Business Profile

Keep your Google listing up to date and answer reviews without leaving Ohanvi. You can edit your business name, contact details, address, categories and opening hours, add booking or order links, and reply to reviews in public.

## Before you start

- Google Business is connected, with at least one location. See [Connect social accounts](connect-social-accounts.md).
- You can see **Social** in the left rail, with **Business Info** in its panel. If not, ask your admin to give your role access. See the [Settings overview](../settings/index.md).

To post to your Google listing, use the composer. See [Create and publish a social post](create-social-post.md).

## Steps

### Edit your business info

1. Open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Business Info**.
2. If you manage more than one listing, pick it in **Location**.
3. Under **Profile**, edit **Business name**, **Description**, **Phone number** and **Website**. Type the phone number with the country code, for example `+911234567890`.
4. Under **Address**, edit **Address line**, **City / locality**, **State / region** and **Postal code**.
5. Under **Categories**, edit **Primary category**. In **Additional categories**, separate each category with a comma.
6. Under **Regular hours**, set each day to **Open** or **Closed**, and type the opening and closing times.
7. Click **Save changes**. The message **Business info saved.** appears.

Google reviews some edits before they go live. Until then, the page shows **Your last edit is still pending review by Google — the values shown may not be live yet.**

Business name can be up to 300 characters. Description can be up to 750 characters.

### Add a booking or order link

This section appears only when your workspace supports it.

1. Under **Booking / order links**, click **Add link**. The **Add booking / order link** window opens.
2. In **Action type**, type the kind of link, for example `BOOK`.
3. In **URL**, paste the link.
4. Click **Add**. The message **Link added** appears.

To remove a link, click the delete icon next to it.

### Verify your listing

1. On **Business Info**, check the **Verification** line at the top.
2. If the listing is not verified, click **Start verification**.
3. In **Method**, keep `PHONE_CALL` or type another method Google offers for your listing.
4. Click **Start**. The message **Verification started — check for a PIN** appears. Google sends the PIN by the method you picked.

### Reply to Google reviews

Reviews sync from Google in the background.

1. Open **Social** in the left rail, then select **Home**.
2. Under **NEEDS ATTENTION**, find **Google reviews** and click **Reply**. The reviews page opens.
3. To narrow the list, pick a **Rating**, or switch on **Unreplied only**.
4. On a review, click **Reply**. The **Reply to …** window opens.
5. Type your answer in **Public reply**. To start from saved text, pick one in **Insert template**. To get a suggested answer, click **Suggest reply**.
6. Click **Send Reply**. The message **Reply posted to Google** appears. The reply is public on Google.

To change a reply, click **Edit** under it, then **Update Reply**.

### Track reviews with your team

1. On a review, click the flag icon (**Flag for follow-up**) to mark it.
2. Click the person icon (**Assign / note**). The **Assign / internal note** window opens.
3. Fill in **Assigned to** and **Internal note**. The note is not visible to the reviewer or on Google.

### Delete a reply

1. Under the reply, click **Delete**. The **Delete reply?** window opens.
2. Click **Delete**. The message **Reply deleted** appears.

!!! warning "This cannot be undone"
    Deleting removes your public reply from Google.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Business info editing is not available on this server yet.** | Business info editing is not turned on for your workspace. | Ask your admin. |
| **No reviews synced yet** | Ohanvi has not pulled reviews from Google yet. | Wait for the next background sync, then click **Refresh**. |
| **No reviews match these filters** | The rating or **Unreplied only** filter hides every review. | Click **Clear filters**. |
| **Reply text is required** | **Public reply** is empty. | Type a reply. |
| **No suggestion available on this server yet.** | Suggested replies are not turned on for your workspace. | Write the reply yourself. |
| Changes do not show on Google | Google is still reviewing your edit. | Wait for Google to approve it. |
