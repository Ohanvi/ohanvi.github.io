# View everything scheduled

Use the **Schedule** section to see everything your workspace has queued, across WhatsApp, CRM, Email, Social and Appointments. At the end you will have a calendar or list narrowed to the day, channel, owner and status you care about.

## Before you start

- You can see **Schedule** in the left rail. If not, ask your admin to give your role access to the schedule. By default only the workspace's Super Admin role has it. See [Settings](../settings/index.md).
- Something is already scheduled in at least one module, for example a WhatsApp broadcast or an email campaign. The Schedule section shows items; you create them in their own module.

## Steps

### Open the Schedule

1. Open **Schedule** in the left rail, then select **All schedules**. The **My Schedule** page opens in **Calendar** view for the current month.
2. Use the arrows next to the month name to move to the previous or next month. Click **Today** to jump back to the current month.
3. Each item shows as a coloured chip with its time and title. The colour tells you the channel.
4. Click a day to open a list of everything scheduled that day, for example **Today · 4 scheduled**. Click an item to open its details.

### Switch between calendar and list

1. At the top of the page, click **List**. Items are listed by time, grouped under day headings such as **Today · 26 Sep**.
2. Click an item in the list. Its details open on the right: **When**, **Recurrence**, **Audience / with**, **Owner**, **Channel** and **Type**.
3. Click **Calendar** to go back to the month view.

!!! note
    The details pane appears only when the window is wide enough, such as on a laptop or tablet. On a narrow window you see the list only.

### Open a ready-made view

Each panel item opens the same page with a filter already applied:

| Panel item | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **All schedules** | Everything, in calendar view. |
| **Today** | Items scheduled for today, in list view. |
| **Upcoming** | Items from today onward that are **Scheduled** or **Live**, in list view. |
| **Drafts** | Items still in **Draft**, in list view. |
| **WhatsApp**, **CRM**, **Email**, **Social**, **Appointments** | One channel only. The page title changes to, for example, **WhatsApp schedule**. |
| **Recurring** | Items that repeat. See [Manage recurring schedules](manage-recurring-schedules.md). |

### Filter the view

1. On **All schedules**, click a channel chip: **WhatsApp**, **CRM**, **Email**, **Social** or **Appointments**. The first click shows only that channel. Click more chips to add channels back.
2. Click **Everyone's schedule** and choose **My schedule** to see only items you own.
3. Click **Any status** and choose **Scheduled**, **Draft**, **Sent** or **Cancelled**. **Scheduled** also includes items that are **Live** or **Paused**.

### Read the status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Draft** | Saved but not yet scheduled to go out. |
| **Scheduled** | Queued for the time shown. |
| **Live** | Running now, for example a recurring series. |
| **Sent** | Already went out. |
| **Paused** | Held in its own module. |
| **Cancelled** | Called off. The record is kept. |
| **Failed** | The send did not go through. Open the item in its module to see why. |

### Schedule something new

1. Click **New schedule**. The **New schedule — pick a channel** sheet opens.
2. Choose **New in WhatsApp**, **New in CRM**, **New in Email**, **New in Social** or **New in Appointments**. Ohanvi opens that module's own screen, where you create the item.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nothing scheduled here** | No items match the channel, owner or status filters. | Click another channel chip, choose **Everyone's schedule**, or set the status back to **Any status**. |
| **Could not load your schedule.** | The page could not reach the server. | Click the retry button on the message. If it keeps failing, sign out and sign in again. |
| **Schedule** is missing from the left rail | Your role does not include schedule access. | Ask your admin to add it to your role. |
| An item you created is not shown | Items without a date and time do not appear on the Schedule. | Open the item in its module and give it a scheduled time. |
| No details pane or action buttons in **List** view | The window is too narrow for the details pane. | Widen the browser window, or use a laptop or tablet. |
