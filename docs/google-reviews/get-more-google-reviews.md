# Get more Google reviews

Set up a review page that is linked to your Google listing, then share its QR code and link. Customers pick stars, answer a few questions about your business, tap names, or speak a few words, get 3 drafts, and post on Google themselves. Setup takes about 10 minutes.

## Before you start

- You can see **Google Reviews** under **More** on the left rail. If not, ask your admin to give your role access. See [Team, roles and permissions](../settings/roles-and-permissions.md).
- Your organisation's name and address are filled in on your Ohanvi account. Ohanvi uses them to find your business on Google.
- Your business has a listing on Google Maps.
- For AI drafts, the branch has the **Google Reviews Annual Pack**, or your credits cover ₹5 a request. For voice input, your credits cover ₹3 a use, or ₹2 with the Annual Pack. See [Add funds](../add-funds.md) and [Buy the Annual Pack for a branch](buy-the-annual-pack.md).
- If you have several branches, each branch has its own review page. Pick the branch in **Branch:** at the top of the screen. See [Manage branches](manage-branches.md).

## Steps

### Confirm your business on Google

Ohanvi searches Google Maps for your organisation's name near its address, and shows the best match. You confirm it with one click. Nothing is linked until you confirm, because two businesses can share a name.

1. Click **More** on the left rail, then select **Google Reviews** → **Review page**. The first time, the page reads **Find your business on Google once. Then share the QR code: customers pick their stars, tap what stood out, and post on Google.**
2. Under **Is this your business on Google?**, wait while it shows **Looking for your business on Google…**.
3. Read the **Best match** card. It shows the business name, address, Google rating and number of reviews, with the line **Found from your organisation name and address in Ohanvi.**
4. If it is your business, click **Yes, this is my business**. The message **Listing confirmed. Your review link is ready.** appears.
5. If it is not your business, click **Not mine, search** and go to [Search for your business](#search-for-your-business).

!!! warning "Check it is your listing"
    Customers who scan your QR code are sent to this listing to post their review. A wrong match sends their reviews to another business. Compare the address before you confirm.

Ohanvi also reads your business type from Google and uses it to pick your **Industry**, which sets the questions customers answer. See [Choose the questions customers answer](#choose-the-questions-customers-answer).

After you confirm, the **Your Google listing** card shows the business with the badge **Confirmed** and a line such as **Confirmed by Asha on 23 Oct 2026. Customers' reviews go to this listing.** Listing details are marked **Data from Google Maps**.

### Search for your business

Use this when the match is wrong, or when Ohanvi found no match.

1. In **Search Google**, type your business name and area, as it shows on Google Maps. Use 2 to 120 characters.
2. Click **Search**. Up to 5 results appear, each with its name and address. Results near your address come up first.
3. Click **Pick** on your business. Ohanvi reads the listing from Google, with its rating and review count, and links it.

If nothing fits, the screen says **No businesses found. Try the name as it shows on Google, with your area.**

To link a different business later, click **Change** on the **Your Google listing** card. The card reads **Change your Google listing**. Search and pick again, or click **Cancel** to keep the current one.

### Paste a review link (last resort)

Use this only if you cannot find your business by search.

1. Open [Google Business Profile](https://business.google.com) and sign in with the account that manages your listing.
2. Click **Ask for reviews** (on some screens it is **Get more reviews** or **Share review form**).
3. Copy the link Google shows. It starts with `https://g.page/` or `https://search.google.com/`.
4. In Ohanvi, on **Review page**, click **Can't find it? Paste your Google review link instead**.
5. Paste the link and click **Save link**. The message **Review link saved** appears.

The listing card then reads **Customers go to the review link you pasted, not to the listing. Remove it to use the listing.** Click **Remove pasted link** to go back to the confirmed listing.

You do not need to connect Google Business Profile to collect reviews. To reply to reviews from Ohanvi you do, and **Auto-reply** shows under **Google Reviews** only once Google approves Business Profile access for Ohanvi. See [Set up auto-reply](set-up-auto-reply.md).

### Choose the questions customers answer

Customers answer 3 or 4 short questions about your kind of business by tapping one answer each, for example **Excellent**, **Good**, **Average** or **Poor**. They don't have to type. The drafts use only the answers they tap.

1. On **Review page**, find **Questions customers answer**. The line under it reads **Customers tap one answer per question, and can add a few words of their own. The AI drafts use only what they pick.**
2. Check **Industry**. Ohanvi picks it from your Google listing when you confirm your business, and shows the type Google gave it, such as **Google lists you as "Software company".** If it is wrong, pick your industry from the list. If none fits, pick **Other business**.
3. Read the questions. Each card shows its number, the question and its answers.
4. To change a question, edit its text right on the card. To change an answer, tap it (**Tap to edit**), change the text, then click **Update answer**. To add an answer, type it and click **Add answer**. To drop an answer, click the **x** on it (**Remove answer**). To drop a question, click the trash icon (**Remove question**).
5. To add your own question, click **Add question**, type the question and add its answers.
6. Click **Save questions**.

Each industry, such as **IT / Software**, **Travel agency**, **Salon / Spa**, **Clinic / Doctor**, **Restaurant / Cafe** or **Retail shop**, comes with its own 3 or 4 default questions. Until you edit them, your page shows your industry's defaults. If you change **Industry** later, the defaults change with it. Questions you edited stay until you reset them.

| Item | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| Questions | 1 to 5. |
| Question text | 3 to 120 characters. |
| Answers per question | 2 to 5. |
| Each answer | 1 to 30 characters. |

Questions and answers show exactly as you write them, in every page language. Write them in the language most of your customers read.

#### Suggest questions with AI

1. Under **Questions customers answer**, click **Suggest with AI**.
2. Read the 3 or 4 questions it suggests for your industry, each with 4 answers.
3. Edit or remove any you don't want.
4. Click **Save questions**. The suggestions are not used until you save them.

Suggestions use the AI model set in **Google Reviews** → **Settings**. See [Choose the AI model for Google Reviews](settings.md).

#### Go back to the industry questions

Click **Reset to industry defaults**. Your own questions are replaced with your industry's default questions.

#### The "Other" box

Inside **Want to add more?**, under the questions, customers also see a short box for anything the questions missed. It reads **Anything else? e.g. the service you used**. They can type up to 120 characters, such as the service they came for. The box is optional, and you don't set it up.

!!! tip
    Ask about what customers can judge in a tap: speed, care, cleanliness, value. Keep every answer honest, with a poor option as well as a good one. Leave out offers and prices.

!!! note "Tags are replaced by questions"
    The page no longer shows tags such as **Great service**. Questions give the drafts more to work with, in the customer's own choice of answer.

### Add names customers can pick

Customers can also tap who helped them and what they came for. Each group you fill shows on the page as its own question, in a card under your questions, so customers always see the names you added.

| Group | Customers see | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Staff** | **Who helped you?** | `Dr. Mehta` |
| **Services** | **What did you come for?** | `Root canal` |
| **Products** | **What did you buy?** | `Chocolate cake` |

1. On **Review page**, find **Names customers can pick**.
2. Under **Staff**, **Services** or **Products**, type a name and click **Add**. Use up to 12 names in each group, each 2 to 40 characters.
3. Click **Save names**. The message **Names saved** appears.

Leave a group empty to hide its question. To drop unsaved changes, click **Undo changes**.

Names, questions and answers show on your public page, so a swear word or slur in any of them stops the save with **Please remove the word "…" and try again.** See [Keep the page polite](#keep-the-page-polite).

!!! note "Names appear only when the customer picks them"
    A draft mentions a name only when the customer picked it. A staff name, service or product the customer didn't tap never appears in a draft. Don't add names to push keywords into reviews.

!!! note "Staff names stay out of drafts by default"
    Google doesn't allow a business to ask for specific content in a review, and that includes staff names. So by default, staff names customers tap go to your [private notes](read-private-notes.md) and [stats](read-google-review-stats.md#see-what-customers-told-you) only, not into the Google draft. Services and products the customer picks still go into drafts. To let drafts mention staff the customer picked, turn on **Staff names in drafts**. See [Set up the page](#set-up-the-page).

### Set up the page

1. On **Review page**, go to **Settings**.
2. Make sure **Page is live** is on. Turn it off to stop the QR code and link from opening.
3. Turn **AI review drafts** on or off. When it is on, customers get 3 drafts written only from their stars, answers and words.
4. Set the **Business name** customers see. Leave it empty to use your Google listing name.
5. Pick the **Page language**. The customer page and its drafts use it. Customers can switch to another language on the page.
6. Click **More settings** to open the rest. It holds **Voice input**, **Private note for 1–3 stars**, **Staff names in drafts**, **Daily AI limit**, **Accent colour**, **Logo**, **Website widget** and **Generate new QR code**.
7. Turn **Voice input** on or off. When it is on, customers can tap **Speak instead** and say their review. The recording is never stored.
8. Turn **Private note for 1–3 stars** on or off. It is on by default. See [Read private notes](read-private-notes.md).
9. Turn **Staff names in drafts** on or off. It is off by default. When it is off, staff names the customer taps go to your private notes and stats, not into the drafts.
10. Set the **Daily AI limit**: the most draft requests a day. After that, customers write their own words. You can change it only while **AI review drafts** is on.
11. Optional: pick an **Accent colour**. It is used for the page's buttons, chips and header. Pick **Default** to use the page's own colour.
12. Optional: to change the **Logo**, click **Change logo in Flyer**. The logo you upload in the flyer shows on the review page and the flyer. See [Design a review flyer](design-a-review-flyer.md).
13. Click **Save settings**. The message **Settings saved** appears.

| Setting | What it allows |
| --- | --- |
| Staff names in drafts | Off by default. When off, staff names customers tap reach only your private notes and stats. Google doesn't allow asking for specific content, such as staff names, in reviews. When on, a draft may mention a staff member the customer picked. |
| Voice input | On by default. Each voice input (one tap of **Speak instead**) costs ₹3 + GST, paid from your credits, or ₹2 + GST with the **Google Reviews Annual Pack**. It is charged whether the customer's phone or our AI turns the speech into text. Each branch takes up to 100 voice inputs a day, with or without the pack. |
| Business name | Up to 120 characters. |
| Page language | 11 languages: English, हिन्दी (Hindi), Hinglish (Hindi in English letters), मराठी (Marathi), ગુજરાતી (Gujarati), বাংলা (Bengali), தமிழ் (Tamil), తెలుగు (Telugu), ಕನ್ನಡ (Kannada), മലയാളം (Malayalam) and ਪੰਜਾਬੀ (Punjabi). |
| Daily AI limit | 1 to 1000 draft requests a day. The default is 200. One tap of **Write my review** is 1 request: ₹5 + GST paid from your credits, or free with the **Google Reviews Annual Pack**. The limit applies with the pack too. |
| Accent colour | One of the colours shown, or **Default**. |
| Logo | Set in the flyer, with **Change logo in Flyer**. Until you add one, **Settings** shows your business initials in its place. |

!!! tip "Unlimited drafts for a busy branch"
    The **Google Reviews Annual Pack** gives one branch unlimited AI drafts and voice input at ₹2 + GST for 1 year, for ₹1,000 + GST from your credits. Buy it with **Buy Annual Pack** under **Settings**. See [Buy the Annual Pack for a branch](buy-the-annual-pack.md).

### Show reviews on your website

The website widget shows your Google rating and recent Google reviews on your own website, with a **Write a review** button that opens your review page.

1. On **Review page**, under **Settings**, click **More settings**, then turn on **Website widget**.
2. Click **Save settings**. Until you save, the screen reads **Save settings to get the code for your website.**
3. Read the line **Paste this code into your website where the reviews should show.** The code box appears below it.
4. Click **Copy code**. The message **Code copied** appears.
5. Paste the code into your website's page, where the reviews should show.
6. Click **Preview widget** to see it as visitors will.

The widget shows:

- Your business name as it shows on Google Maps, your Google rating, and the number of Google reviews.
- Up to 5 Google reviews, each with the reviewer's name, stars, how long ago it was posted, and up to 300 characters of the text.
- A **Write a review** button that opens your review page.
- **Data from Google Maps** and a **See all reviews on Google Maps** link.

The widget is in English, whatever the page language. Ohanvi refreshes its reviews from Google at most once a day, so a new review can take a day to show.

To take the widget off your site, turn **Website widget** off and click **Save settings**. It can take up to 5 minutes to disappear from your site, because browsers keep a copy for that long. Then the widget reads **These reviews are not available.** Remove the code from your site too.

### Share the QR code or link

1. On **Review page**, find the **Share** card. The badge reads **Live** when the page is on, or **Off** when it is not. With several branches, the card names the branch, such as **For Andheri West**. Each branch has its own QR code and link.
2. Click **Copy link**. The message **Link copied** appears. Share the link on WhatsApp, SMS, email or your bill.
3. Click **Download QR** to save the QR code as an image. The message **QR code downloaded** appears, or **QR code saved to** followed by the file's location.
4. For a printed flyer, click **Design flyer**. See [Design a review flyer](design-a-review-flyer.md).

Put it on the counter or the bill, or send it on WhatsApp after the visit.

!!! tip
    Ask every customer, at the same moment in their visit. Click **Preview page** in the top bar to open your page the way customers see it.

### Check what customers see

Open your page link on your phone and try it. The labels below are the English page. Pages in other languages show the same steps in that language.

The page uses your **Accent colour** for its buttons, chips and header, or indigo when none is set. It turns dark when the customer's phone is in dark mode. A small **Powered by Ohanvi** line sits at the bottom.

1. Optional: pick another language in the 🌐 menu at the top. The page reloads in that language, and the drafts are written in it.
2. Read the top of the page: your logo or initials, your business name, your area, and the line **Tap a few answers and we'll write a review you can post on Google.**
3. Under **How was your experience?**, tap the stars. A word under them names the rating: **Very poor**, **Poor**, **Okay**, **Good** or **Excellent!**
4. Answer the numbered question cards, **01**, **02** and so on. Tap one answer on each card. Tap it again to clear it. If you added names, tap them under **Who helped you?**, **What did you come for?** or **What did you buy?**, in the card below the questions.
5. Optional: tap **Want to add more?** (**Optional**). The card opens with:
    - **Other (optional)**, a short box that reads **Anything else? e.g. the service you used**, up to 120 characters.
    - **In your own words (optional)**, up to 300 characters, with **Speak instead** next to it. See [Voice input](#voice-input).
6. Tap **Write my review** at the bottom of the screen. On a phone it stays in reach while you scroll. It reads **Writing…** while the drafts are made.
7. Under **Pick one to post**, read the cards **Short**, **Detailed** and **Warm**. The line above them says **Each draft uses only what you told us. Edit anything before you post.** Below the drafts, the page says **These are suggestions. Please edit them to reflect your genuine experience.**
8. Tap **Edit** to change a draft, or **Write my own instead** to write from scratch.
9. Tap **Copy & post on Google** on a draft. The review is copied, and a sheet opens from the bottom.
10. Read the sheet. It shows **✓ Review copied** and **Google opens in 3s**, counting down, with 3 steps:
    1. **Tap the stars on Google**
    2. **Long-press the text box**
    3. **Tap Paste, then Post**
11. Wait 3 seconds, or tap **Open Google now**. Google's review form opens.
12. On Google, tap your stars, long-press the text box, tap **Paste**, then tap **Post**.

Back on the page, the sheet has **Copy again** if the text didn't paste. Tap **Close** to go back to the drafts.

| Draft | How it reads |
| --- | --- |
| **Short** | 1 to 2 sentences. |
| **Detailed** | 3 to 4 sentences. |
| **Warm** | Friendly and personal, 2 to 3 sentences. |

If the phone doesn't let the page copy, the sheet shows **Couldn't copy it. Select the text below and copy it, then open Google.** with the review in a box, and Google doesn't open on its own. The customer copies the text, then taps **Open Google now**.

If the customer taps **Write it myself**, or **Write my own instead**, the page shows **Post it on Google** with their text in **Your review**. It lists the steps **Choose your stars on Google · Paste · Post** and the button **Open Google review form**, with the line **You post it yourself; nothing is posted for you.** **Open Google review form** copies their text and shows the same sheet.

A draft never states a star count. The customer chooses their stars on Google.

!!! note "When 'Want to add more?' is open from the start"
    With no questions on the page, **Other** and own words show open, without the **Want to add more?** button. The card also opens on its own when the customer taps **Write my review** with nothing picked, or when their words need a change.

### How drafts differ by stars

The stars the customer picks on your page decide how the drafts are written. Every customer sees the same **Copy & post on Google** button, whatever they pick.

| Stars | How the drafts read | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 or 5 | 2 to 4 sentences. Each mentions your business name, the service or product the customer picked or typed, and your city or area once, in a natural way. It uses the customer's answers. The 3 drafts are worded differently. | "Got our WhatsApp API setup done by Ohanvi IT Solutions in Gurugram. Onboarding was smooth and the support team replied within an hour." |
| 1, 2 or 3 | 1 or 2 plain sentences, only from the customer's answers and words. No location, no service keywords and nothing that promotes the business. | "Setup took longer than expected and support replies were slow. Hope it improves." |

For every rating:

- The customer edits the draft as they like, and posts it on Google themselves. Ohanvi never posts a review.
- A draft uses only what the customer tapped, typed or said. It never adds details, keyword lists, hashtags or phrases such as "best salon in town".
- The line **These are suggestions. Please edit them to reflect your genuine experience.** shows under the drafts, in the page's language.

!!! warning "Why drafts don't stuff keywords"
    Google removes reviews that look alike or repeat the same phrases, and can put a warning on your profile. Drafts mention your name, service and area once, the way a customer would, and each draft is worded differently. Don't edit your own page or flyer to push keywords into reviews.

When drafts are not available, the page offers only **Write it myself**. That happens when **AI review drafts** is off, the **Daily AI limit** is reached, or the branch has no **Google Reviews Annual Pack** and your credits can't cover ₹5.

### Voice input

When **Voice input** is on, customers see **Speak instead** next to **In your own words (optional)**. Their words are added to the text box, where they can change them.

- On phones that type speech themselves, the button reads **Listening… tap to stop** while the customer speaks.
- On other phones, the page records up to 1 minute. It shows **Recording** with a timer and a **Stop** button, then **Turning your voice into text…**.
- Either way, each voice input (one tap of **Speak instead**, until it stops listening or recording) costs ₹3 + GST, paid from your credits, or ₹2 + GST on a branch with the **Google Reviews Annual Pack**. A pause while speaking is not a new voice input.
- Each branch takes up to 100 voice inputs a day, with or without the pack.

The recording is never stored. It is turned into text and dropped.

The customer may see:

| Message | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Allow the microphone to speak, or type instead.** | The phone blocked the microphone for the page. |
| **Couldn't catch that. Try again, or type instead.** | Nothing clear was heard, or the recording failed. |
| **Voice input isn't available right now. Please type instead.** | The branch has had 100 voice inputs today, or voice input is off. |
| **Too many tries. Please wait a few minutes, or type instead.** | Many recordings came from one network in a few minutes. |

**Speak instead** is hidden when **Voice input** is off, when the phone can neither type speech nor record, when your credits can't cover a voice input, or when the branch has had 100 voice inputs today.

### What a 1 to 3 star customer also sees

When the customer picks 1, 2 or 3 stars, the drafts read **Thanks for being honest** with the line **Your review helps others, and helps the business improve.** in place of **Pick one to post**. The drafts are short and plain, and **Copy & post on Google** works the same as for 5 stars.

When **Private note for 1–3 stars** is on, a card **Also tell the owner privately** also shows below the Google buttons.

1. The customer reads the line **Only the owner sees this. It doesn't replace your Google review.**
2. They type in **Message to the owner**, 3 to 1000 characters.
3. Optional: they fill **Your name (optional)** and **Phone (optional)**. Under the phone, the page explains: **Share your name and number only if you want {your business} to contact you about this, including on WhatsApp. Used only for this.**
4. If they want you to contact them, they tick **Yes, {your business} may contact me about this**.
5. They tap **Send to the owner**. The page shows **Sent to the owner. You can still post on Google.**

The page uses your business name in place of **{your business}**. Name and phone are saved only when the box is ticked. Without the tick, the note reaches you without them. The note also carries the answers and names the customer tapped, and the page language, so you see the whole visit. Name and phone are deleted 90 days after the note. See [Read private notes](read-private-notes.md).

If drafts aren't available (**AI review drafts** is off, the **Daily AI limit** is reached or the AI didn't answer), the same card shows under **Write it myself** instead, so a 1 to 3 star customer can still tell you privately.

The Google button stays on the screen the whole time. The note goes to **Private notes**, and **Review page** shows an alert such as **2 unread private notes** with **Read notes**. See [Read private notes](read-private-notes.md).

### Keep the page polite

Swear words and slurs are blocked on the review page, on both sides. Honest criticism is not: "the service was slow" or "the food was cold" goes through, and the drafts keep it, in polite words. Google doesn't allow a business to hold back negative reviews, and Ohanvi never does.

What customers see:

- Words with a swear word or slur in **In your own words** or **Other** are not sent. The **Want to add more?** card opens and shows **Please keep it polite. You can still share honest feedback.** The customer changes the word and taps **Write my review** again.
- A private note with a swear word or slur is not sent, and shows the same line.
- Words spoken with **Speak instead** come back with any swear word taken out.
- Drafts never contain a swear word or slur, even when the customer used one. A draft that has one is dropped. If no clean draft is left, the customer sees **Write it myself** and writes their own words.

What you see:

- A swear word or slur in your questions, answers, names, flyer headline, tagline or icon labels stops the save with **Please remove the word "…" and try again.**, naming the word.
- A Google reply, typed by you or drafted for auto-reply, with a swear word or slur is not posted. See [Set up auto-reply](set-up-auto-reply.md).

Ohanvi catches common tricks too, such as `f.u.c.k`, `sh1t` or long repeated letters. It checks whole words only, so words such as "class" or "assistant" are fine. The word list covers English, Hindi and Hinglish.

### Make a new QR code

Make a new code if your link was shared somewhere you don't want it.

1. On **Review page**, under **Settings**, click **More settings**, then click **Generate new QR code**. The **Generate a new QR code?** window opens.
2. Click **Generate new code**. The message **New QR code ready. Download it again before printing.** appears.
3. Download and print the new QR code or flyer.

!!! warning "Old QR codes stop working"
    The old link and every printed QR code and flyer stop working at once. Update any link you shared. Paste the new link into any WhatsApp review request flow that has the link typed in, such as the store **Review request** automation.

### Ask for reviews on WhatsApp

- **After an appointment.** The starter **Ask for a Google review after an appointment** sends your review link 2 hours after an appointment is marked completed. It always uses your current link. See [Ask for a Google review after an appointment](ask-for-a-review-after-an-appointment.md).
- **After a store delivery.** The **Review request** store automation asks for a review 3 days after delivery. If your review page is live when the store automations are first set up, Ohanvi fills its review link with your page link. If the automation was set up earlier, or you made a new QR code since, click **Copy link** and paste your page link into it yourself. See [Automate store messages](../flows/store-journeys.md).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The **Best match** is another business | Another listing has a similar name near you. | Click **Not mine, search** and search with your area added. |
| **No match found** | Google found nothing for your organisation's name and address. | Search for your business by name. If it still does not show, paste your review link. |
| **We couldn't look you up** | Your organisation has no name or address in Ohanvi yet. | Search for your business by name. |
| **Finding your business on Google isn't available** | Google search is not available on your account. | Paste your Google review link instead. |
| **Google didn't respond** or **Google search isn't responding. Try again.** | Google did not answer. | Click **Try again** in a minute. |
| **Couldn't confirm this business with Google. Try again.** | Google did not answer when Ohanvi read the listing. | Try again in a minute. If it keeps failing, paste your review link. |
| **Type 2 to 120 characters to search.** | The search text is too short or too long. | Type the business name and area. |
| **Use your Google review link (it starts with https://g.page/ or https://search.google.com/)** | The pasted link is not a Google link, or starts with `http://`. | Copy the link again from **Ask for reviews** in Google Business Profile. |
| **Check the questions** or **Couldn't save the questions** appears | There are more than 5 questions, a question is shorter than 3 or longer than 120 characters, or a question has fewer than 2 or more than 5 answers, or an answer longer than 30 characters. | Remove or shorten questions or answers, then click **Save questions**. |
| The questions don't fit your business | **Industry** is wrong, or Google lists your business under another type. | Pick your industry under **Industry**. If you edited the questions, click **Reset to industry defaults** to load the new industry's questions. |
| **Suggest with AI** gives no questions | The AI model didn't answer. | Try again in a minute, or check the model in **Google Reviews** → **Settings**. |
| **Use up to 12 names.**, **Each name must be 2 to 40 characters.** or **You already have this name.** | A group has too many names, a name is too short or too long, or it is a repeat. | Remove or shorten names, then click **Save names**. |
| **Choose one of the listed languages.** | The page language isn't one of the 11 languages. | Pick a language from **Page language**. |
| Customers don't see **Speak instead** | **Voice input** is off, their phone can neither type speech nor record, your credits can't cover a voice input (₹3, or ₹2 with the Annual Pack), or the branch has had 100 voice inputs today. | Turn on **Voice input** under **More settings**, or [add funds](../add-funds.md). After 100 voice inputs, voice comes back the next day. Customers can still type. |
| Customers see **Voice input isn't available right now. Please type instead.** | The branch has had 100 voice inputs today. | No fix needed. Customers can type, and voice input comes back the next day. |
| The website widget reads **These reviews are not available.** | **Website widget** or **Page is live** is off, or the page has no review link. | Turn on **Website widget** and **Page is live**, click **Save settings**, and finish setup. |
| The website widget reads **No Google reviews to show yet.** | Google returned no reviews for your listing. | No fix needed. Reviews show once Google has them, within a day. |
| The website widget reads **Please try again in a few minutes.** | The widget was loaded many times from one network in a few minutes. | Wait 5 minutes and reload the page. |
| **No review link yet** | No listing is confirmed and no link is pasted. The page stays closed. | Click **Find my business**, or paste your review link. |
| Customers see **This review link is not active** | **Page is live** is off, a new QR code was made, or no review link is set. | Turn on **Page is live**, share the current link, or finish setup. |
| Customers see **Drafts aren't available right now. Tap Write it myself; a few words are enough.** | **AI review drafts** is off, the **Daily AI limit** is reached, or the branch has no **Google Reviews Annual Pack** and your credits can't cover ₹5. | Turn on **AI review drafts**, raise the **Daily AI limit**, [add funds](../add-funds.md), or [buy the Annual Pack for the branch](buy-the-annual-pack.md). Customers can still post their own words. |
| Drafts don't mention the staff member the customer tapped | **Staff names in drafts** is off. It is off by default, because Google doesn't allow asking for specific content in reviews. | No fix needed. The name is in your private notes and stats. To let drafts mention it, turn on **Staff names in drafts** and click **Save settings**. |
| Customers see **Too many tries. Please wait a few minutes, or write it yourself.** | Many draft requests came from one network in a few minutes. | Wait 5 minutes. Customers on their own mobile data are not affected. |
| A printed QR code stopped working | A new QR code was made. | Print the current QR code or flyer. |
| **Please remove the word "…" and try again.** | A question, an answer or a name has a swear word or slur. They show on your public page. | Remove or replace the word shown, then save again. Honest words such as "slow" or "poor" are fine. |
| Customers see **Please keep it polite. You can still share honest feedback.** | Their own words, **Other** box or private note has a swear word or slur. | No fix needed. The customer changes the word and tries again. Criticism without swear words goes through. |
