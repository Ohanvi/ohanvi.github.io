# Use Social Home

Social Home is the first screen in **Social**. It shows what needs action today, the posts going out, your results and your recent posts. You can also start a post and reply to comments from here. It takes about 5 minutes to learn.

## Before you start

- You can see **Social** in the left rail, with **Home** in its panel. If not, ask your admin to give your role Social access. See the [Settings overview](../settings/index.md).
- At least one network is connected. See [Connect social accounts](connect-social-accounts.md).

## Steps

### Open Social Home

1. Open **Social** in the left rail.
2. Select **Home**.

If no network is connected, Home shows **Connect your first social account**. Click **Connect Platforms** to start. See [Connect social accounts](connect-social-accounts.md).

### Show one network only

1. Click the platform button at the top. It reads **All platforms** until you pick one.
2. Click a network that has a tick to filter Home to it.
3. To see everything again, click **All platforms**.
4. A network that is not connected reads **… — connect**. Click it to open the connect screen.
5. Click **Manage connections** to open **Connect** → **Social Connections**.
6. Click **Refresh** to load the latest numbers.

### Start a post from Home

1. Click the box that says **What's on your mind?**. The **Create Post** window opens.
2. Or click one of the small icons under the box. They are for a photo, a video, an emoji, a tag, a place and a note. Each one opens the same **Create Post** window.
3. Finish the post. See [Create and publish a social post](create-social-post.md).

### Act on what needs attention

1. Read **NEEDS ATTENTION**. Each row shows a count, a label and an action. A coloured stripe on the left shows how urgent it is.
2. Click a row to act on it:
    - **Comments · need replies** — click **Reply →**. The **Comments** window opens.
    - **Google reviews · needs response** — click **Reply →**. The reviews page opens. See [Reply to Google reviews](reply-to-google-reviews.md).
    - **Failed posts · needs retry** — click **Retry →**. **Publish** opens. See [Schedule posts and manage the queue](schedule-social-posts.md).
    - **Platform connections · needs attention** — click **Fix →**. **Connect** → **Social Connections** opens.
3. A row appears only when there is something to do.

### Reply to a comment

1. In the **Comments** window, find the comment.
2. Click **Reply** beside it. A box opens that says **Write a reply…**.
3. Type your answer.
4. Click the send icon. A spinner shows while it sends.
5. To close the box without sending, click **Cancel**.
6. Click **Close** when you are done.

If there is nothing to answer, the window says **No comments waiting on a reply.**

### Check today's posts

1. Read **TODAY**. It lists posts going out today.
2. If nothing is planned, it says **Nothing scheduled**.
3. Click **Calendar** to open the full calendar in **Publish**.

### Read your results

1. Under **Performance**, pick **This Week**, **Last 30 Days** or **Last 90 Days**.
2. Read **Reach**, **Engagement**, **Followers** and **Posts**.
3. For charts and a post-by-post table, open **Insights**. See [Read social insights](view-social-insights.md).

### Review recent activity and posts

1. Scroll to **RECENT ACTIVITY**. Click **View more** to see more rows, or **View less** to fold them.
2. If there is nothing yet, it says **No activity yet**.
3. Under **RECENT POSTS**, click **View all** to open **Publish**.
4. Click a post to see it. Click **Open in Publish** to manage it, or **Close** to go back.
5. If you have no posts, it says **No posts yet**.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home shows **Connect your first social account** | No network is connected. | Click **Connect Platforms** and connect one. |
| **NEEDS ATTENTION** is empty | Nothing needs action. | No action needed. |
| The numbers look old | The page was not refreshed. | Click **Refresh**. |
| A network shows **— connect** in the platform list | That network is not connected. | Click it, then finish connecting. |
