Sample guide

Set up a WhatsApp broadcast campaign

Create an approved template, select contacts, schedule the broadcast, and review delivery results.

Beginner10 minutesWhatsApp API

Before you start

  • Your WhatsApp Business account is connected to Ohanvi.
  • Your message template is approved by Meta.
  • Your contact list has valid opt-in status.

Steps

  1. Open WhatsApp Marketing from the Ohanvi sidebar.
  2. Select Broadcasts, then choose Create broadcast.
  3. Pick an approved template and map dynamic fields such as name, offer, or appointment date.
  4. Select a saved audience or upload a CSV contact list.
  5. Review the preview, schedule time, and publish the broadcast.
Keep one screenshot after step 3 showing the template preview and another after step 5 showing the schedule confirmation.

Troubleshooting

  • Template unavailable: Confirm that Meta approved it for the connected business number.
  • Contacts skipped: Check opt-in status and phone number format.
  • Low delivery: Review failed reasons in the delivery report.
