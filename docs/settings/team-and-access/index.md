# Team and access

Team and access holds your own profile, the people in your workspace and what each of them can do. Use it to add or remove teammates and to set their roles.

## Where to find it

Click your initials in the bottom-left corner, then click **Settings**. Under your name, open **Team & access**.

## Guides

| Guide | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| [Update your profile and password](../update-your-profile.md) | You want to change your name, email, phone or password. |
| [Manage team members, roles and permissions](../roles-and-permissions.md) | You want to add, edit or remove teammates, or pick their role. |
| [Invite teammates with a link](../invite-teammates.md) | You want teammates to sign up on their own. |
| [Deactivate your account](../deactivate-your-account.md) | You are the owner and want to pause the whole account. |

## Before you start

- You are signed in. See [Sign in to Ohanvi](../../sign-in.md).
- Team and invite settings are for the **Super Admin**.
